Business Turnover Index (BTI)

June 2026. Provisional data. The annual rate of the Business Turnover stands at 6.0% in the seasonal and calendar-adjusted series and 8.9% in the original. The monthly variation of the BTI stood at 0.2%, after seasonal and calendar adjustment.

17 August 2026

Services Sector Activity Indicators (SSAI)

June 2026. Provisional data. The annual variation of the Market Services Sector turnover index was 7.2.0% in the calendar and seasonally adjusted series. In May, the monthly rate was 1.2% if seasonal and calendar effects are removed.

14 August 2026

Industrial Turnover Indices (ITI)

June 2026. Provisional data. The annual rate variation of the Total Industrial Turnover Index stands at 5.3% in the seasonal and calendar adjusted and 9.2% in the original. The monthly variation stands at -0.5%, after seasonal and calendar adjustment.

14 August 2026

Consumer Price Index (CPI)

July 2026. The annual variation rate of the July CPI stood at 3.6%, four tenths of a percentage point higher than that recorded in June. The annual rate of core inflation increased by one tenth to 3.0%. The monthly variation of the general rate was 0.3%.

Harmonised Index of Consumer Prices (HICP)
