About

Luca is a professor of computer science at Bocconi University. He works on theoretical computer science and he lives in Milan. Earlier, he lived for almost twenty years in beautiful and troubled San Francisco. When he is out of town he can often be found in Taipei.

In theory is about the food he likes to eat, the movies he likes to watch, and about math and computer science theory.
