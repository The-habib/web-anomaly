Guides by Cancer Type

At MSK, we care for and treat people with all types of cancer and related diseases. We have information about how we diagnose and treat each cancer type. You can also find doctors, surgeons, and other experts who treat each cancer.

Learn about cancer care and treatment

This page has a list of cancer guides for adults. Our pediatric cancer guides are on MSK Kids. Each guide gives you simple, clear information about a specific kind of cancer. You’ll learn what the cancer is, how it’s found, and the treatments that may help. The guides also explain side effects, ways to get support, and what to know about clinical trials and cancer screening.

You can also read about our expert doctors and find links to helpful news and resources.

These guides are here to help you feel informed and ready to make decisions about your care with your doctors. Just pick a guide from the list to get started.
