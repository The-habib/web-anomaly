This website uses cookies

Some of these cookies are necessary and are used to help make our site work. With your consent, we will also use cookies to improve your experience, analyse site usage and assist in our marketing efforts. By clicking 'Accept all', you consent to our use of cookies.

You can learn more about these cookies and manage your preferences at any time on our cookies page .

Necessary

These cookies enable essential website functions and ensure the site operates properly. They do not store personally identifiable information. While you can disable them in your browser settings, some features may not function correctly.

Analytics

These cookies help us understand how visitors interact with our website by collecting anonymous usage data. We use this information to improve website performance and enhance your experience.

Personalisation

These cookies allow us to customise the content you see based on your activity, such as the courses you view, applications you make, or your location. This helps us provide a more relevant and tailored experience when using our site.

Marketing

These cookies deliver personalised ads based on your browsing activity and help us measure the effectiveness of our advertising campaigns.

Giving

The generosity of our alumni and philanthropic benefactors has played a critical role in the University of Surrey’s history, and has helped us support students. We are training and educating young people who will contribute to society and be the employees and leaders of the future. We are also addressing the most urgent issues faced by the world through our research.

We are making a difference but with your support, we can do so much more.

Current campaigns

The Forever Surrey Fund

The Forever Surrey Fund supports students through scholarships, societies, and emergency grants—widening access and enriching the university experience.

Future Doctors Fund

The Future Doctors Fund provides crucial support across the University, helping to widen participation to study medicine and greatly support the student journey to becoming a doctor.

Scholarships

By supporting scholarships, you can invest in a talented student who may be the first in their family to go to university.

Tonmeister Scholarship Fund

By supporting the Tonmeister Scholarship Fund, you’re investing in the next generation of leaders in sound engineering.

Telephone Campaign

Our annual fundraising campaign in support of The Forever Surrey Fund, which provides crucial help across the University along with our team of friendly student callers.
