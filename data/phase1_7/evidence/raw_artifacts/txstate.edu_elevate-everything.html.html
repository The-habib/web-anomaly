Rising to This Moment

If you haven’t looked closely at Texas State lately, now is the time. Growing enrollment means more Texans have access to a high-quality education. Increased funding fuels research. Student and faculty achievements build momentum. Bobcat athletics is raising visibility. And that visibility is opening doors. The Quad, the river, the people—the heart of Texas State remains the same. Now, TXST enters the new Pac-12 ready to elevate everything.

To mark TXST’s entry into the new Pac-12, we published a full-page letter to the people of Texas in major media markets across the state. Read the full statement from President Kelly Damphousse on how this moment reflects Texas State’s growth, ambition, and impact.

More Investment. More Impact for Texas.

TXST is investing $300 million over the next five years across five areas where the state and nation need solutions: water and the environment, semiconductors, dementia and aging, artificial intelligence, and the digital humanities. TXST will show how public investment in transformation can translate into concrete outcomes.

Building the Future on Every Field

The 2025–2035 Campus Master Plan lays out a roadmap to facilitate TXST’s ambitions as a top-flight research institution and projected growth to 50,000 students. The plan calls for 30 new buildings plus Athletics upgrades to round out a decade of building that spans every corner of campus. The next chapter of TXST is being built right now.

Learn More

Aerial rendering of the TXST San Marcos Campus, showing a wide range of new and improved buildings and spaces.

The proposed Bobcat Landing will create a new outdoor event space on Chautauqua Hill, replacing the 1950s-era president’s residence.

Included are a Baseball and Softball Clubhouse, UFCU Stadium Bridge, the South End Zone Complex, and Indoor Practice Facility.

Along with renovations at Spring Lake and Sewell Park, the Master Plan calls for infrastructural improvement at the intersection of Aquarena Springs and Sessom Drive.
