                              Is Clearing for me

                              Clearing is an opportunity to find and apply for courses that still have places available. 

                              You can use Clearing if: 

                              • your results weren't what you expected
                              • you didn’t receive any offers 
                              • you received offers you don't want to accept 
                              • you’ve changed your mind about what or where you want to study 
                              • you’re applying after 30 June 2026

                              Clearing FAQs

                              UK students  

                              • 2 July 2026: Clearing opens
                              • 6 July 2026: International Baccalaureate (IB) results day
                              • 13 August 2026: A Level, BTEC and T Level results day
                              • 2 September 2026: Deadline for meeting your offer conditions (UCAS deadline)
                              • 14 September 2026: Enrolment  

                              International students  

                              • 2 July 2026: Clearing opens 
                              • 6 July 2026: International Baccalaureate (IB) results day
                              • 13 August 2026: A Level, BTEC and T Level results day
                              • 14 August 2026: Clearing closes to international students 
                              • 21 August 2026: Deadline for meeting your offer conditions and paying your deposit
                              • 31 August 2026: Deadline for students outside the UK to request their Confirmation of Acceptance for Studies (CAS)
                              • 11 September 2026: Deadline for students already in the UK to request their CAS
                              • 14 September 2026: Enrolment  

                              Yes. You’ll need all your results before applying through Clearing, including any GCSE results required for your course.

                              Make sure you have your transcripts and certificates ready, as you’ll need to provide copies when you apply.  

                              Yes. If you already have your results, you can apply through Clearing.

                              Learn more on our Clearing for students with alternative qualifications page. 

                              If you don’t meet the standard entry requirements listed on the course page, we still encourage you to apply.

                              Yes. If you’re an international student and already have your results, you can apply through Clearing.

                              The deadline to submit your application is 14 August 2026.

                              Learn more on our Clearing advice for international students page. 

                              Yes. A foundation course can be a great option if you didn't get the results you expected or don’t feel ready to start a full degree yet.

                              Learn more on our Foundation courses in Clearing page.   

                              You can find tailored guidance on our Clearing advice for parents and supporters page.

                              If you’d like to transfer to Year 2 or Year 3 of a similar course, you may be able to do this through Clearing. This is known as advanced entry and is subject to entry requirements and course availability.

                              If you’d like to transfer to a different subject, you’ll usually need to apply for Year 1 of a new course through Clearing.

                              For more help, email [email protected]. 

                              You'll need to request an official release if one of the following applies:

                              • you’re holding a confirmed place with us and want to change to another university 
                              • you’re no longer planning to attend university this year

                              To do this, sign in to the UCAS Hub and select the “Decline my place” button.

                              When you’ve requested your release, UCAS will send you a confirmation email.

                              Offer holders

                              When you receive your Clearing offer, you'll need to do the following before the deadline in your offer email:

                              • log in to the UCAS Hub
                              • click “Add Clearing choice” and enter your course details to accept your offer
                              • we'll then verify your results and confirm your place via email

                              If you'd like to change your course, defer your place or enquire about enrolment, see our Offer holders page.
