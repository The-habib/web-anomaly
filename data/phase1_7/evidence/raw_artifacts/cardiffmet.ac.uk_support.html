Student Support at Cardiff Met

01 - 02

Capital of Support

At Cardiff Met, everything we do is focused on making sure you have the best chance to fulfil your academic and professional potential.

Our dedicated teams are on hand to provide support throughout your studies, regardless of any challenges you may face.

01 - 04

Support Services

With an integrated network of academic, wellbeing and administrative support, you can access comprehensive care that is tailored to your needs.

You only need to tell your story once – when you’re ready, and our team will be here to partner with you and bring in the right supports.

 

Money Advice and Welfare

University may be the first time you manage your own finances. We provide budgeting support and specialist advice to help you develop your financial literacy.

Mental Health and Wellbeing

Our specialist staff are always here to listen, whether it be a chat with one of our Wellbeing Advisors, confidential 1:1 counselling, group wellbeing sessions or access to a specialist course.

Disability Support

We provide comprehensive support to disabled students, promoting inclusivity and ensuring equal opportunities for all. Our advisors will work with you to develop a support package to help you thrive.

Learning Support

Excelling at University

Student Services works in partnership with your academic team so you can maximise your academic potential.

With resources, workshops and 1:1 support, you’ll be well supported to develop your skills and succeed in your academic journey.

Support & Community at Cardiff Met

"One of the things that has made my experience at Cardiff Met so positive is the level of support available from lecturers, tutors, and the wider university community. The staff genuinely care about students, and it shows in the way they teach, communicate, and guide you throughout the course."

- McKenzie McDonald, BSc Biomedical Science (Health, Exercise & Nutrition)

From Passion to Profession

"The lecturers and tutors aren’t just knowledgeable - they genuinely cared about us as individuals. Whether it was academic advice, emotional support during stressful times, or simply being there to listen, their encouragement made a huge difference in my journey."

- Georgia Hawkins, BSc Sport & Physical Education

Careers Service

From day one to graduation and beyond, Career Services will help you shape your future. Submit the Career Plans questionnaire to receive personalized resources and advice to help you reach your goals.
