Current and Upcoming Events

  • Aberdeen Artworlds: The Linklater Collection

    02 February - 06 December 2026

    The current exhibition from University Collections is Aberdeen Artworlds, marking 50 years of the Linklater Collection of works by some of the most important Scottish artists of the 20th century.

  • Echoes of the Nakba: Palestinian memory and diaspora in Aberdeen

    17 March - 27 September 2026

    A new bilingual Arabic-English display with a case in the Ground Floor Foyer of the Sir Duncan Rice Library explores historic and current links between Aberdeen and Palestine.

  • Breathing Bodies

    01 August - 22 November 2026

    Discover the amazing science inside us all in Breathing Bodies. Use medical images to explore how our bodies look and work with three exhibits located across the city.

  • 77th International Sachsensymposion

    29 August - 02 September 2026

    International Archaeology Conference

  • Advanced School on Chest MRI

    15 - 17 September 2026

    The Chest MRI School will explore key challenges and emerging directions in lung imaging, from fundamental physiology to cutting-edge developments in MRI across field strengths.
