                              It all starts here

                              Our campuses in central London and Harrow are easy to reach, and you'll benefit from the vibrant opportunities that the city offers. Our practical courses are designed to meet the needs of employers and global job markets, taught using industry-standard facilities, so you feel prepared for your future career.

                              You’ll also benefit from Zone29, our new digital and physical destination for career success. Whether you're looking to find a graduate role, dive into the world of start-ups, launch your own business or you're still unsure what you want to do, we have something for everyone. 

                              At Westminster, you can be yourself and feel part of a supportive and diverse community, where everyone is welcome, and anything is possible. Join us this September.

                              Degree apprenticeships

                              A degree apprenticeship is an alternative route to achieving a university degree while gaining valuable work experience and earning while you learn. 

                              They're ideal if you're a school leaver or if you’re already working and seeking higher-level training with the support of your employer.

                              We offer a range of degree apprenticeships at undergraduate level.
