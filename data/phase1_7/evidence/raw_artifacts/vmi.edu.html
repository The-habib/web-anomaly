  • ROTC & Military Life
        • ROTC & Military Life

        • The unique military aspect of a VMI cadetship prepares the next generation of leaders to serve with discipline, honor, and integrity.

        • ROTC at VMI

        • VMI is among the very few colleges that offer ROTC options in Army, Navy, Marine Corps, Air Force, and Space Force.

        • Naval ROTC

        • The Naval ROTC Unit provides two programs in which cadets may enroll: US Navy or US Marine Corps.

        • Other Ways to Serve

        • Cadets can prepare for other roles in government, intelligence, cyber defense, and public safety, both at the state and federal level.

        • Army ROTC

        • VMI boasts the largest ROTC program in the country and an award-winning Ranger Challenge Team.

        • Air Force ROTC

        • The Air Force ROTC program prepares cadets for service in the U.S. Air Force or U.S. Space Force air, space, and cyberspace domains.

        • Corps Military Experience

        • From the very first day of post, the Corps of Cadets is guided in a military lifestyle and code of conduct.

        • Research

        • Cadets, faculty, staff, and community members can find a host of resources and opportunities to delve into research topics here on post.

        • Preston Library

        • Explore collections including over 300,000 volumes of materials, over 5,000 non-print items, and more than 300 scientific, literary, and general interest periodicals.

        • Museums and Archives

        • Delve into the story of VMI and Virginia through three unique museums and a fine art collection.

        • Institutional Review Board

        • Guidance and oversight for research conducted by cadets, faculty, and visiting researchers in regard to human subjects.

        • VMI Archives

        • A wealth of primary source collections of interest to both scholars and the general public.
