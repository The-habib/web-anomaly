TECHNOLOGY & CONVENIENCE

Bring your world closer.

RAV4 redefines every drive with standard advanced technology designed to empower. Customize the 12.3-in. digital gauge cluster, enjoy more peace of mind with Drive Recorder Dash Cam, and experience Toyota’s most advanced safety suite yet with Toyota Safety Sense™ 4.0 (TSS 4.0).

  1. Technology
  2. Convenience

12.9-In. Toyota Audio Multimedia

Tap into the latest. RAV4’s new available 12.9-in. multimedia touchscreen puts entertainment, directions and controls front and center with crisp visuals and seamless voice commands.

12.3-In. Digital Gauge Cluster

Designed to feel like a natural extension of your multimedia touchscreen experience, the standard customizable 12.3-in. digital gauge cluster keeps your most important info right in sight.

Traffic Jam Assist

This available feature helps you navigate the ebb and flow of stop-and-go traffic on controlled-access highways by providing control of the vehicle at speeds under 25 mph. Requires an active Drive Connect trial or subscription. 5G network dependent.

Advanced Park

RAV4’s available Advanced Park makes fitting into a tight spot possible with the press of a button. Take your hands off the wheel and watch this advanced system work its magic.

1500-Watt AC Power Outlet

Power up the good stuff, from coffee makers to air pumps. RAV4’s available rear cargo 1500W outlet helps you bring more fun to your latest adventure.

Five USB-C Charging Ports

Stay charged and connected no matter where you sit with up to five USB-C ports throughout the cabin—including a high-power front media port for accelerated charging and media access.

Dual Qi Wireless Chargers

Keep the energy flowing. Available dual Qi-compatible wireless chargers help keep you powered up, no matter where you’re headed.

Hands-Free Power Liftgate

With a kick sensor under the rear bumper, RAV4’s available hands-free power liftgate opens with a simple foot motion, making loading and unloading easy and convenient.
