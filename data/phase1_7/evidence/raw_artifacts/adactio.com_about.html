About

My name is Jeremy. I’m from Ireland originally but now I live in Brighton, England working with Clearleft, a design transformation consultancy I co-founded back in 2005.

I’ve been writing on this website for over twenty years. I really like having a place on the web to call my own.

I also really enjoy speaking about the web, and I’ve even written some books:

I play bouzouki in the rock band Salter Cane and I play traditional Irish music on the mandolin. I combined my love for the web and my love for trad by making The Session, a community website dedicated to Irish traditional music that’s been going for over two decades.

If you like, you can find out more about me.
