President and Leadership

Jeffrey D. Armstrong

University President

Jeffrey D. Armstrong serves as the ninth president of Cal Poly, overseeing the university’s San Luis Obispo campus as well as the Cal Poly Maritime Academy in Solano County. Appointed in December 2010, he is the longest‑serving president in the California State University system.
Armstrong maintains an unwavering commitment to student success and to strengthening Cal Poly’s renowned Learn by Doing educational model. 

Under his leadership, Cal Poly has consistently earned recognition as the No. 1 university in the West from U.S. News & World Report. He has expanded financial aid to broaden access for students from all economic backgrounds and significantly increased philanthropic support — resulting in new teaching facilities, laboratories, athletic spaces and expanded housing options for students.

Armstrong attended Murray State University in Kentucky, where he earned a bachelor's degree in agriculture in 1981. He earned both a master's (1984) and doctorate (1986) in physiology and endocrinology from North Carolina State University.

Albert A. Liddicoat

Provost and Executive Vice President for Academic Affairs

Dr. Albert A. Liddicoat serves as Provost and Executive Vice President for Academic Affairs. He previously served as Interim Vice Chancellor for Human Resources for the CSU system, providing strategic leadership in HR operations. At Cal Poly, he has held several leadership positions, including vice president for University Personnel, vice provost for Academic Affairs, interim dean of the Orfalea College of Business, and director of the Computer Engineering Program. A faculty member since 2002, he is the Forbes Professor of Computer and Electrical Engineering. He earned his Ph.D. and master’s degrees in electrical and industrial engineering from Stanford University and holds a bachelor’s in electronic engineering from Cal Poly.

Visit the Office of the Provost

Allison Baird-James

Senior Vice President, Administration and Finance, Chief Financial Officer (CFO)

Allison Baird-James oversees a broad portfolio that includes budget and fiscal management, facilities management, internal audit, housing operations, information technology, procurement, risk management, public safety and oversight of auxiliary operations. Baird-James has spent 14 years at UCLA, most recently serving for two years as interim vice chancellor and chief financial officer. Additionally, she served for 12 years as associate vice chancellor for business and finance solutions at UCLA, overseeing budget and fiscal management, insurance and risk management, procurement, tax, and business operations & systems. Prior to her work at UCLA, Baird-James spent 13 years at Stanford University, in a variety of university finance roles. Baird-James is a CSU alum, having earned a degree in business administration from Chico State. She is also a graduate of the executive program in management at UCLA’s Anderson School of Management, and she completed the Leadership at Stanford Program.

Corey Cook

Vice President and Chief Executive Officer, Cal Poly Solano Campus

As Cal Poly Solano Campus' chief administrative officer, Corey Cook provides strategic leadership across all operations, including planning, development, enrollment, compliance and risk management. Cook holds bachelor’s degrees in Political Science and Peace and Conflict Studies from UC Berkeley, and master’s and doctoral degrees in Political Science and Public Policy from the University of Wisconsin–Madison. Before joining Cal Poly, he served as Executive Vice President and Provost at Saint Mary’s College of California, Founding Dean of the School of Public Service at Boise State University and Executive Director of the Leo T. McCarthy Center at the University of San Francisco. A Professor of Political Science, Cook’s scholarship centers on electoral and institutional reform, California politics, public opinion and political representation. He has published in leading journals and teaches courses on environmental policy, urban politics, American government and data analysis.

Visit Cal Poly Maritime Academy

Lauren Daly

Interim Vice President of University Personnel and CHRO

Lauren Daley came to Cal Poly in March 2024 and currently serves as interim vice president for University Personnel. In a short time, she has made a significant impact — leading a talented team across Employee and Labor Relations, Talent Acquisition, Learning and Development, Compensation and Classification, HR Systems, Payroll and Benefits. Lauren maintains a strong focus on strategic alignment, operational efficiency and employee engagement. A proud Cal Poly alumna with a Bachelor of Science degree in business administration, human resources management, Lauren brings both experience and a deep connection to our campus community.

Jessica Darin

Vice President, Strategic Initiatives and Advocacy 

Jessica Darin leads the proposed integration of Cal Poly and Cal Maritime, acting as a central point of oversight for several such projects at various stages of development. She also leads strategic planning, advocacy (federal, state, and community relations), and economic development efforts to align with the university’s mission and goals. Most recently, Jessica served as the Associate Vice Chancellor for Strategic Initiatives at the CSU Chancellor's Office on a two-year interim assignment. Before that, she was Chief of Staff to Cal Poly President Jeffrey Armstrong for six years and spent three years as Deputy Chief of Staff for CSU Chancellor Timothy P. White, focusing on policy initiatives and strategic communication. Earlier, she worked for seven years at the Chancellor’s Office at UC Riverside. Jessica earned her bachelor's degree in English from UC Riverside and a master's degree in management from the University of Redlands.

Jennifer Haft

Chief of Staff

Jennifer Haft oversees the daily operations of the President’s Office, which includes the office of Student Ombuds Services. In this role, she supports President Armstrong as a strategic advisor and works collaboratively with the senior leadership of the university to advance priorities and initiatives. She provides leadership for the president’s administrative staff as well as the units reporting to the president, including: University Events, Economic Development, and the Native American Graves Protection and Repatriation Act (NAGPRA) coordinator. She also serves as President Armstrongʼs point of contact with Cal Polyʼs Academic Senate, Associated Students, Inc., and other internal and external stakeholders. Jen was previously Assistant Vice President for Operations, Administration & Finance, and she served as Chief of Staff and Director of Presidential Operations at Texas A&M University-San Antonio (A&M-SA). Jen holds a bachelor’s degree in Organizational Communication and a master’s degree in Business Administration, both from Ohio University.

Terrance Harris

Vice President, Strategic Enrollment Management and Student Affairs

Terrance Harris serves as the Vice President for Strategic Enrollment Management and Student Affairs. He has spent the majority of his professional career in the enrollment space, holding roles in recruitment, admissions advising and operations, data management, communications, and enrollment development. Beyond his professional work, Harris has coached high school basketball for 20 years and mentored hundreds of students during this pivotal stage in their lives. His spouse and three young daughters are the inspiration for all that he does. Harris earned his bachelor’s degree in Psychology and a master’s degree in Educational Leadership from Cal Poly.

Carter Henderson

Director of Athletics

Carter Henderson began as Director of Athletics in October 2025. He previously served as the Deputy Athletics Director for External Relations at Stanford University for nearly five years. He brings to the role a deep experience in intercollegiate athletics from around the country. Henderson also spent a decade at the University of Washington, where he advanced through leadership roles in external relations, ultimately serving as senior associate director of athletics for external relations. He began his athletics career at Jacksonville University, where he held director-level roles in marketing, ticketing and creative services, building a foundation in external operations and brand management.

Meggan Levitt

Vice President, Information Technology and Chief Information Officer

Meggan Levitt is the Vice President for Information Technology and Chief Information Officer at Cal Poly, where she leads the strategic use of technology to foster innovation and collaboration across the university. Previously, she was the Associate Chief Information Officer and Assistant Vice Provost at the University of California, Davis, with over 27 years of IT experience. Chris Murphy

Vice President, University Communications and Marketing

Chris Murphy leads the communications and public relations arm of Cal Poly and serves on the President's Leadership Council, Cal Poly Local Economic Development Committee, Cal Poly Inclusive Excellence Council, as well as chairing the Cal Poly Strategic Preparedness Committee. Robin Webb

University Counsel

Robin Webb joined the Office of General Counsel in 2017 and previously served in a similar role at CSU San Bernardino. She also serves as resource attorney for first amendment, FMLA and residency. Before joining the CSU, Webb was a partner in the Irvine law firm of Grant, Genovese & Baratta where she was a trial attorney and insurance coverage counsel in many different types of civil litigation matters. Webb also served as a settlement officer for the Los Angeles County Superior Court and as a mediator in various jurisdictions. Webb earned a juris doctorate and a bachelor’s degree from USC.
