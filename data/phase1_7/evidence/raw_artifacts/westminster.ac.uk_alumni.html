                              Alumni and supporters

                              Our alumni community

                              As a University of Westminster graduate, you are part of a global community of more than 200,000 alumni in over 180 countries around the world.

                              We're here to help you stay in touch with the University and the worldwide network that has shared the Westminster experience.

                              As a former student, you can enjoy free access to campuses and libraries, a discount on postgraduate study and tailored career support – all part of your alumni benefits.  

                              Westminster Alumni Awards

                              Westminster Alumni Awards recognise and celebrate the outstanding achievements of graduates from the University of Westminster, as well as from our predecessors, including the Polytechnic of Central London and Regent Street Polytechnic.

                              Read moreGo to the Westminster Alumni Awards page

                              Zone29: our new home of careers and enterprise

                              Zone29 is where former students like you can realise your career goals. with opportunities to:

                              • access job listings, expert advice and dedicated programmes 
                              • attend inspiring careers events and workshops
                              • share your expertise with emerging talent
                              • represent the Westminster community in your country

                              Read moreGo to 29 Marylebone Road page
