Welcome to GBS Poland

Our Group Business Services centre in Kraków is central to providing access to talent and transformative capabilities across the whole of Rolls-Royce.

A gateway to a global impact

Group Business Services (GBS) Poland is a critical part of the Rolls-Royce ecosystem.


As a centre of excellence for key business functions including People, Procurement and Finance Services among others, GBS plays an essential role in keeping our global operations running smoothly and efficiently. Based in Kraków, we connect with teams across the world, giving our people opportunities to grow, build networks and develop the soft skills that come from working in a truly global environment.

A mission rooted in impact

Our mission is simple: to deliver world-class business services that create value for Rolls-Royce, and career growth for our people.

We focus on operational efficiency, process simplification and service excellence (EFFICIENT, EFFECTIVE, EXPERIENCE) to ensure that we support the broader Rolls-Royce strategy while creating new opportunities for talent across Poland.

A vision for tomorrow

We are building a scalable, flexible and digitally enabled service model that allows our business and our people to thrive.

That means fostering a culture of continuous improvement, developing future-focused skillsets, and ensuring GBS Poland remains a strategic hub for transformation across Rolls-Royce.

A strategic location, by design

Located in Kraków, our site is home to a range of talented professionals and serves as a launchpad for business services across the world.

With its strong talent pool, innovation mindset and supportive infrastructure, Kraków is the ideal location to attract and retain top-tier professionals.

Purpose that powers progress


At Rolls-Royce we are a force for progress; powering, protecting, and connecting people everywhere. At GBS Poland, that purpose is made real every day by the people who manage the systems, processes and services behind the scenes. From enabling major procurement programmes to driving digital finance transformation, our work supports critical missions across civil aerospace, defence and beyond.

Careers with GBS Poland

Working at GBS means more than doing a job. It means being part of a high-performing team with a shared commitment to continuous learning and internal mobility. Our teams work across HR, Finance, Procurement and Digital Operations, with many people moving between roles and departments to broaden their skills and explore their potential. Structured development, strong mentorship and formal recognition give everyone a platform to grow.

Why join us?

  • Be part of a globally recognised brand with deep roots in engineering excellence.
  • Access a strong internal culture, with open leadership and supportive colleagues.
  • Take part in meaningful work that supports global innovation and customer delivery.
  • Enjoy work-life balance, professional development and the satisfaction of being recognised for your contributions.
