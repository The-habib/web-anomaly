Degree Programs

Whether you are a college graduate with a passion for the law, a working professional looking to transform your career, an international legal professional hoping to gain practical knowledge about the U.S. legal system, or an entrepreneur in need of legal insights to enhance your business decisions, Fordham Law’s graduate degree programs provide what you need to achieve your career aspirations.

  • J.D. ADMISSIONS Offered in day and evening programs, the J.D. degree directs your path to a career as an impactful lawyer.
  • LL.M. ADMISSIONS With flexible online and in-person options, choose from nine in-demand specializations for international and U.S.-based legal professionals.
  • S.J.D. ADMISSIONS Embark on a research-intensive program with thought leaders and leave an indelible mark in legal scholarship.
  • M.S.L. ADMISSIONS Created for non-lawyers, this one-year master’s degree program—offered in-person and online—provides a legal perspective to advance your career.
