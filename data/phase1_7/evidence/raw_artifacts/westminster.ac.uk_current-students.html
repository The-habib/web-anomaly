Support for all those affected by global conflict

At Westminster, we appreciate that conflicts in different parts of the world can significantly impact our diverse community in a range of ways.

We are concerned about the safety and wellbeing of all, including those directly affected through personal and family links. We have a wide array of support in place for those who are impacted.
 

Read moreGo to Support for all those affected by global conflict page

Help with enquiries

We’re here to answer any questions you have – and to provide guidance on a range of services to support your wellbeing and help you succeed.

If we can’t resolve your enquiry immediately, we'll direct you to the team or colleague who can. We can also help you to book appointments across different student support services.

Read moreGo to Support and Services page
