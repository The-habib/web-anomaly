International students

We're a home away from home

Join our students from more than 150 countries around the world

International Hotline: +44 (0)23 9284 5113

 

 

Each year, people from 150 countries journey across the globe to join us. So when you choose to live and study here, chances are you'll make the same life-changing trip as countless other students from wherever you call home.

Here, you'll find all the information you need to make the big leap to studying with us in Portsmouth – from the courses you can take and how much they'll cost and plenty more besides.
