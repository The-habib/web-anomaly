Why Fordham School of Professional and Continuing Studies?

Our wide-ranging professional and continuing studies programs won’t just shape your career – they’ll offer a transformative experience.

Fordham University’s School of Professional and Continuing Studies (PCS) is dedicated to meeting the academic interests and practical needs of working adults. By delivering a prestigious Fordham University education at a competitive, affordable price, we bring college graduation and professional dreams to life.

We offer Bachelor’s degrees, master’s degrees, professional certificates, and a pre-med program, designed for the working professional. 

  • Evening, weekend, and online classes are available at three campus locations, the Bronx, Lincoln Center, and Westchester.
  • Ranked as a top Military-Friendly Yellow Ribbon University by Military Times. We offer unlimited Yellow Ribbon seats to veterans.
  • Classes have 10 to 20 students, on average, so you’ll know your teachers and classmates and receive the support you need.
  • Flexible transfer credit opportunities for our undergraduate programs: We accept up to 75 credits and offer multiple ways to receive credits including life experience.

Our Programs

We'll waive your application fee when you attend an information session.

Enhance your credentials or reinvent yourself completely with Fordham School of Professional and Continuing Studies:

  • UNDERGRADUATE DEGREES Start or finish your degree with our flexible evening, weekend, and online options for adult learners. Transfer students welcome!
  • M.S. Applied Health Informatics Learn how to harness big data to improve patient outcomes in this exciting program offered in New York or online.
  • CONTINUING EDUCATION We offer professional certificates in human resources (SHRM), project management, and more, as well as our College at 60.
  • REAL ESTATE INSTITUTE AND HOSPITALITY Explore our MS in Hospitality, MSRE, MSCM, MSRED and our bachelor’s in real estate, as well as a full suite of non-credit professional certificates.
  • POST BACC PRE-MED / PRE- HEALTH Take the science courses you need and prepare to apply to medical school. Special tracks for pre-veterinary, dental, PT, and other health professions.
  • HAVE QUESTIONS? We’ve got answers! Email us at [email protected] or call 718-817-2600, or complete an inquiry form.
