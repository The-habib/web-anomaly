Compare Business SaaS Products with Slashdot

SaaS, software as a service, is one of the most user-friendly and cost-effective methods to use business software and apps. The service is hosted on the cloud, which is easily accessible through the Internet. Instead of paying a hefty fee to purchase individual software, SaaS allows individuals and companies to use multiple types of business apps by subscribing to a monthly fee.

The service provider hosts the software on a remote server. Anyone with an Internet connection can access the software using a web browser. If required, organizations can also integrate the web-based application into the existing setup using the API offered by the service provider. Using the SaaS model, you and your employees can work efficiently because the cloud service provider is responsible for the upkeep of the software that includes updates and bug fixes.

Features of Business SaaS

  1. Can Be Scaled: The success of SaaS is mainly attributed to its scalability features. Instead of waiting for upgrades and expensive installations, business owners can meet the demands of customers on the fly. The cloud-based software can easily handle a growing customer base and intelligently distribute resources to offer the same level of service quality. The use of load balancing and virtual servers allows business owners to adjust to changing demands and scenarios.

  2. Better Security: SaaS is often more secure than on-premises software. The security is enhanced by the use of powerful firewalls, intrusion prevention systems, and private access controls. SaaS providers invest heavily in security; therefore, you can rely on them to update, secure, and resolve security loops.

  3. Cut Down on Costs: Implementing SaaS can save companies thousands of dollars in software costs. You will save upfront costs on purchasing and installing the system. Once the system is in place, there is no need to hire someone who can maintain and upgrade the software. Instead of dealing with unexpected costs, SaaS offers a per-user pricing structure or a monthly subscription cost.

  4. Saves Time and Effort: Besides controlling expenses, SaaS solutions are extremely productive because they are designed to save time by allocating resources efficiently. For instance, business owners can enhance their productivity by purchasing a specified number of user licenses. As per the job requirement, the number of users can be adjusted accordingly.

  5. Ease of Use: Just like any other paid online subscription, SaaS is easy to use. You just need to create an account and purchase a plan. Most service providers offer a comprehensive online tutorial to decrease the learning curve. In fact, you also get expert customer assistance to deal with common issues. The 24/7 support staff can make things easier when you're depending on the system to perform at its peak during weekends and major holidays.

Using on-demand services, business owners can access hundreds of software within minutes. They can easily upgrade their license to add more users and use extra features at a moment's notice.

Easy to Use

The high adoption rate of SaaS is attributed to the web interface and cloud-based solutions. Your employees and contractors will surely appreciate a flexible solution easily accessible on tablets, mobiles, and PC.

Extensible with Integrations

Most SaaS service providers offer seamless integration with popular applications. You can also use our database to filter software that can consolidate your existing setup. If needed, use the API to create a customized solution to meet individual requirements.

Try Before You Buy

Most SaaS providers offer free trials to their customers. Sometimes, you only need to pay a fraction of the total cost to try a paid plan without making any sort of a commitment. You can also expect a full money-back guarantee up to a certain period. Based on the payment models, users may pay to select specific features. In fact, business managers should use the free version to get a feel of the platform before upgrading the software.

Downsides of Business SaaS

You Need an Internet Connection

The provision of SaaS mainly depends on the Internet. It means that any downtime will negatively affect the performance and quality of service. Such interruptions can prove costly when large numbers of team members simultaneously work on a critical project. To avoid mishaps, it is critical to choose a reputable ISP that has a history of consistent service. Another solution is to ensure that you always backup your data.

Budget Constraints

Most reliable SaaS providers offer transparent pricing structures. However, business managers tend to overspend on services because paying a small amount per user seems negligible compared to the overall cost of licensing the software. As a result, it is common to spend a lot more than what is needed.

Unforeseen Costs and Charges

Pay attention to extra costs and hidden service charges. The complicated payment structure and business model of a SaaS provider can often result in price manipulations. You can avoid these payment hassles by reading the terms of your contract. Read independent customer reviews and expert opinions to get a better idea of the service quality.

Today's Business Software Landscape

Data Security

Whenever companies use BYOD, bring your own device, to improve productivity, they often struggle to keep devices secure. DaaS can help businesses overcome this problem by letting employees store important data in the cloud and use it when required.

Mobile Apps and APIs

Most popular SaaS applications offer API that can help businesses connect the service to other cloud-based apps and on-premises apps. This is a very useful solution that can enhance business productivity and improve existing features. Once connected, your team can share and use data freely with other team members, service partners, and business contacts.
