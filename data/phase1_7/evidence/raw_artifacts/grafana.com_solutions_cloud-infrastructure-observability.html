Why use Grafana Cloud for infrastructure and cloud observability?

Kubernetes monitoring made easy

  • Deploy in minutes with our custom Helm chart, identify fleet-level issues with a single snapshot, and seamlessly navigate from clusters to containers

  • AI-powered analysis for guidance on incident response, workload rightsizing, and cost-saving suggestions

  • Full-stack visibility to link metrics, logs, and traces for complete context on the state of your entire system

Streamlined multi-cloud monitoring

  • One tool to monitor AWS, Azure, and GCP

  • Start fast, go deep with easy setup, prebuilt dashboards, and granular configuration controls

  • Faster troubleshooting and reduced MTTR with clear, actionable insights for cross-cloud incidents

Optimized integrations

  • Easily install, connect, and maintain components without deep domain knowledge

  • Simplify Prometheus- and Grafana-based observability by bundling Alloy configuration snippets, tailored dashboards, and alert defaults

  • Support for more than 100 data sources, including Linux, Windows, MySQL, PostgreSQL, Kafka, and more
