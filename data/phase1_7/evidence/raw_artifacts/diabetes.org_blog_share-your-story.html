Share Your Story

Here at the ADA, our mission is to prevent and cure diabetes, and to improve the lives of all those impacted by it. By sharing your story, you help us raise awareness of diabetes so we can bend the curve on it once and for all. Will you help us awaken the world?

Video Testimonial
Added guidance for sharing your video

We ask that all videos be filmed in a horizontal format at the highest quality your phone or camera can share. 

For guidance on what to include in the video, please use the questions below for reference: 

  1. Can you tell us about your personal experience with diabetes?
  2. What do you wish more people understood about diabetes?
  3. What do you wish you knew when you were first diagnosed or what would you tell someone who was recently diagnosed? 

For additional questions about videos submissions, please email [email protected].
