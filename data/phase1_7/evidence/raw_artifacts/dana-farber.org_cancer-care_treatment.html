Treatment Centers and Clinical Services

We care for adults and children with cancer in our specialized treatment centers, focused on specific cancer types. Our teams of specialists work closely together to offer patients the latest therapies and clinical services, including access to innovative clinical trials.

Explore more information about our adult and pediatric treatment centers, clinical services, and specialty programs.

Adult Treatment Centers and Programs

Breast Oncology Center

We treat all types of breast cancers and have the following specialty programs:

  • Breast Cancer During Pregnancy Program
  • Ductal Carcinoma in Situ (DCIS) Program
  • Inflammatory Breast Cancer Program
  • Invasive Lobular Breast Cancer Program
  • Metastatic Breast Cancer Program
  • Program for Breast Cancer in Men
  • Program for Older Adults with Breast Cancer
  • Program for Patients with Breast Cancer Brain Metastases
  • Young and Strong Program for Young Adults with Breast Cancer

Cancer Genetics and Prevention

We provide advanced options in genetic testing across the spectrum of new and established inherited cancer syndromes, including:

  • Lynch Syndrome
  • Li-Fraumeni Syndrome and TP53
  • Hereditary Breast and Ovarian Cancer
  • Hereditary Gastrointestinal Cancer Syndromes
  • Hereditary Paraganglioma-Pheochromocytoma Syndrome (PGL/PCC)
  • Gastrointestinal Stromal Tumors (GIST)

Cutaneous (Skin) Cancer Treatment Center

We treat all types of skin cancers.

Centers for Early Detection and Interception

Centers for Early Detection and Interception is a multidisciplinary clinical and research program for individuals who are at increased risk of developing cancer. Specialty centers and programs include:

  • Center for Early Detection and Interception of Blood Cancers
  • Center for Early Detection and Interception of Gastrointestinal Tumors
  • Center for Early Detection and Interception of Gynecologic Tumors
  • Center for Early Detection and Interception of Solid Tumors
  • Multi-Cancer Early Detection Clinic

Gastrointestinal Cancer Treatment Center

We treat all types of gastrointestinal cancers and have the following specialty programs:

  • Center for Esophageal and Gastric Cancer
  • Colon and Rectal Cancer Center
  • Liver Cancer Center
  • Neuroendocrine and Carcinoid Tumors Program
  • Older Adults With Gastrointestinal Cancer Program
  • Pancreas and Biliary Tumor Center
  • Young-Onset Colorectal Cancer Center

Genitourinary Cancer Treatment Center

We treat all types of genitourinary cancers, including prostate, kidney, bladder, and testicular cancers.

Gynecologic Oncology Program

We treat all types gynecologic cancers.

Head and Neck Cancer Treatment Center

We treat all types of head and neck cancers and have the following specialty programs:

  • Center for Salivary and Rare Head and Neck Cancers
  • Thyroid Cancer Center

Hematologic Oncology Treatment Center

We treat all types of blood cancers and have the following specialty programs:

  • Lymphoma Program
  • Center for Chronic Lymphocytic Leukemia
  • Multiple Myeloma Program
  • Waldenström's Macroglobulinemia Program
  • Adult Leukemia Program
  • Adult Histiocyte Disorders Center
  • Older Adult Hematologic Malignancy (OHM) Program
  • Blastic Plasmacytoid Dendritic Cell Neoplasm (BPDCN) Center
  • Amyloidosis Program
  • Precursor Hematologic Conditions

Hematology Treatment Center

We treat all types of blood and bone marrow disorders.

Melanoma Treatment Center

We treat all types of melanoma skin cancer and have the following specialty programs:

  • Ocular Melanoma Center
  • Melanoma with Brain Metastasis

Neuro-Oncology Treatment Center

We treat all types of brain tumors and have the following specialty programs:

  • Adolescent and Young Adult (AYA) Neuro-Oncology Program
  • Brain Metastases Program
  • CNS Lymphoma Center
  • Spinal Tumor Program
  • Glioma Patient Support Program

Sarcoma Center

We treat all types of sarcoma and bone cancers, and a variety of mesenchymal tumors, and have the following specialty program:

  • Adolescent and Young Adult (AYA) Sarcoma Program

Susan F. Smith Center for Women's Cancers

The Susan F. Smith Center for Women's Cancers focuses on advancing patient care and research in women's cancers, including:

  • Breast Cancer
  • Gynecologic Cancers
  • Genetics and Prevention
  • Basic Research in Women's Cancers

Thoracic (Lung) Cancer Treatment Center

We treat all types of thoracic cancers and have the following specialty programs:

  • Chen-Huang Center for EGFR Mutant Lung Cancers
  • Young Lung Cancer Program

Adult Clinical Services

  • Adolescent and Young Adult (AYA) Neuro-Oncology Program
  • Adolescent and Young Adult (AYA) Sarcoma Program
  • Adult Fertility Preservation (Oncofertility) Program
  • Adult Palliative Care
  • Adult Psychosocial Care
  • Chemotherapy
  • Imaging Services (Radiology/Nuclear Medicine)
  • Oncology Hospital Medicine (Inpatient Care)
  • Oral Medicine and Oral Oncology
  • Pathology Services
  • Radiation Oncology
  • Surgical Oncology

Adult Specialty Centers and Programs

  • Adult Stem Cell Transplant Program
  • Adult Survivorship Program
  • Cancer Diagnostic Program
  • Center for Cancer Therapeutic Innovation
  • Cellular Therapies Program
  • Center for Immuno-Oncology
  • Zakim Center for Integrative Therapies and Healthy Living

Pediatric Treatment Centers and Programs

Blood Disorders Center (Benign Hematology)

We treat all types of childhood blood disorders and have the following specialty programs:

  • Bone Marrow Failure and Myelodysplastic Syndrome Program
  • Boston Bleeding Disorders Center
  • Platelet Function Disorders Program
  • Rare Anemia and Iron Disorders Program
  • Sickle Cell Disease Program
  • Thalassemia Program
  • Thrombosis, Anticoagulation, and Vascular Patency Program

Brain Tumor Center

We treat all types of childhood brain tumors and have the following specialty programs:

  • Childhood Glioma Program
  • Childhood Medulloblastoma Program

Hematologic Malignancy Center

We treat all types of childhood blood cancers and have the following specialty programs:

  • Childhood Histiocytosis Program
  • Childhood Leukemia Program
  • Childhood Lymphoma Program
  • Node Assessment Program (NAP)

Solid Tumor Center

We treat all types of childhood solid tumors and have the following specialty programs:

  • Childhood Bone and Soft Tissue Tumors Program
  • Childhood Endocrine-Oncology Program
  • Childhood Kidney Tumor Program 
  • Childhood Liver Tumor Center
  • Childhood Neuroblastoma Program 
  • Childhood Ocular Oncology Service
  • Childhood Rare Tumors Program 
  • Childhood Retinoblastoma Program

Pediatric Clinical Services

  • Adolescent and Young Adult (AYA) Neuro-Oncology Program
  • Adolescent and Young Adult (AYA) Sarcoma Program
  • Inpatient Care – Boston Children's Hospital
  • MIBG Therapy for Childhood Neuroblastoma
  • Outpatient Care – Jimmy Fund Clinic
  • Pediatric Cancer Genetic Risk Program
  • Pediatric Cancer Survivorship Programs
  • Pediatric CAR T-Cell Therapy Program
  • Pediatric Chemotherapy
  • Pediatric Fertility Preservation Program
  • Pediatric Imaging Services
  • Pediatric Palliative Care
  • Pediatric Pathology
  • Pediatric Psychosocial Care
  • Pediatric Radiation Oncology
  • Pediatric Stem Cell Transplant Program
