Accessible, flexible, world-class Warwick education for adult learners

For over 30 years, the Centre for Lifelong Learning has offered an award winning, inclusive, flexible, world-class education for adult learners, regardless of background, and at all ages and stages of their lives. Through our ongoing commitment to community, regional and (inter)national engagement, we deliver multi-disciplinary courses, aligned with people-focused professions. We champion real world, applied teaching, formal and informal learning, and research and scholarship. Our vision is to:

  • Be a global centre for lifelong learning, with a strong sense of identity and community.
  • Offer accessible and flexible continuing and professional education at Warwick for those who are under represented in higher education.
  • Support the diverse needs and experiences of our learners and staff, and work together to ensure a mutually supportive professional and learning community.
  • Equip our learners with the aspirations, knowledge, skills and values needed for personal and professional growth.
