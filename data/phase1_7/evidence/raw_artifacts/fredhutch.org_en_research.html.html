Pioneering Scientific Research

Fred Hutch Cancer Center researchers are tireless risk takers and innovators who are known for their rich collaborations across scientific disciplines in pursuit of life-saving discoveries. The next great advances are already underway in our labs.

We’re tackling cancer from every angle in our state-of-the-art research labs and in collaborative projects that span the globe.

Clinical Trials and Studies

Clinical trials and volunteer studies are a vital and critical component of disease research. Through the Cancer Consortium, we typically have more than 750 active cancer clinical trials enrolling nearly 7,000 participants. 

Survivorship Program

Our Fred Hutch Survivorship Program offers support, consultation and education for years after cancer treatment is over. 

Long-Term Follow-Up

Fred Hutch has two Long-Term Follow-Up (LTFU) programs focused on patients who have undergone a bone-marrow or stem-cell transplant and immunotherapy.
