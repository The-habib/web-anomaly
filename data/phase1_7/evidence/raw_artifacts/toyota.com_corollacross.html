PERFORMANCE

Power for every plot twist and turn.

  • Powertrain Options

    Power your way. Corolla Cross offers both gas and hybrid powertrain options, giving you the perfect balance of power and efficiency to match your lifestyle.

  • Hybrid Performance

    Smart, sporty and efficient. With its 42 combined EPA-estimated mpg rating and sport-tuned suspension, Corolla Cross Hybrid makes every drive feel fresh and fun.

  • Gas Powertrain

    Corolla Cross’ 2.0L engine brings a rugged vibe and the oomph to get you where you’re going. An EPA-estimated combined 32 mpg rating keeps the fun rolling as the suspension smoothly adapts to every twist and turn.

    Enjoy the best of both worlds with the convenience of an automatic transmission and the sporty feel of a manual drive. Corolla Cross Hybrid’s available paddle shifters allow you to manually shift up or down directly from the steering wheel.

DESIGN

Built to turn dreamers into doers.

Corolla Cross is all about bringing your ideas to life. From its striking exterior to its spacious interior, its versatile design is built to handle everything you need to do exactly what you want.

  1. Exterior
  2. Interior

Impressive Style

Bold, rugged and impossible to ignore. Corolla Cross brings fresh attitude with a striking new grille insert and a silhouette that means business.

Hybrid Flair

Corolla Cross Hybrid’s sleek, high-tech grille, dynamic two-tone design and new bumper inspire you to take your next step with confidence.

A Perfect Fit

Impressive in looks and versatile in size. With Corolla Cross’ seating for up to five and a spacious rear cargo area, your possibilities are endless.

18-In. Alloy Wheels

Complementing Corolla Cross’ athletic stance are available gloss-black painted, edgy 18-in. alloy wheels that balance a look that’s sporty and chic.

Spacious Interior

Corolla Cross’ 8.1-in. ground clearance and ample head room naturally provide a heightened view over traffic, for a more confident drive.

Cargo Capacity

60/40 rear seats provide adaptable cargo space that gives you flexibility to pack for road trips, getaways and everything in between.

Heated Front Seats and Steering Wheel

When things get chilly, the available heated steering wheel and heated front seats bring the warmth.

Rear Seat Vents

Keep your rear passengers in the comfort zone. With standard rear-seat vents, everyone riding in Corolla Cross feels right at home.

Beyond Zero is Toyota’s vision to reach carbon neutrality with our products, services and operations, and go beyond, as we find new ways to make a positive impact on our planet and society. The Hybrid EV Toyota Corolla Cross is one of the many ways we are reducing carbon emissions to drive toward our Beyond Zero vision.

Corolla Cross Hybrid is a part of the Hybrid EV Family.

We currently offer more low and zero emission vehicles combined than any other automaker, to give customers numerous choices to reduce their carbon footprint.

Our lineup brings you more than just your choice of electrified powertrains: Hybrid EV, Plug-in Hybrid EV, Battery EV and Fuel Cell EV. Whatever you’re looking for in a ride—size, style, efficiency, speed—there’s a vehicle to suit your lifestyle that allows you to put more electrified miles on the road.

Whether Toyota is decreasing its plastic waste, supporting water conservation efforts, or expanding programs that protect critical species, we are committed to reducing our environmental footprint and creating a positive impact on society. See Environmental Sustainability (toyota.com) for more information.
