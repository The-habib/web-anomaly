A PREMIER ARTS DESTINATION

In Houston’s historic Third Ward, UH’s Arts District brings art, music & culture to life.

At the center of it all is the Kathrine G. McGovern College of the Arts—fondly known as KGMCA—home to the School of Art, the Moores School of Music, the School of Theatre & Dance, Public Art UHS, the Blaffer Art Museum, and the Cynthia Woods Mitchell Center for the Arts. This is where your Cougar pride finds its rhythm with the Spirit of Houston marching band, where art exhibitions are always free for UH students, and where live theatre—both timeless and brand-new—hits the stage. Creativity isn’t just celebrated here, it’s part of everyday life.

OUR IMPACT

At the Kathrine G. McGovern College of the Arts, creativity drives powerful impact. As a key contributor to Houston’s $26 billion creative economy, KGMCA helps fuel more than 180,000 arts jobs across the city. Each year, our students and faculty produce over 200 performances and exhibitions, advancing the arts locally and globally. With 1,500 students from 60+ countries and more than 30 degree programs across three schools, we are shaping the next generation of creative leaders.

  • $26B City of Houston Creative Economy
  • 180K Arts Jobs in City of Houston
  • 200+ Arts Jobs in City of Houston

LEARNING BEYOND THE CLASSROOM

At the Kathrine G. McGovern College of the Arts, we believe your education should extend far beyond the studio, classroom, or rehearsal hall. Our students gain real-world experience through internships, mentorships, immersive courses, and global opportunities—equipping them to thrive as artists and professionals.
