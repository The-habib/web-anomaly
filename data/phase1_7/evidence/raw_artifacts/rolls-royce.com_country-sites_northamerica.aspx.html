Our U.S. Story

Rolls-Royce is an engineering company that designs, manufactures and services complex power and propulsion systems, primarily for the civil aviation, defense and energy sectors, leveraging advanced technologies to deliver more efficient solutions across air, sea and land.

For more than a century, Rolls-Royce has helped move America forward. Our engines carry U.S. service members into mission-critical operations and bring them home safely. We help keep the lights on when it matters most, powering hospitals, data centers and essential infrastructure when the grid can’t, even as AI and electrification stretch the limits of today’s capacity. In the skies, our technology helps connect families, businesses and communities across the country and around the world.

Our work spans 26 states, employing over 5,000 people directly and supporting thousands more jobs through our supply chain. In 2024 alone, our operations contributed $6.2B to the U.S. economy.

Our U.S. Impact

In 2025, Rolls-Royce released an Economic Impact Report to answer a central question: What is the annual economic and social impact of Rolls-Royce’s operations across the United States?

The report highlights Rolls-Royce’s contribution to the U.S. economy, measuring the impact of our operations, suppliers, and partners across every business unit and tracing how value flows through the supply chain. Today, Rolls-Royce continues to invest in American people, communities and capabilities, strengthening U.S. industry and delivering when it matters most.

Defense
Strengthening Global Security. Delivering Power and Propulsion Excellence.

Our engines help power our Armed Forces and protect national sovereignty for the U.S. and our allies, safeguarding the missions of U.S. service members.

From the C-130, V-22 Osprey and the MQ-25, our proven engines helped us win iconic programs like MV-75 FLRAA and B-52 CERP. Whether deployed in contested environments or responding to wildfires and natural disasters, we enable vital 24/7 humanitarian and security missions when and where it counts.

With 92 million AE engine flight hours completed on our dual-use AE engine family, service members trust Rolls-Royce engines not just to fly, but to bring them home safely. On land and at sea we provide the U.S. military with proven propulsion and drive systems that have been trusted for more than half a century.

Civil Aviation
 Shaping the Future of Flight.

Rolls-Royce is evolving its role in U.S. civil aviation – from growing our presence with U.S. customers of widebody aircraft to preparing for the next-generation of narrowbody flight with our UltraFan program.

Rolls-Royce powers passenger and cargo flights throughout the U.S. on the Boeing 787, Boeing 757, and Airbus A330 and A350 platforms. Including business aviation, our engines have logged more than 500 million flight hours globally - helping people get where they need to go, reliably and safely every time.

Energy Solutions
Powering Critical Infrastructure.

Under our Power Systems division, we deliver trusted solutions for mission-critical environments — across marine, energy and industrial sectors. As AI and electrification stretch the limits of current infrastructure, our systems keep essential applications running, ensuring they are never offline. And we’re building these systems here in the U.S. from our manufacturing sites in Mankato, Minnesota and Aiken, South Carolina.

With decades of experience serving the most demanding applications in power generation, Rolls-Royce is well positioned to meet the country’s growing demand for dependable, American-made, no-fail energy systems from emergency backup systems to prime power generation including gas power plants and Small Modular Reactors (currently in U.S. regulatory review).

Locations

Washington, D.C.

The Rolls-Royce North America headquarters is located in Reston, Virginia, just outside the Nation's Capital. The site provides management direction for all Rolls-Royce businesses and operations in the U.S. and Canada, and houses Rolls-Royce North America corporate support functions, including finance, human resources, legal, government relations and corporate communications. Reston is also the worldwide headquarters for the Rolls-Royce Defense and Corporate & Regional Aircraft businesses.

Indianapolis

Rolls-Royce Corporation, Indianapolis, Indiana

Rolls-Royce has become a leading industry supplier in the U.S. with a significant and growing presence. Today, more Rolls-Royce products are built in Indianapolis than anywhere else in the world.  Approximately 4,000 employees work in Indianapolis in manufacturing, assembly, test, engineering and a variety of staff support roles.

Indianapolis businesses includes: Defense, LibertyWorks, Civil Small & Medium Engines, Marine and Helicopters.

Metro Detroit

Rolls-Royce Solutions America is the North American subsidiary of our Power Systems division, which has its global headquarters in Friedrichshafen, Germany and produces products under the mtu brand. Power Systems has major facilities in Novi, Michigan (regional headquarters); Aiken, South Carolina (engine manufacturing, machining, R&D, and remanufacturing); and Mankato, Minnesota (power generation sales, service, engineering and manufacturing). It also has a product training center in Canton, Michigan, a distribution center in Brownstown, Michigan and several sales and service locations across the U.S.
