Not completely sure of your plan? Clearing is your chance to look again

Whether you are rethinking your course, reconsidering your university, or getting prepared just in case, you’ve come to the right place. Register your interest now and stay up-to-date with Clearing at Reading.

Why the University of Reading?

We are a university focused on your future. Here are just a few reasons to look again.

  • Degrees designed for employability – Gain in-demand skills and real-world experience. We are ranked in the top 10 universities in the UK for work experience support (Higherin's Top 50 Universities for Work Experience 2026-27).
  • Guaranteed accommodation – Our University accommodation is on, or a quick walk away from, our award-winning parkland campus (guarantee criteria apply).
  • Award-winning scholarships – The University of Reading has been named "University of the Year for Scholarships and Bursaries 2026" in the Times and Sunday Times Good University Guide. High-achieving clearing applicants will be invited to apply for our Global Sustainability Leaders Scholarships – get £6,000 a year, plus a tailored development scheme.

Frequently asked questions about Clearing 2026 at the University of Reading

When does Clearing 2026 open and closes?

Clearing opens on 2 July 2026 for applicants that already have their final results, and stays open until 17:00 on 5 August. After the A level embargo, Clearing will reopen on 13 August (A Level results day). The last day of Clearing will be 19 October 2026. 

I am an international student. Can I apply through Clearing?

Yes, you can apply to a course at the University of Reading through Clearing even if you’re based outside of the UK. Read our dedicated guide to Clearing for international applicants.

How do I apply for Clearing at the University of Reading?

You can apply online or, from results day onward, through our dedicated hotline. See our step-by-step guide explaining how applying through Clearing works, what you need to do and what to expect.

Is university accommodation available if I apply through Clearing?

We are able to guarantee single-occupancy accommodation to students who apply via Clearing at the University of Reading. Find out what criteria you need to meet to secure your place in university halls.

Can I get student finance if I apply through Clearing?

There are plenty of options available for funding your studies at the University of Reading, including the Global Sustainability Leaders Scholarships, which is also available for eligible Clearing applicants.

I’m the parent/guardian of a student who’s going through the Clearing application process. How can I support them?

UCAS has put together a Clearing toolkit for parents that includes what you need know and practical tips to support a student through the Clearing process.
