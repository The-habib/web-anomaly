Colleges & Schools

Aerospace

Get a nationally acclaimed degree from the School of Aerospace Sciences. We're home to the largest nonmilitary training fleet of aircraft in the world.

Arts & Sciences

Gain the insights to lead and the skills to make a tangible impact at the College of Arts & Sciences.

Business & Public Administration

Earn your degree at the Nistler College of Business & Public Administration. Our accreditation places us in the world's top 6% of business colleges.

Education & Human Development

Pass the torch to tomorrow's leaders by becoming a licensed educator at the College of Education & Human Development. 

Engineering & Mines

Learn with world leaders in energy research and technology at the College of Engineering & Mines.

Graduate School

Continue your legacy of leading by pursuing your graduate education at the School of Graduate Studies.

Law

Make a difference by earning your law degree at the School of Law. We're considered among the best values in the nation.

Medicine & Health Sciences

Gain skills that drive the world forward at the School of Medicine & Health Sciences. We're home to a top rural medicine program.

Nursing & Professional Disciplines

Strengthen your in-demand health care skills at the College of Nursing & Professional Disciplines.
