        Managed Services

        Let's empower your digital transformation through network operations

        Ericsson Managed Services delivers comprehensive, end-to-end network operations and optimization, including field operations for communication services providers (CSPs), backed by advanced automation, artificial intelligence (AI), and deep global expertise. 

        Our portfolio spans multi-vendor, multi-technology environments and empowers service providers to maximize efficiency, elevate service quality, and optimize network performance. With data-driven Network Service Operations and Optimization, AI-enhanced Energy Infrastructure Operations, and predictive Customer Experience Insights, we deliver streamlined operations, reduced energy consumption, fewer site visits, and proactive customer care – driving higher satisfaction, lower emissions, and improved business outcomes. 

        Ericsson Managed Services is the bridge between today’s network operations and tomorrow’s autonomous networks transformation, combining deep operational expertise with automation, analytics, and AI to translate business intent into network action. It ensures performance through closed-loop assurance, drives agility through intelligent orchestration, and builds trust through continuous governance. 

        Let’s join forces to create autonomous, secure network operations that unlock new value and drive the next wave of digital transformation.

        Unlock the benefits of Ericsson Managed Services today

        Customer experience has emerged as the primary differentiator in competitive telecom markets. Our Experience Centric Managed Services shifts the focus from traditional technical KPIs to service and customer experience KPIs, including service availability, accessibility, service activation time, and service response time.

        Operational efficiency is a critical concern for telcos that manage increasingly complex multi-vendor, multi-generation networks. Ericsson's data-driven operations approach delivers substantial reductions in TCO through intelligent automation and optimization.

        Our managed services portfolio includes multiple AI-driven energy optimization capabilities that reduce power consumption while maintaining or improving network performance.

        Ericsson embeds security intelligence into the operational DNA of your network, delivering both the highest performance and the strongest defense against sophisticated cyber threats to critical telecommunications infrastructure.

        Ericsson Managed Services enables you to monetize 5G investments through differentiated connectivity offerings. The AI-powered intent-based operations within the Operations Engine translate business requirement into real-time network actions, supporting premium 5G services like network slicing at scale.

        Meet Ericsson Operations Engine

        Ericsson Operations Engine is the award-winning operating model created by Ericsson to run multivendor, multi-technology network and field operations worldwide for our mobile and fixed managed services customers. 

        It is the result of more than 20 years of experience and leadership in the telecom managed services market. That experience is combined with expertise and domain knowledge gained from processing an unparalleled multinational, multivendor digital data store and training sophisticated algorithms to operate telecom networks. 

        Ever evolving to be future-ready, its transformational capabilities underpin customer journeys to data-driven and autonomous network and field operations, while maximizing customer experience.

        Explore our offerings further

        Ericsson Network Service Operations and Optimization

        Delivers a full end-to-end operational service across multi-vendor and multi-technology environments, including mobile, fixed, converged, and FWA networks. It transforms existing operations, including field operations, through a data-driven model that enhances network availability, speeds restoration, and reduces incidents, work orders, and truck rolls, ultimately lowering operational costs while providing predictable, high-quality service outcomes.

        Ericsson Energy Infrastructure Operations

        Provides a comprehensive, AI-enabled solution for managing and optimizing energy consumption across network sites. By applying predictive analytics to both active elements – through Predictive Cell Energy Management – and passive infrastructure – through Predictive Site Energy Operations – it reduces energy waste, improves operational efficiency, limits the need for on-site interventions, and significantly decreases environmental impact.

        Ericsson Customer Experience Insights

        Focuses on elevating customer satisfaction and business performance by identifying key drivers of negative sentiment and service degradation. Using AI-powered 360° analytics across surveys, complaints, social media, and network KPIs, it predicts issues before they occur, performs root-cause analysis, and recommends proactive actions that improve ARPU, reduce churn, and strengthen customer loyalty and acquisition.
