    Six Colleges. One University.

    Cal Poly's six academic colleges offer more than 60 undergraduate majors and over 50 graduate programs.

    College of

    Agriculture, Food and Environmental Sciences

    One of the nation’s most prestigious undergraduate agriculture programs, the College of Agriculture, Food and Environmental Sciences takes you into the field, the farm and the forest to apply cutting-edge techniques to the ancient challenge of feeding a growing society, while also building long-term stability for our food and agricultural systems.

    more about College of Agriculture, Food and Environmental Sciences

    College of

    Architecture and Environmental Design

    The College of Architecture and Environmental Design offers a hands-on approach to working in all aspects of the built environment, from designing homes and gardens to planning cities.

    More About the College of Architecture and Environmental Design

    College of

    Engineering

    A national leader in engineering education, the College of Engineering promotes project-based learning to link theory with hands-on practice to solve the future’s most pressing technical challenges.

    More about the College of Engineering

    College of

    Liberal Arts

    The College of Liberal Arts houses seventeen departments offering programs in the humanities, social sciences, communications and performing arts that share focus on the evolving nature of human experience and expression in the modern world.

    More about the College of Liberal Arts

    BailEy College of

    Science and Mathematics

    The Bailey College of Science and Mathematics prepares you to become a leader in an increasingly scientific and technological society through education, research and practical experience.

    More about the Bailey College of Science and Mathematics

    Orfalea College of

    business

    At the Orfalea College of Business, you gain expertise in the fields of management, finance, economics and entrepreneurship by working with real companies in your community and developing your own business ideas.

    More about the Orfalea College of Business
