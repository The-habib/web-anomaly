Standard Blind Spot Monitor With Rear Cross-Traffic Alert Standard Blind Spot Monitor With Rear Cross-Traffic Alert To help look out for your safety, Corolla Hatchback's Blind Spot Monitor (BSM) is designed to warn you when a vehicle in the next lane enters your vehicle’s blind spot on either side of the vehicle. And when you’re in reverse, Rear Cross Traffic Alert (RCTA) is designed to help detect vehicles approaching from either side.
Integrated Backup Camera Integrated Backup Camera When you’re in reverse, the standard backup camera activates and gives you a view of what’s happening directly behind your car. Let this feature help you when you’re backing up or parking in a tight spot.

Armed with these standard safety features, Corolla Hatchback can help you drive with confidence and avoid accidents in the first place. This level of safety is standard on every Corolla Hatchback.
