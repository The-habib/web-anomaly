Apply to UND

We're excited you're ready to invest in a lifetime of success.

If you are pursuing an online degree or certificate, the application process is the same as an on-campus student. You do not need to complete a separate application.

Those taking individual Self-Paced Enroll Anytime Courses or Personal & Professional Development Courses are not required to complete the formal admissions application.

Undergraduate students who leave the University for at least one semester, not including the summer semester, are required to apply for readmission.

Apply for Readmission

Graduate students who leave the University for at least one semester, not including summer semester, are required to apply for readmission through the School of Graduate Studies. If the last term of enrollment was over 3 years ago, a new application must be submitted.

APPLY FOR READMISSION - NON-DEGREE

APPLY FOR READMISSION - DEGREE- OR CERTIFICATE-SEEKING

Admission Applications

Follow the application below that best meets your circumstances.

Freshmen

Apply as a freshman if you're currently in high school or you've graduated from high school but have never enrolled at another college or university.

Apply as a Freshman

Transfer Students

Apply as a transfer student if:

  • You're a high school graduate who previously registered or enrolled at another college or university.
  • You would like to obtain an additional bachelor’s degree or undergrad certificate.

Apply as a Transfer Student

Graduate

Apply to specific programs (except for law or medicine) if you have earned a bachelor's degree or equivalent from an accredited college or university.

Apply to Graduate School

International Students

Apply as an international student if you are not a U.S. citizen, permanent resident or refugee and will need a visa to study in the U.S.

International Admissions Process

Military and Veteran Students

Apply as a military or veteran student if you're an active-duty service member, previously served in the U.S. armed forces or plan to use military education benefits.

Military Admissions Process

Non-Degree Students

Apply as a non-degree student if you just want to take a class or two at UND.

Apply to Take a Class

Medical School

Apply to medical school through the American Medical College Application Service (AMCAS). All other School of Medicine & Health Sciences (SMHS) programs should use admission process that fits them best.

SMHS Admissions

Law School

Apply as a law student if earned a bachelor's degree or equivalent from an accredited college or university and have registered with the Credential Assembly Service.

Law School Admissions

 

For Aviation Students Only

Aviation Management and Commercial Aviation applications for fall 2027 opened on Aug. 1, 2026, and close on Oct. 31, 2026. Applicants must apply during this window using the UND application for consideration.

*Aviation degrees are NOT available on the Common App.

Fall 2027 Application Review
Applications for fall 2027 for the Aviation Management and Commercial Aviation programs will close on Oct. 31, 2026. 

After Oct. 31, UND Admissions will review completed applications, which consist of official transcripts with core high school course requirements met. ​Admission decisions will be communicated no later than January 2027.

GPA Requirements
To be considered for these competitive programs, a minimum unweighted high school GPA of 3.25 (2.60 college GPA for transfer students), official transcripts and a senior course schedule are required.
