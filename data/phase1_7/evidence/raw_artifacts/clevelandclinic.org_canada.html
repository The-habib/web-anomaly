Why Choose Cleveland Clinic Canada?

Patient-centred care:

We don’t just care for your health conditions. We care about you. That means our providers take the time to listen to what’s important to you before recommending next steps.

Skilled collaborative providers:

Our multi-disciplinary team of physicians and wellness experts provide in-person care in downtown and midtown Toronto.

Globally recognized:

Cleveland Clinic is a trusted healthcare leader. We're recognized in the U.S. and throughout the world for our expertise and care.

Virtual care:

Receive a diagnosis, prescription and/or referral from the comfort of your home with on-demand virtual care at Cleveland Clinic Canada.
