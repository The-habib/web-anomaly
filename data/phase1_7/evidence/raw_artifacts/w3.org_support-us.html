Support us

Read this page in:
  • 中文（简体）
  • 日本語

The support from generous individuals and organizations such as you, who share our values, make a huge difference to our operations as a public-interest non-profit, and help us to achieve our vision.

Why support W3C? We work to support the community that develops web standards, the building blocks of our digitally connected world. We work to ensure the long-term growth of the open, interoperable web that caters for accessibility, privacy, security, and internationalization.

Ways you can support the World Wide Web Consortium

  • Donations

    Support our vision for a web that works, for everyone. W3C recognizes that giving takes many forms. Make a one-time or regular donation, or reach out to discuss how our work aligns with your giving portfolio.

    Donate

  • Sponsoring

    Your sponsorship can directly contribute to our work on accessibility, internationalization, privacy and security which guide our technical work and community engagement, and support business ecosystem development.

    Sponsor

  • Partnering

    We are actively seeking funding opportunities to expand our work and support our community. Get in touch if you are interested in partnering with us through grants, fellowship, and other mechanisms, to strengthen our programming.

    Contact us

World Wide Web Consortium, Inc. is a nonprofit, tax-exempt charitable organization under Section 501(c)(3) of the U.S. Internal Revenue Code EIN: 84-4023862.
