What sets Nextcloud apart?

Disconnected apps create risks

Switching between different tools is more than cumbersome and inefficient: it also increases the chance for mistakes that can result in data leaks.

One platform brings unified experience

Nextcloud offers the most comprehensive, deeply integrated on-premises digital workspace on the market, replacing dozens of other tools. Easy integration lets you use Nextcloud alongside other apps you have.

Simple IT = better security and control

Having a complete, all-in-one solution simplifies the IT infrastructure and makes compliance and IT management easier for the administrators. One comprehensive platform built on open standards is the most resilient digitalization path.

Run Nextcloud Hub in a private cloud

You can run Nextcloud Hub the way you want: on-premises, with a trusted hosting provider, or even with a hybrid configuration. There is flexibility for all infrastructure and compliance needs.

On-premises

Running Nextcloud on-premises provides ultimate control over who has access to the data, when and how. No third party access or date exchange to external servers is required. Even a completely air-gapped setup physically separated from the internet is possible.

  • 100% control
  • Cost-optimized
  • Easy integration with existing infrastructure
