Alumni Relations

Nurturing the lifelong relationship between the University of Aberdeen and our graduate community

Alumni Relations

The Aberdeen Family

From the day you begin studying at Aberdeen you become a part of our family. On graduation you automatically become a member of our global alumni family, with over 100,000 alumni in 170 countries.

From Aberdeen To...

The University of Aberdeen boasts a fantastic array of alumni working in diverse industries across the globe. See what these inspiring individuals are doing now and how they got where they are today!

Support Your University

Throughout its proud history the University has received the support of alumni. Find out more about how you can join this group.

University Merchandise

We have a selection of University-branded clothing and gifts including hoodies, mugs, stationery and more available for purchase.
About

Alumni Relations helps nurture the lifelong relationship between alma mater and graduate. We keep our alumni updated, provide benefits and services and volunteering opportunities, organise events, and more!
Stay in Touch

As one of our 100,000+ alumni living in 170+ countries, you are an important part of the Aberdeen family. We love keeping our graduates up to date, but we need your permission to stay in touch!
Get Involved

Volunteering your time with the University of Aberdeen is a wonderful way to stay connected to the University while helping current and prospective students and fellow alumni. Why not get involved?
Benefits and Services

As a graduate of the University of Aberdeen you are entitled to an amazing selection of benefits and services. Find out how to take advantage of them here.
Events and Reunions

We hold regular in-person and virtual events for our graduates covering a huge range of topics. Find out what's coming up, or get in touch to see how we can support your class reunion!
Our Alumni

Our alumni work in a huge range of professions and areas. Find out what some of our graduates have gone on to do, or browse our alumni bookshelf for books written by Aberdeen alumni!
Giving

The life of the University of Aberdeen over 500 years has been sustained by a generous history of philanthropic giving. Find out how you can give back to benefit today's students.

Alumni Bookshelf

Browse our Alumni Bookshelf, showcasing the work of just a few of our alumni authors! Do you have a book to add? Get in touch!
