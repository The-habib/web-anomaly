Welcome to a city open to all, a university ever-evolving

What does our ancient tradition of modern thinking mean for you? It means being at the forefront of change - the chance to explore your potential at a research-intensive university that's home to over 525 years of discovery. Since 1495, we've held space for debate, discussion, and discourse, and now it's your turn to join us.

Are you ready to join the Boundary Breakers and study in Aberdeen?

Your future is what you make it, so get started now and apply to join us.

Despite being over 530 years old, we are not bound by tradition or the status quo. At Aberdeen we go:

  • Beyond disciplines, with a flexible approach to study so you can tailor your degree to your interests and career goals, and bring the global, cross-disciplinary mindset employers are really looking for.
  • Beyond borders, welcoming staff and students from over 130 countries worldwide, to an age-old campus nestled between the ocean and the city in the friendly Northeast of Scotland.
  • Beyond ordinary solutions, working together to spark lasting change in alignment with the UN Sustainability Development Goals. We work towards a cleaner energy future and pioneering preventative healthcare solutions.

Follow your True North home, with an offer of accommodation guaranteed for all new students to the University

At Aberdeen, we know studying is only one part of your university experience. It's also your new home.

Our exciting city is nestled between the mountains and the sea, the perfect place to start a new adventure.

Our accommodation is not only a safe and supportive environment, with a dedicated team providing 24 hours support and information, but a place where you'll make friends for life.

No matter where you come from, you'll find home in Aberdeen.

A city beyond limits

Scotland's alternative city is closer than you think. Home to innovation and culture, sandy beaches and rolling hills, discover a unique student city that has the best of all worlds, right on the doorstep of a historic campus.
