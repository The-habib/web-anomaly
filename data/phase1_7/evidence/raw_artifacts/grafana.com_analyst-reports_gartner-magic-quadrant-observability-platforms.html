Grafana Labs: A Leader in the 2026 Gartner® Magic Quadrant™ for Observability Platforms

Read the report

Grafana Labs is honored to be recognized – for the third consecutive year – as a Leader in the 2026 Gartner Magic Quadrant for Observability Platforms.

Gartner evaluated 19 vendors based on their Ability to Execute and Completeness of Vision, and Grafana Labs was positioned furthest to the right for Completeness of Vision for the second year in a row. 

We believe this recognition reflects what we've built with Grafana Cloud – a fully managed, open, and composable observability platform built for scale. With actually useful AI at its core, Grafana Cloud helps organizations see, understand, and act on all their disparate data to move at the speed of their ambitions.
