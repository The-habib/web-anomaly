Your Campus Journey

Our world-class campus is made up of a thriving, diverse, and inclusive community. With an inspirational blend of welcoming, modern facilities and buildings as well as vibrant, green outdoor spaces. It is an excellent place for everyone to study, research, learn, work, and live.

We benefit from a semi-rural location in the heart of England, meaning that while the countryside is nearby and easily accessible, we are also only three miles (5km) from the buzzing centre of Coventry and on the border with Warwickshire.

To enable you to navigate to, from and around our campus, whether that's on foot or by another mode of travel, you'll find all the information you need right here.
