OCaml on the internet

Join Our Communities!

Ask and answer questions, share and discuss OCaml-related articles and posts, let people know about your projects and find collaborators

    Discussion

  • Discuss

    Discuss with the community, ask questions and share your work on OCaml - https://discuss.ocaml.org/

  • Reddit

    Subreddit for discussions, memes, project updates, and sharing web articles and news - https://reddit.com/r/ocaml/

    Development

  • GitHub

    Submit bug reports and feature requests for the OCaml compiler - https://github.com/ocaml

  • Stackoverflow

    Ask and help answer OCaml questions - https://stackoverflow.com/questions/tagged/ocaml

    Chat

  • Discord

    Chat with other members of the community on Discord - https://discord.gg/cCYQbqN

  • IRC Chat

    Chat with other members of the community at #ocaml on the Libera Chat IRC network irc://irc.libera.chat/#ocaml

  • Matrix

    Chat in the #ocaml-lang Matrix chat room, or join the #ocaml-space Matrix space

  • Zulip

    Chat with other members of the community on Zulip - https://ocaml.zulipchat.com

    Video

  • Watch

    Community videos such as previous OCaml Conference presentation - https://watch.ocaml.org/videos/recently-added

  • Twitch

    Live Streams about OCaml - https://twitch.tv/search?term=ocaml

  • YouTube

    OCaml on YouTube - https://www.youtube.com/@OCamlLang

    Microblogging

  • Twitter

    Latest announcements and events from the community - https://twitter.com/ocaml_org

  • Bluesky

    OCaml on Bluesky - https://bsky.app/profile/ocaml.org

  • Mastodon

    Both the types.pl and discuss.systems Mastodon homeservers enjoy popularity

    Mailing Lists

  • Caml User's Mailing List

    Share experience, exchange ideas and code, and report on applications - https://inbox.ci.dev/caml-list
