  • ROTC & Military Life
        • ROTC & Military Life

        • The unique military aspect of a VMI cadetship prepares the next generation of leaders to serve with discipline, honor, and integrity.

        • ROTC at VMI

        • VMI is among the very few colleges that offer ROTC options in Army, Navy, Marine Corps, Air Force, and Space Force.

        • Naval ROTC

        • The Naval ROTC Unit provides two programs in which cadets may enroll: US Navy or US Marine Corps.

        • Other Ways to Serve

        • Cadets can prepare for other roles in government, intelligence, cyber defense, and public safety, both at the state and federal level.

        • Army ROTC

        • VMI boasts the largest ROTC program in the country and an award-winning Ranger Challenge Team.

        • Air Force ROTC

        • The Air Force ROTC program prepares cadets for service in the U.S. Air Force or U.S. Space Force air, space, and cyberspace domains.

        • Corps Military Experience

        • From the very first day of post, the Corps of Cadets is guided in a military lifestyle and code of conduct.

Admissions & Aid

Why Choose VMI?

Virginia Military Institute develops leaders of character through a challenging academic, military, and leadership experience unlike any other college. With nationally recognized academics, a close-knit Corps of Cadets, and graduates who lead in every profession, VMI prepares you for success long after graduation. 

The VMI Difference

  • Exceptional Academics – Earn a nationally respected undergraduate degree in engineering, the sciences, humanities, or social sciences while learning from dedicated, award-winning faculty in small classes that foster mentorship and individualized support.  
  • Leadership Through Experience – Develop the confidence, discipline, and leadership skills employers and graduate schools value through VMI’s unique military environment and the nation’s largest ROTC program.  
  • A Community Like No Other – Join a close-knit Corps of Cadets built on honor, integrity, self-discipline, and the lifelong friendships forged through the “Brother Rat” experience.  
  • An Investment in Your Future – VMI offers scholarships, grants, and other financial aid opportunities to help make a world-class education more affordable for qualified students.
