Governance and leadership

Photo: WFP/Giulio Napolitano
Photo credit

The World Food Programme (WFP) is governed by the WFP Executive Board, which consists of 36 Member States and provides intergovernmental support, direction and supervision of activities.

WFP is headed by an Executive Director, who is appointed jointly by the UN Secretary-General and the Director-General of the Food and Agriculture Organization of the United Nations. The Executive Director is appointed for fixed five-year terms and is responsible for the administration of WFP as well as the implementation of its programmes, projects and other activities. Since 1 June 2026, the post has been held by Carl Skau on an acting basis.

WFP also has one Deputy Executive Director and three Assistant Executive Directors.

Senior leadership

Carl Skau - Acting Executive Director, Deputy Executive Director and Chief Operating Officer

Carl Skau is the Acting Executive Director of the World Food Programme (WFP), appointed by United Nations Secretary-General Antonio Guterres to serve as interim head of WFP until the designation of its next Executive Director.

Rania Dagash-Kamara - Assistant Executive Director, Partnerships and Innovation

Rania Dagash-Kamara leads the organization’s engagement and resource mobilization activities with global partners, international financial institutions and programme countries. She also oversees WFP’s expanding work in innovation.

Stephen Omollo - Assistant Executive Director, Workplace and Management

Stephen Omollo is WFP's Assistant Executive Director for Workplace and Management. In this role, he leads the Department overseeing human resources, management services, security, technology and the promotion of wellness. Stephen is also passionate about ensuring the duty of care for all personnel, fostering a safe, inclusive and healthy work environment.

Matthew Hollingworth - Assistant Executive Director, Programme Operations

Matthew Hollingworth is WFP’s Assistant Executive Director for Programme Operations. In this role, he leads WFP’s work in assessment and analysis, programme and policy design, and supply chain to ensure WFP delivers on its mandate of saving lives and changing lives.

Christophe Monier - Chief Financial Officer

Christophe Monier took up the role of Chief Financial Officer in August 2026. Full biography to follow.

Valerie Guarnieri - Chief of Staff a.i.

Valerie Guarnieri is the Chief of Staff, ad interim of the World Food Programme (WFP). From 2018 through 2025, Ms. Guarnieri served as WFP’s Assistant Executive Director for Programme Operations, overseeing the development of WFP’s Strategic Plan and the design and delivery of food, cash, and technical assistance programmes to meet emergency needs, strengthen human capital, and improve food security. Previously, she led the global programme function, and held regional and country leadership positions, based in Kenya, the Philippines and Zimbabwe.
