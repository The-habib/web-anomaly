Panix V-Colo Plan Features, Options, and Prices

Standard Features

All V-Colo packages come with these features:
  • Your choice of OS
  • Easy-to-use Web Control Panel for controlling your virtual hardware, modifying your account info, paying bills, etc.
  • One IP address
  • Storage on triply mirrored all-SSD storage servers, so:
    • single-disk failures won't kill your server
    • total server hardware failure can be recovered from in minutes, not hours
    • storage can be increased or decreased flexibly, unconstrained by the limits of physical media
  • Free nightly backup of your storage (details in this FAQ)
  • DNS for up to 100 domains
  • Reboot your server over the Internet using our console server
  • Console access via ssh to our console server
  • Plenty of expandability, with our Add-Ons

If you need help choosing a plan or deciding what features you need, please feel free to ask. And if you're a Panix Unix shell subscriber, try one of the panix local newsgroups.
