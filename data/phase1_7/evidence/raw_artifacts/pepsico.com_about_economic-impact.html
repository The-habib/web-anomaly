PepsiCo's Impact on the US Economy

PepsiCo’s brands and operations play a vital role in driving economic activity across the United States, supporting hundreds of thousands of American jobs and contributing billions in national GDP.

Our Impact is felt nationwide

PepsiCo’s network — from farm to shelf — fuels economic activity across industries and communities. External analysis and company reporting demonstrate the impact of our operations, investments, and growth on jobs and America’s economy.

Read more on PepsiCo's 2024 Impact on the U.S. Economy

PepsiCo is Rooted in Agriculture

Our business relies on farmers. Many of PepsiCo’s beloved foods and drinks exist because of crops and ingredients grown on American farms. It’s why we’re investing in the future of American agriculture, to help strengthen farmer resilience, productivity and profitability. PepsiCo’s Positive Agriculture agenda aims to support a more resilient food system built on collaborations with farmers and trusted local farm-facing organizations, practical action, and measurable outcomes.

Supporting Farmers

PepsiCo and its network spend $4.60 billion annually on agricultural products in the US and support 14,500 jobs in the agricultural supply chain
