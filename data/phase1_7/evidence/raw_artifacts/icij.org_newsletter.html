How women across the world fell prey to scam profiles on a Russian billionaire’s dating site empire

As Canada and several U.S. states try to ban bitcoin ATMs, the major crypto players that keep them running have avoided scrutiny. Following the paper trail to Guatemala to uncover what records can’t reveal about access to Keytruda

For the Cancer Calculus, an ICIJ reporter traveled to Guatemala, where her reporting on the bestselling drug Keytruda took her into crowded cancer wards and living rooms. Introducing Cancer Calculus: An exposé into the true cost of the world’s bestselling drug

Keytruda has been hailed as a game changer in cancer treatment, but much of the world can’t afford it. Now, a yearlong ICIJ investigation sheds new light on how pharmaceutical giant Merck has fended off competitors to keep the price of Keytruda sky-high, locking out patients and squeezing health care systems worldwide.
