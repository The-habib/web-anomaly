Server Colocation

If you have a specialized server strategy, or would like to administer your own machine without the added cost of a leased-line connection, you can colocate it with Panix. Colocated servers are plugged directly into an uninterruptible power supply (UPS) in our existing cage at a major NYC facility, and are provided with remote power switch control and remote serial console access.

The base price includes:
  • One gigabit Ethernet port on our switch
  • One 120V outlet, controllable by you over the Internet
  • One serial port on our console server for serial console access to your server
  • Up to 16 IP addresses
  • Full control of your server (we won't touch it unless you ask us to), and onsite maintenance visits by appointment
  • 25 Tbit trasfers per month
  • One No-Dial shell account

Details:
  • Your server should be in a 19" rackmount case, or a Mac Mini. If you need to install other hardware, please contact us before ordering to ensure that we can accommodate you.
  • If you would like serial console access on a Mac Mini, you must add a USB<->Serial converter, and then set up console access on that serial port.
  • Onsite visits are restricted to business hours, by appointment only.
  • Prices listed are for the complete package, regardless of whether you opt to use all features included. Services and resources cannot be shared among accounts unless otherwise stated.
