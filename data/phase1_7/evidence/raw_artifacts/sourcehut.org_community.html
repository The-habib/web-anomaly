Keeping up with the community of hackers working on and with Sourcehut.

Mailing lists

The sr.ht-discuss mailing list is used for general Sourcehut-related questions and discussions, sharing your projects, and so on. The sr.ht-dev list is used for discussing the development of Sourcehut itself.

Real time chat

We use IRC for chat. You can join us on Libera.Chat with your favorite IRC client at #sr.ht. There’s not always someone online, so be prepared to send a follow-up email if necessary.
