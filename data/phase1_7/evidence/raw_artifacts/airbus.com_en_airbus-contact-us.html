Frequently asked questions

I am looking for a job or an internship or I have login problems, Adobe flash player issues

I have login problems, Adobe flash player issues

Please report your problem to the following e-mail address: erecruiting@airbus.com. Airbus handles a large volume of job openings at any time, along with many forms/applications, so please be very specific in your trouble report.

 

I am looking for a job or an internship

We have created a comprehensive “Careers” section of the website, which contains information on current Airbus employment opportunities at all levels. Please take a few minutes to review the section career.

I am a professional and I have a question related to the operation and maintenance of Airbus Commercial Aircraft.

For all questions related to the operation and maintenance of Airbus Commercial Aircraft, we ask our operators, customers and industry colleagues to use the privileged channels that are available to them through our Airbus offices worldwide.

Furthermore, Airbus has established a global network of resident customer support managers. Their role is to facilitate customers’ access to the entire range of support services available and ensure customers’ needs are met.

To find out how to contact your nearest Airbus representative, go to the technical support & services page on aircraft.airbus.com.

I am a professional and I have a question related to helicopter services.

Airbus Helicopters has developed extensive resources to support its operators and maintainers around the world. Please visit the services section of the airbus.com website where you will find links to technical support (with contact numbers and e-mail address for Airbus HCare [24-hour hotline], and a link for technical publications via the Keycopter portal).

I work at an airline, I am a pilot or a training-related professional and I have a query.

For Airbus commercial aircraft, information covering the full range of services including core services aircraft availability, flight operations and in-flight experience is available on our dedicated website: https://aircraft.airbus.com/en.

To contact the services team, use the form on this page.

Where can I find photos and videos of Airbus products?

We have a dedicated Media Centre with plenty of assets, whether you are looking for photos or videos, for wallpapers or infographics. All assets on the Media Centre are downloadable and can be used as described in the disclaimer on the site. 

Our Media Centre has both extensive search and browse options to help you find what you are looking for. 

If you are a media professional you can also use the broadcast room ( only TV-stations, professional format).

Can you send me a goodie?

Due to the large volume of requests we receive from the public, Airbus is not able to give away items. 

However, Let's shop Airbus is a great source for officially licensed Airbus materials including scale models, stickers, posters, magnets, pins, badge holders, pens, binoculars, T-shirts, caps, jackets, sweaters, umbrellas and other products. 

I would like to buy an aircraft.

Airbus does not encourage contacts on such commercial relations via its website. Should you wish to clearly identify yourself as a potential purchaser and send us an overview of your business plan to establish an airline operation, a member of the Airbus Customer Affairs team can contact you upon its review. Please send your details to asset.management@airbus.com. 

I have an idea or an invention.

As we receive a very large number of emails with new ideas, we unfortunately are unable to respond individually. However, Airbus encourages potential new suppliers – including those with ideas to propose – to review this page: Becoming an Airbus Supplier.

I am a journalist or media industry professional looking for specific contacts at Airbus.

Please contact the Airbus Media Relations team via media@airbus.com.

I am a student looking for research material or wanting to interview someone from Airbus.

While we encourage academic research, we cannot offer individual assistance to students due to the very high volume of requests. We suggest to review our website airbus.com for suitable information. The main menu gives you access to our topical sections and the search function (top right) can help you find more specific content, whether that be detailed pages, press releases or stories.

We wish you all the best with your project.

Looking for Airbus Aircraft 3D views?

3D view drawings of Airbus aircraft are available via the airport operations page on our dedicated commercial aircraft website.

These drawings are accurate to within 10 centimetres.

Where can I find more about the Airbus Ethics and Compliance Programme?

All information on our programme is available in the dedicated integrity and compliance section of this website. 

Airbus also maintains a dedicated system to receive alerts called the Airbus OpenLine. If you would like to report a concern, please visit airbusopenline.com and select "submit new alert". The Airbus OpenLine is available to employees and third parties.

What is Airbus’ commitment to human rights?

Airbus seeks to ensure respect for human rights throughout its business, operations and supply chain. Information on our commitment to international human rights standards & principles, our Supplier Code of Conduct and our Human Rights Policy Statement by Airbus CEO Guillaume Faury is available on the human rights page.

Vulnerability disclosure guidelines

Safety and security of products, services or assets made by or belonging to Airbus are of utmost importance in our industry. We welcome any reports of potential vulnerabilities related to our products, services or assets that you may submit in good faith and in accordance with these guidelines. 

Important: If you are a customer, supplier or contractor of Airbus products or services, contact your Airbus business point directly rather than using the below process. 

Safety first

Airbus is strongly committed to safety and security and therefore urges you to not do anything that could harm or damage yourself or others. 

How to report a vulnerability to Airbus

Send your English language report by encrypted email (please use the Airbus PGP public key) to vuln@airbus.com as soon as possible after the discovery of the potential vulnerability together with the following information: 

  • Description of the vulnerability;
  • Details to reproduce;
  • Discovery timeline;
  • Related product, service or asset;
  • Your contact details including your PGP key.

We will acknowledge receipt of your report in a timely manner, usually within 72 hours. If you do not receive any acknowledgement receipt from Airbus within this timeframe, please resubmit your report to ensure we have received it. 

What about trust? 

We may need time to assess and fix any vulnerability. We rely on you to provide us with reasonable time before sharing or publishing any potential vulnerabilities to the public or third parties. Please keep in mind that any public disclosure or sharing of information concerning any unresolved vulnerability may cause harm and expose your responsibility. 

We respect the interests of the reporting party and anonymous reports are welcome. 

Note: Airbus does not operate a bug bounty program. We do, however, recognise reporting parties who have brought an acknowledged security vulnerability to our attention. Airbus is working to set up a public Hall of Fame. Only reporting parties who express a wish and authorisation to be named will be listed in the Hall of Fame. 

Be ethical and compliant

The purpose of these guidelines is to ease the disclosure of potential vulnerabilities in an ethical way and in accordance with the law. They shall not be construed as a permission to infringe any law or to reverse engineer any code or other technology. 

Disclosure of any vulnerability will comply with the following principles: 

  • Do not cause any harm to Airbus, our customers, suppliers, partners or any other individuals or companies;
  • Do not act so as to compromise safety of our products, their operation and/or related services;
  • Do not infringe any applicable intellectual property rights or trade secrets, laws or regulations;
  • Do not lock, disclose, destroy or compromise integrity of Airbus’ and Airbus’ customers and partners’ data;
  • Do not turn a financial transaction into a precondition to the disclosure of potential vulnerability;
  • Do not breach any applicable data privacy laws and regulations.
