The sight of my life

~nsequeira119

He was the sight of my life

I could die now- happy

He was magnificent - I'd

Never seen anything like him

And likely never would again

He had an enchanting posture

Handsome black pants

Black and white suit all over

But not in a regular suit way

It was variegated- like striped in places

Even iridescent, and flamboyant in others

Especially near the lower back

And there was bright red ornamentation

High up, that decorated his already beautiful appearance

He loved the ladies, and they loved him

I was lucky to get to glance him

I'd walked many miles to do it

He was splendiferous

One of a kind, most certainly

Then sunset came, and suddenly…

He stood up straight, puffed up his chest, and…

R-R-R-Rrrrrrrrr!

And in 5 minutes, he was perched up with his ladies

Where he was no longer visible

But I was satisfied.

I'd gotten to see him.

On my way there, I'd gotten stung by a bee

And it hurt so bad I screamed

But once I'd gotten to see him,

It seemed no matter

No matter at all

I'd gotten to see the sight of my life
