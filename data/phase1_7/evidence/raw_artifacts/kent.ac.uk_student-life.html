Location, location, location

Canterbury

Beautiful, unique, peaceful, green. Canterbury campus. Built on 300 acres of parkland, with views over the historic city of Canterbury, it's an idyllic place to study.

Take the tour

Explore the University of Kent Canterbury campus with students Harry and MJ as they take you on a tour of their favourite spots.

Medway

Diverse, friendly, engaging. You're surrounded by historic buildings, renovated for 21st-century study. With restaurants, cafes and our student Hub, there's space to relax.

Take the tour

Explore the University of Kent Medway campus with students Amarachi and DJ. Hear from them what they like about living in Medway and all the opportunities available on campus.
