    Welcome to Research, Economic Development and Innovation (REDI)!  

    JMU research contributes to our understanding of the world, drives innovation, develops solutions to global challenges, promotes collaborations, shapes our educational approaches and provides student learning opportunities in and beyond the classroom. As JMU’s central research division, REDI supports faculty-scholars, students and staff engaged in a rich diversity of creative and intellectual endeavors across and between disciplines. Follow us on social media to discover our inclusive approach to supporting and promoting inquiry.
