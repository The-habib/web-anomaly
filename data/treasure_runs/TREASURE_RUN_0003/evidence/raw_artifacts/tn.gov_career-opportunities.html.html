The State of Tennessee is the largest employer in the state, and there is no better place to serve. TeamTN works together to maintain infrastructure, protect natural resources, and develop thriving communities.  Thank you for your interest to join us in the meaningful work to build a better Tennessee.

Featured Departments
Tennessee Department of Health
Tennessee Department of Education
Tennessee Department of Agriculture

IMPACT

The services our employees provide are essential to the people of Tennessee.
In public service, you will spend every day doing work that is vital to your state and your community. There is no better place to serve than on TeamTN.

CULTURE

Our culture is founded in employee ownership. Together, we are one team committed to building a better Tennessee. TeamTN is dedicated to continuous improvement in providing better service and results for the citizens of Tennessee.

INNOVATION

We are building state government for the future. Our innovative employment practices include merit-based compensation, mobile work models, and modern workspaces. Not to mention our commitment to implementing new ideas to drive better
results.

EXPERIENCE

The State of Tennessee offers a wide variety of careers, ranging from accounting to forestry. More than 43,000 employees are part of TeamTN. With so many opportunities to enjoy diverse work, people, and experiences, you will never be short on trying something
new.
