Fifty one years ago, a fleet of miners arrived at Kepler 22. They were young and
hopeful, sold on the promise of independence from the Fed.

Fifty years ago, those same miners - and hundreds more that had arrived since -
began building Kepler Delta, our home. Sixty kilometers in diameter, spinning
every ten minutes.

Forty eight years ago, Kepler Delta was finished. And the very same year, the
Fed swooped in and installed their own government.

Since then, we have been fighting for our home. The Fed is corrupt; rotten to
the core. They'd have you believe Delta is a paradise: gleaming metal curves as
far as the eye can see, clean air, freedom.

In reality, our home is falling apart. The corps take the food out of your
mouths, and the Fed skims whatever's left. You work for hours, and your reward
is barely enough money to scrape by.

In this moment, you have a choice: you can lay down and let the corps and the
Fed walk all over you, or you can stand and fight.

Taking back paradise.
