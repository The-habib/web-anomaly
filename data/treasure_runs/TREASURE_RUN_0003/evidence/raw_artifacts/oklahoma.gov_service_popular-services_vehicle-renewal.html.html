Vehicle Renewal

In the State of Oklahoma, each year that you own a registered vehicle you must renew your registration, even if the vehicle is not in operation. You can choose to renew your vehicle online or in person.

 

If you have not yet registered your vehicle, visit our registration page.

Commercial Fleet Registration

Starting November 1st, 2024, owners of 10 or more commercially registered vehicles will have the opportunity to establish a commercial fleet, consolidating all their vehicles into one unit. Please note that all fleets will expire in December, so a prorated registration fee may be required to adjust the expiration date. This initiative will streamline the management and renewal of your fleet vehicles. You can complete this process through your local licensed operator or directly on Navigate.

New Iconic Oklahoma Plate is Here!

Starting Sept. 1, 2024, Oklahoma’s standard issue license plate will have a new design. Unlike previous plate redesigns, Oklahomans will not automatically be reissued the newly designed plate. Anyone who would like the new Iconic Oklahoma Plate can pay a $4 plate replacement fee with their annual vehicle registration renewal or $9 outside of their annual renewal at a local licensed operator.
