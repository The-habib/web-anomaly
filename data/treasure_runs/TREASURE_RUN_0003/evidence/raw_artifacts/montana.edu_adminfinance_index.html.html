Administration & Finance



Mission Statement

To manage, deliver, and enhance the administrative and financial services that enable the University to achieve its mission.

About Us

The dedicated employees of the Administration & Finance Division provide support services for Montana State University, through leadership, management and stewardship of fiscal, human, physical and information technology resources to enable the University community to focus on its mission. We deliver quality, cost-effective services in a positive, professional, ethical and timely manner. 

Please take a moment to tour our web site. Here you will learn about the wide array of services we deliver to our students, faculty and staff. Check out our departments and meet our employees - we're dedicated to making MSU better than ever! Go CATS!
