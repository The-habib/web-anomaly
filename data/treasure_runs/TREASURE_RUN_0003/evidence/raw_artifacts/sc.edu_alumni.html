Alumni Communications

Stay connected with the latest stories, podcasts and features about the University of South Carolina.

  • Carolinian Magazine

    Get the inside story on all that's happening in Carolinian, the university's official alumni magazine published three times a year by the USC Alumni Association.

  • Inside Carolina

    Subscribe to Inside Carolina — our monthly e-newsletter highlighting the people that make Carolina so special — and catch this month's edition of our Inside Carolina video series.

  • Remembering the Days Podcast

    Explore the ever interesting and sometimes quirky history of the state's flagship university through episodes of our Remembering the Days podcast. 

Career Development

Whether you are looking for a new job or changing careers, our partnership with the USC Career Center provides an edge in the job market. Sharpen your resume, practice your interview skills or attend our industry-specific career fairs.

Gamecocks Network

There is something for every alumnus, alumna and friend to stay connected to USC. Join an alumni club near you, mentor a current USC student, explore the globe with other Gamecocks, advocate for higher education and more.

 

  • Affinity Groups

    Join our Black Alumni Council, Veterans Alumni Council, LGBTQ+ Council, Young Alumni Council or Alumni Interest Group for Carolina Band.

  • Local Clubs

    From Atlanta to Los Angeles, you'll find hundreds of volunteers leading fun and meaningful activities in over 45 cities around the world.

  • Travel Programs 

    Explore the world with fellow Gamecocks on unique excursions with all-inclusive iteneraries designed for the intellectually curious traveler.

 

 

University News and Stories

 

  • USC to celebrate 225 anniversary

    Founded just 25 years after the signing of the Declaration, USC will celebrate its 225th anniversary throughout the 2026-27 academic year — beginning today (Aug. 18) with the start of the Fall 2026 semester.

  • USC named best-in-state employer for 2026

    The University of South Carolina has been named one of America’s Best-in-State Employers for South Carolina for 2026.

  • USC expands faculty pipeline to strengthen South Carolina's nursing workforce

    A successful USC program that prepares future nursing faculty is now a statewide partnership to help address one of South Carolina's biggest healthcare workforce challenges. Expanding Nursing Faculty Pipeline is a College of Nursing initiative to prepare the next generation of nurse educators.

  • Social media interest in sororities fuels record-breaking recruitment class

    The popularity of sorority life — including the recruitment process — on social media has sparked a surge of interest in Greek life at the University of South Carolina, which had the largest Potential New Member class in the nation this fall.
