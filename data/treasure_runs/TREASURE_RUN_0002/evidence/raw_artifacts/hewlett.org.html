Helping people, communities & the planet flourish

The Hewlett Foundation invests in creative thinkers and problem solvers with different perspectives, all working to build an inclusive society where everyone has a meaningful opportunity to thrive.

Our Priorities

We make grants across a number of program areas, many of which we have supported for decades, as well as limited-time initiatives focused on specific problems.

Education

Provide students with rigorous, relevant, and innovative educational opportunities in order to foster mobility and help them contribute to the future of their communities and our country.

Environment

Address climate change globally and conserve the U.S. West to ensure people, communities, and the planet flourish.

Performing Arts

Support meaningful artistic experiences for communities throughout the San Francisco Bay Area.

Gender Equity and Governance

Promote gender equity and responsive governance in East and West Africa, Mexico, and the U.S. to foster inclusive societies where everyone can thrive.

U.S. Democracy

Strengthen our shared national community through nonpartisan support for our governing institutions to build public trust in a durable democracy where all Americans have a meaningful opportunity to thrive.

Effective Philanthropy

Strengthen the capacity of Hewlett grantees, and nonprofits and philanthropy in general, to benefit people, communities, and the planet.

Economy and Society

Identify and help spread ideas from across the ideological spectrum that will inform what comes next for capitalism in the U.S. and in democratic societies around the world.

Racial Justice

Promote equal opportunity for all by addressing systemic inequalities across issue areas and in society.

Special Projects

Respond to opportunities that advance our philanthropic values, incubate new ideas, strengthen our field, and support the Bay Area.
