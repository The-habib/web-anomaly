  • TRANSFER STUDENTS

We are here to help you complete your undergraduate degree and serve as a launchpad to your next endeavor whether that is getting your dream job, heading to graduate school, or anything else you can imagine!

From the options below, tell us how we can help you and we’ll set up a personalized appointment with you to accomplish your goals.

Please note that Transfer Center Appointments are offered in February, March, April, and May for fall enrollment and September, October, and November for spring enrollment. In addition, Virtual Transfer Days are offered in May, June, July, and August for fall enrollment, and in November, December, and January for spring enrollment.

Nothing here seem to fit your needs? Contact our transfer team at transferadmission@hofstra.edu or call us at 516-463-6700 and someone will steer you in the right direction!

This is all new to me!

‌ I don’t know where to start!

  • Schedule a Transfer Center Appointment (Approximate time commitment: 1 hour.)

‌ I want to complete my application and need help getting started.

  • Watch our How to Fill Out a Transfer Application instructional video. (Approximate time commitment: 30 minutes.)
  • Fill out your Hofstra Transfer Application as you watch the video listed above!

‌ I want to apply and register for classes in one day. (I like to check things off my list!)

  • Register for one of our Virtual Transfer Days. (Approximate time commitment: 1.5-2 hours depending on your needs.)

‌ I just want to tour campus.

  • Sign-up for a Virtual Information Session and Tour.  (Approximate time commitment: 1 hour.)
  • Sign-up for an In-Person Information Session and Tour on our campus.  (Approximate time commitment: 1.5 hours.)

I’ve already applied!

‌ I have questions and/or need to submit additional documents to complete my application and receive my admission decision.

  • Register for one of our Virtual Transfer Days. (Approximate time commitment: 1.5-2 hours depending on your needs.)

I’ve been admitted!

‌ I want to go over my credit evaluation.

  • Register for one of our Virtual Transfer Days. (Approximate time commitment: 1.5-2 hours depending on your needs.)

‌ I need to submit my enrollment deposit and register for classes.

  • Submit your deposit at my.hofstra.edu. Once we receive your deposit, we will reach out to you and schedule an appointment with advising so you may register for your classes. (Approximate time commitment: 5 minutes.)

‌ I have questions about student life, residence halls, or academic offerings.

  • Register for one of our Virtual Transfer Days. (Approximate time commitment: 1.5-2 hours depending on your needs.)

‌ I want to register for classes.

  • RSVP for a New Student Registration Day where you will meet with an advisor and create your class schedule. (Approximate time commitment: 1 hour.)

I’d like to speak with Financial Aid.

‌ I have questions about financing my education.

  • Schedule a virtual appointment with Student Financial Services. (Approximate time commitment: 30 minutes.)
  • Register for one of our Virtual Transfer Days where you’ll have time to ask quick questions with Student Financial Services staff. (Approximate time commitment: 1.5-2 hours depending on your needs.)

I've submitted my enrollment deposit!

‌ I need to register for classes.

  • RSVP for a New Student Registration Day where you will meet with an advisor and register for classes. (Approximate time commitment: 1 hour.)

I’d like to re-enroll at Hofstra!

‌ I’d like to complete my Hofstra degree but need to know my next steps.

  • Schedule a Transfer Center Appointment. (Approximate time commitment: 1 hour.)

‌ I’d like to return to Hofstra.

  • Fill out a Hofstra Undergraduate Reactivation Application.

I just want to tour campus.

  • Sign-up for a Virtual Information Session and Tour.  (Approximate time commitment: 1 hour.)
  • Sign-up for an In-Person Information Session and Tour on our campus.  (Approximate time commitment: 1.5 hours.)

 

Nothing here seem to fit your needs?

Email us at transferadmission@hofstra.edu or call us at 516-463-6700 and we will schedule a personalized appointment for you.
