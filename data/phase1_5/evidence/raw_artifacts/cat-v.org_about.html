Cat-v.ORG

Cat-v.org hosts a series of sites dedicated to diverse subjects that share an idiosyncratic intellectual perspective, questioning orthodoxy and fomenting elitism and high standards in topics from software design to politics, passing by art and journalism and anything else interesting.

Other than total and complete world domination, the overriding goal is to encourage and stimulate critical and independent thinking.

About the server

Cat-v.org lives on a server running the 9front fork of Plan 9 from Bell Labs.

Most cat-v.org sites run the werc web content management system.

To help cat-v.org stay online, you can donate using Patreon.

Contact

Comments, suggestions, questions, rants and anything else remotely interesting are all very welcome in the official cat-v.org mailing list:

   echo subscribe | mail cat-v-owner@cat-v.org

Then send messages to cat-v@cat-v.org.

Archives are available on Plan 9 via 9p (web archives coming soon):

   9fs lists.9front.org

The irc channel #cat-v on irc.oftc.net is where much discussion, trolling and even work happens.
