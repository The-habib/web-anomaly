DIGITAL ENERGY SOLUTIONS

Transform your electrification infrastructure

Electrification X helps renewable energy operators, utilities, industries and infrastructure customers solve the challenges of the energy transition — all with one integrated suite.

Electrification X offers:

  • Asset management: Monitor equipment conditions, operation data and health status.
  • Energy management: Improve energy efficiency, reduce carbon emissions and optimize the energy mix.
  • Load management: Oversee EV charging infrastructure, enable remote control and manage loads dynamically while keeping the grid stable.
  • Microgrid management: Enable data transparency across all microgrids empowering the optimal utilization of renewable energy.
  • Distribution grid visibility: Gain oversight of distribution stations and street cabinets at medium- and low-voltage.
  • Fault detection: Monitor overhead and substation electrical networks to facilitate prompt and efficient fault management.
  • Operational technology (OT) support: Gain continuous inventory, vendor-agnostic vulnerability and baseline management, and workflows.

Why Electrification X?​

Every minute of downtime in electrification infrastructure costs an average of €3,000–€12,000. Electrification X transforms operations by minimizing unplanned outages and mean time to repair, while extending asset lifetime. Thanks to centralized monitoring and advanced diagnostics that minimize the need for field crew trips to remote sites, mean time to repair can be reduced by up to 50%.​
