                          We are creative minds, problem solvers, and innovators. We are game designers, educators, philanthropists, analysts, advocates, entrepreneurs, actors, musicians, and artists… just to name a few. Take your first step in joining us - explore our programs.

                          The arts, humanities, and social sciences enable us to explore and understand the human condition. Our disciplines are more vital than ever in addressing complex societal challenges. We seek to advance knowledge through research and creative activity to contribute to a better world. We prepare students to be inquisitive, reflective, life-long learners; to solve problems collaboratively; engage with a world rich in perspectives; and be leaders in their careers and communities.
