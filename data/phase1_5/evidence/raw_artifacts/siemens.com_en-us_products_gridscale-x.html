One platform to enable true system operations

Gridscale X unifies grid planning, operations, asset management, and metering in a single, open and interoperable platform. Replacing siloed tools and disconnected workflows, it connects long-term planning with real-time operations through a common, scalable backbone, making insights reusable, workflows consistent, and complexity lower.

The cloud-based Gridscale X platform forms the foundation of an open, evolving ecosystem and enables seamless integration of Siemens and third-party technologies as well as custom applications. It allows utilities to make faster decisions, innovate continuously, and reduce complexity and risk through shared services and harmonized data models that support true system operations.
