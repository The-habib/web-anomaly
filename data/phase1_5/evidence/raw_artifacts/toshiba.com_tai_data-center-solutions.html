Toshiba Data Center Solutions: A Unified Approach to Reliability and Performance

Toshiba Data Center Solutions

Toshiba data center solutions deliver integrated technologies that support the growth of AI, cloud computing, and high-density infrastructure. As demand accelerates, reliable power from the grid to the rack is essential.

 

These solutions include power generation, transmission, and energy storage, along with digital and storage technologies to keep power efficient, secure, and intelligent.

Stable Power Starts with a Strong Grid

The rapid growth of AI and high-density computing is driving unprecedented power demand, placing increased strain on utility networks and making capacity and grid stability critical challenges for data center operators. Toshiba data center solutions address these challenges at the grid level by combining power generation with advanced transmission and distribution technologies to strengthen power quality, improve reliability, and support high-capacity energy delivery.

As demand continues to rise, Toshiba’s grid solutions provide the electrical resilience needed to reduce outages, manage variability, and maintain uninterrupted data center performance.

Continuous Power for Critical Operations

When uptime is everything, Toshiba UPS systems deliver dependable power protection for mission-critical data centers. Designed for efficiency, scalability, and reliability, our UPS portfolio covers capacities from 15 kVA to 2,000 kVA, supporting operations from edge facilities to hyperscale environments.  

With advanced diagnostics, intelligent load management, and seamless transition during grid disturbances, Toshiba UPS systems minimize downtime and protect sensitive equipment. Whether deployed as standalone protection or integrated with SCiB™ energy storage, Toshiba’s UPS technology ensures continuous operation — even when the grid falters.

Fast-Charging, Long-Life Battery Energy Storage

Toshiba lithium-ion battery SCiB™  deliver the reliability, responsiveness, and safety modern data centers demand. Built on Toshiba’s proprietary lithium titanium oxide (LTO) chemistry, SCiB™ provides exceptional cycle life, fast charging capability, and stable performance — even in demanding operating conditions.  

Beyond complete system solutions, Toshiba also offers SCiB™ cells and modules for OEMs and battery integrators developing custom energy storage or backup applications. With superior charge/discharge efficiency, wide temperature tolerance, and an industry-leading safety record, SCiB™ technology provides the foundation for flexible, scalable energy systems — from modular backup to large-scale storage.

Smarter Power Conversion at Every Level

Toshiba’s semiconductor portfolio, including MOSFETs, IGBTs, and SiC devices, enables high-efficiency energy conversion across UPS systems, power supplies, cooling systems, and renewable integrations.

Engineered for fast switching, low conduction loss, and thermal stability, our devices help reduce total power consumption and improve system reliability. From rack-level components to grid-scale converters, Toshiba semiconductors empower designers to build smarter, more efficient power architectures for the AI era.

HDD Data Storage for Mission-Critical Workloads

As data volumes surge, storage reliability becomes mission-critical. Toshiba’s enterprise HDDs are engineered for 24/7 operation, combining high capacity, speed, and durability to meet the needs of hyperscale, enterprise, and cloud environments.

With proven innovation in drive mechanics and recording technology, Toshiba HDDs deliver class-leading reliability and performance for AI, analytics, and high-density storage applications. Whether archiving, processing, or scaling your infrastructure, Toshiba provides the dependable data foundation that keeps digital ecosystems running smoothly.
