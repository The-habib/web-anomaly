A Reputation for Academic Excellence

Baylor University stands apart as a Christian research university. Our faculty deeply value teaching and mentoring students while also pursuing research and scholarship at the highest levels. At Baylor, learning takes place in the classrooms and in the educational experiences outside the classroom that expand knowledge and help prepare students as leaders in an ever-changing global society.

Research at Baylor

Baylor is an R1 research university. This means undergraduate and graduate students work alongside Baylor researchers who are actively pursuing new knowledge and seeking solutions to our world’s most meaningful challenges. In the classroom, their research enhances teaching through engaging insights that bring texts, theories and ideas to life in ways that encourage problem solving, broaden students' understanding and ignite new interests. 

Learn More

Global Education

Preparing students for success in our global society means providing academic and cross-cultural opportunities that expand students’ experiences and knowledge. From on-campus events and programs to studying abroad and discipline-specific travel, students develop globally essential skills that prepare them for leadership and service. Baylor also is home to a diverse group of international students whose cultures, beliefs and perspectives are invaluable to our campus community.

Graduate Education

Graduate School

The Graduate School at Baylor University serves students in over a hundred programs across the humanities, arts, and sciences with resources designed to holistically support their academic and professional development. 
Graduate Professional Education

Baylor Professional Education offers a variety of degrees, certificates and courses to help professionals improve their skills, transition to a new career or become an industry expert. 
All Graduate Degrees & Programs

Baylor Graduate School is home to more than 4,300 students across over 100 academic and professional fields. 

Libraries

Baylor’s vast library resources and intentional collaboration and study spaces support scholarship, foster teaching and learning and build community. Amongst a network of libraries and associated institutes and centers Moody and Jones Libraries serve as the central hub of activity for students and offers a range of academic and research support.
