Parent and Family Programs

Welcome to our Bearcat family!

We believe establishing a relationship with parents and family members ensures a successful Binghamton University experience for students. We are here to offer support and guidance so we can work together to encourage your student to advocate for themselves — on campus and in life. Our hope is that you will choose to stay involved, interact with other parents and contribute to the educational experience of your student.
