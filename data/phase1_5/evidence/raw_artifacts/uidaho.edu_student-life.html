    Your campus, your community, your adventure

    There are countless ways for you to connect, lead and engage at U of I. Here you’ll find a welcoming and friendly environment where you’re encouraged to pursue your passions, challenge yourself and define a future that’s uniquely yours. Live on campus, join one of 250+ student organizations, dive into events or volunteer in the community. However you choose to get involved, you’ll make lasting memories and friendships.

    Find your campus home

    Our residential campus offers a variety of housing options, from welcoming residence halls to fraternity and sorority chapter facilities.

    Where you live shapes your college experience. Living on campus keeps you close to classes, dining and campus events, making it easy to connect, get involved and build a routine that works for you. 

    Housing and residence life

    Our residence halls and living communities are designed to help you succeed academically, socially and personally.

    Explore housing options

    Fraternity and sorority life

    Find your chapter, build lifelong friendships and become part of a tradition rooted in leadership, service and academic excellence.

    Explore greek life

    Dine on campus

    Enjoy convenient dining options across campus. First-year students living in the residence halls have a residential meal plan with access to our all-you-care-to-eat dining hall and popular on-campus spots. Grab a quick bite between classes or refuel after a busy day.

    Check out Idaho EatsCheck out Idaho Eats

    Get involved

    With hundreds of clubs and organizations to choose from, it’s easy to find your community and build new skills. There are also plenty of leadership and volunteer opportunities that help you make an impact and grow along the way. However you choose to get involved, you’ll find your place—and your people—at U of I.

    Connect, lead and make an impact

    Join a community

    U of I is home to a wide range of campus groups where you can connect with others who share your background, passions and academic goals. Whether you’re part of the Honors Program, ROTC Program, Tribal Nations Student Affairs or another organization, you’ll find connection here.

    Explore UIdaho campus communities

    Stay active and healthy

    Take advantage of a wide range of programs and activities designed to support your wellbeing at U of I. Join intramural sports, explore outdoor adventures, take a fitness class or spend time at the Student Recreation Center’s open gym. You can also show your Vandal pride and be part of the energy of Vandal Athletics.

    See student rec opportunities

    Dive into research

    Research is part of campus life. Work alongside faculty, contribute to active projects and apply what you’re learning beyond the classroom. You might work in a lab, gather data in the field or present findings at a conference, all while building practical skills and adding to your college experience. No matter your major, you can get involved in research.

    Discover research at U of I

    See what’s happening

    Looking for something to do? Campus is full of opportunities, from concerts and athletic events to research expos and networking nights.
