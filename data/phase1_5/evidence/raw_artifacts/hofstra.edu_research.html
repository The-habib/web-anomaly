At Hofstra, an R2 Carnegie Institution, research drives innovation and discovery across disciplines. Faculty and students collaborate on impactful projects that address today’s challenges, from health care to law, business, and the arts.  Through hands-on learning and access to cutting-edge resources, Hofstra fosters a culture of inquiry and creativity. Whether in laboratories, studios, or communities, research at Hofstra empowers scholars to question, explore, and contribute meaningful solutions that shape a better future.

R2 Research Classification

Hofstra University has earned the distinction of a High Research and Doctorate Production University (R2) from the Carnegie Classification of Institutions of Higher Education, marking a significant milestone in the university’s academic and research trajectory. This recognition highlights Hofstra’s high-caliber faculty, whose efforts have led to increased research expenditure, the expansion of doctoral programs, and a commitment to advancing knowledge that improves society.
