Coziness + Convenience

Ole Miss is a special place. Just ask any student or alum. You'll spend some of the most transformative years of your life on our campus, making lifelong memories along the way.

We want to make your time here as easy as it is exciting. That's why we offer all the services you'll need. You'll have support from our caring staff, who will help you whenever you need it. And with plenty of chances to get involved and have fun, you'll never be bored.

The best part? It's all right here on the Ole Miss campus, so you'll never have to go very far.

Student Organizations

Joining a student organization can be a fun and exciting experience for students. Clubs at Ole Miss allow students to explore their interests, develop new skills, and make new friends.

The ForUM is a centralized platform where students can browse and sign up for various clubs and organizations on campus. Once a student has found a club of interest, they can attend meetings and events, and engage with other members. With so many clubs to choose from, students are sure to find one that suits their interests and schedule.

We offer a variety of academic and professional organizations, including pre-professional societies, academic honor societies, and cultural organizations. These groups provide students with opportunities to explore their interests, build their resumes, and develop skills that will serve them well after graduation.

The ForUM 

Greek Life

One of the most prominent types of student organizations at Ole Miss are fraternities and sororities, which have a rich history and continue to be a significant part of campus life. There are over 20 fraternities and sororities on campus, which provide students with the opportunity to develop leadership skills, make lifelong connections, and engage in community service.

Explore Greek Life

Community Service

Community service is a cornerstone of Ole Miss student life. Many student organizations focus on giving back to the community, with opportunities ranging from volunteering at local charities to participating in service trips abroad. The university has a dedicated Office of Community Engagement, which works to connect students with meaningful service opportunities both on and off campus.
