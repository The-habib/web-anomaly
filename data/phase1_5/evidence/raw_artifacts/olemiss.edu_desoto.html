Graduate from Ole Miss.

Shaped by Southaven,

ready for anywhere.

Turn your two-year degree into a four-year degree or earn your master's—all while staying close to the community you love.

Ole Miss-DeSoto partners with Northwest Community College to help you finish what you started.

With Ole Miss classes & advising offered here in Southaven, get the same quality education as students on our Oxford campus—with the support of our tight-knit community.

Student Stories

Johanna Herrell-Carthen

Social Work

As a first-generation college student, Johanna's bachelor's will mark a significant milestone in her path to make a difference. Drawn to a campus near home in Memphis, Johanna found the perfect balance of accessibility and affordability while maximizing scholarships, including several transfer scholarships. “My education is laying the groundwork for me to advocate for others.”

Alexandra James

Social Work

After 20 years in the restaurant industry, Alexandra returned to education in her mid-40's, rising from community college student to Chancellor’s Honor Roll senior. The close-knit campus community, where both she and her son work, provides unwavering support for her educational journey. “Everyone always has time for you. No matter if it is faculty or staff, they are never too busy to help.”

CJ Johnson

Management Information Systems

Drawn from Olive Branch by the scholarship opportunities, CJ discovered that quality education doesn't require overwhelming debt. Ole Miss-DeSoto's commitment accessibility through generous financial aid opportunities has made CJ’s academic dreams achievable —without financial burden. “They offer many great scholarships to help students out as much as they can. All you have to do is apply.”

Ainsley Cathey

Business Administration

Through the 2+2 program, Ainsley seamlessly transitioned from NWCC, balancing part-time work, community involvement, and real-world experience through her role with the Hernando Main Street Chamber of Commerce. “Choosing Ole Miss-DeSoto has not only been a smart financial and academic decision, but it’s also opened doors for real-world experience that’s shaping my career.
