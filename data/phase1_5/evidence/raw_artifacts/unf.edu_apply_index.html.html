Apply to UNF

We're excited you've chosen to apply to the University of North Florida!

Begin Your Application Here!

Students can apply via the UNF application (all students) or Common App (freshmen only). Please only submit one application. The UNF freshman Summer and Fall 2027 application is now open. Common App opens on August 1.

Early Action: Students who submit their application and all required materials by September 2 will receive an admission decision by September 16. See additional benefits of applying early so you can soar into senior year!
