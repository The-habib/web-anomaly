July Bug Accelerator 2026

July 31, 2026, 10:40 p.m.

With July being the final month for the 1st round of the 2026 Bug Accelerator Program, we focused on rapidly landing quality of life fixes and clearing the way for the next round of BAP work in the next 3 months. With this round of changes and fixes, Inkscape 1.5 is now in a much stronger position, with over 60% completion of the current milestones.

Mike and Sean worked through a wide range of regressions and usability problems, including both issues new to 1.5, and old issues that have existed in Inkscape for years. Some of the new changes include document tabs scrolling, text and font fixes, grid fixes (a much improved UX), startup screen improvements, various shortcut fixes, improved SVG font handling, extension management fixes, and so much more.

Frankly, with so many changes, it's hard to fully quantify just how much work has been landed this month (and really, this cycle). Many thanks go to the BAP devs and the Inkscape veterans who've been providing guidance, assistance and also fixing issues alongside the BAP devs.

We still have more to go, so testing, feedback, and even suggestions are much appreciated.
