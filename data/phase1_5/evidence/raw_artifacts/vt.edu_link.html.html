                The front door to partnership
                at Virginia Tech

                We connect dynamic organizations with world-class talent and expertise. A top-tier research university with global distinction, Virginia Tech is home to more than 4,000 faculty driving innovation across engineering, health, technology, and the sciences. Our sponsored research expenditures have grown 40 percent over the past three years, offering companies a ready-made platform for breakthroughs, collaboration, and results.

                About us

                Housed under the Office of Research and Innovation and Virginia Tech Advancement, Innovation and Partnerships connects organizations with top talent, groundbreaking research, and the resources to grow. We’re portfolio managers, creatives, experience makers, technologists, workshoppers, problem solvers, and doers - working to unlock potential through alumni, founders, foundation, and corporate partnerships. Bring us your bold ideas, and together we’ll shape solutions that change the world.
