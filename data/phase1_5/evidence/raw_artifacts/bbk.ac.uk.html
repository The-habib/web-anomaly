Top reasons to choose Birkbeck

Birkbeck is a member of the prestigious University of London and a leading research university. Find out more about the ways Birkbeck could change your life.

Funding

Birkbeck is committed to doing all we can to help you to finance your studies.

London campus

Our campus is based in the heart of central London, close to several major transport hubs, lots of green spaces and the best galleries, shops and restaurants the capital has to offer.

Bengaluru campus

Students in India will be able to study for a globally-recognised University of London degree without relocating overseas with the opening of the Birkbeck, University of London campus in Bengaluru.
