Apply through Clearing

When you've received your results, contact us to apply and start planning your next step.

Whether you've achieved the grades you expected or are exploring new opportunities, we'll help you understand your options and move forward with confidence. 

The quickest way to apply is to call our Clearing hotline on +44 (0)300 555 5030. You’ll speak to our team directly and get the guidance you need to secure your offer.

Prefer us to call you? You can also request a call back or apply online.

Why Choose the University of Salford

Your future is shaped by experiences, and when you join the University of Salford, we'll give you opportunities to try things out for real.

We work closely with partners across industry to ensure our teaching reflects the world you'll step into. That could mean studying abroad, building your portfolio on a placement, or developing your skills through an internship.

Whatever path you take, you'll graduate with valuable experience and a strong foundation for your future career.

Who can apply through Clearing?

Clearing is available to a wide range of applicants. You can apply through Clearing if any of the following apply: 

  • You're making your first application after 30 June 2026 
  • You didn't receive any offers (or none you wanted to accept) 
  • You don't meet the conditions of your offers 
  • You've changed your mind about what or where you want to study

Join the Early Access Club

The Early Access Club makes applying to Salford through Clearing simple. No jargon, no stress – just the right information, advice and support at the right time. 

When you join, we'll send you a dedicated phone number for priority access to our Clearing team, plus regular emails with guidance and advice. We'll help you find the right course and make sure you're ready when it's time to make the call.

Life as a Salford Student

Life at university is about finding your place, not just choosing a course. At Salford, you’ll have the chance to make new friends, explore your interests and build a student experience that works for you. 

Whether you live on campus or commute from home, you’ll be well connected to everything Salford and Manchester have to offer. From clubs, societies and events to culture, music, sport and green spaces, there are plenty of ways to make the most of your time here.

Visit us
