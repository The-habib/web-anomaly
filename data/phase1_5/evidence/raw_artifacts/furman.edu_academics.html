Academics, Personalized: Mentoring, Flexibility, and Exploration

Guided by Mentors. Defined by You.

A place where you belong and the tools to support you as you grow. With individual mentoring, small classes, and the freedom to shape your studies, Furman gives you the resources you need to explore deeply and chart your own career course.

  • 1:1 advising and mentoring
  • Ability to create your own major
  • Faculty-led research projects, as early as your first year
  • Small class sizes (avg. 14 students)

A Community That Learns Together

Where Small Classes Create Big Thinkers

Living and learning alongside your peers, you’ll take part in group projects, join (or lead!) student-led organizations, and discover what it means to be part of something bigger—inside and outside the classroom.

  • Unique residential campus experience
  • 185+ clubs and organizations
  • Classrooms that feel like communities
  • Group-based learning, collaborative projects
  • Vibrant traditions, student leadership, service-learning integration

Resources for Academic Success

You don’t have to figure it all out alone. From faculty office hours to tutoring, success coaching, and accessibility services, Furman offers the support you need to stay challenged—and supported.
