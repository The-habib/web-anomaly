Features

Secure data across environments

Built-in tools like tagging, prebuilt policies, easy-to-use workflows and long-term data retention help you meet standards such as PCI DSS, GDPR and CPRA quickly.

Easily track data access, user activity and policy changes so you can get actionable alerts to focus on what matters and remediate quickly.

Manage data security across all your hybrid cloud and SaaS applications in one place. Get clear insights, act faster and cut down on risks.

Use orchestration that provides elastic scalability and flexible deployment throughout the edge gateway. Adapt security as your data and infrastructure grow, while keeping costs down.

Deploy IBM Guardium Data Protection in under 60 minutes — no Guardium expertise needed. Automated discovery of AWS data sources (RDS, Aurora, DynamoDB, Redshift), out-of-the-box PCI, SOX, HIPAA, and GDPR reports. AWS EDP eligible.
