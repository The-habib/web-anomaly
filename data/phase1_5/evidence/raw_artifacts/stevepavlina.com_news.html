CGC Year 9 Is Now Closed – Next Opening April 2026

May 2, 2025

Our one-week annual window to join Conscious Growth Club for our 9th powerful year together is now closed, so if you haven’t joined us at this point, you’ll need to wait till April 2026 for our next opening because we are sailing on without you. You’re welcome to circle back for Year 10’s opening if you’re ready for CGC by then. That opening will most likely be April 25th through May 1st, 2026.

You can’t join us mid-year. People always ask about this, and it doesn’t happen. We never let anyone in partway through the year. We all start together and progress through the year together.

The club is really taking a big leap forward this year with a higher standard, including only on-camera, fully present Zoom calls (no passive off-cam attendance anymore). The invite was largely crafted to repel mismatches because CGC definitely isn’t for everyone. It’s really meant only for the core inner circle of this community – those who are truly the most dedicated to self-development and really want to let go of limits and step into a much fuller expression of their true selves. It’s certainly not for the timid.

Most of our members for Year 9 are renewals, so we’re really anchoring a lot of the club’s wisdom for the long run. It’s important that we keep pace with our core members and keep the club stimulating and challenging for them.

I’m really pleased with this transition to Year 9. I love how it feels to stand in this energy field with such devoted people who show up and do the work. It’s super rewarding seeing them grow and evolve year by year inside the club.

Join Round 3 of the Bucket List Challenge – Reach for the Stars

Feb 17, 2025

Round 3 of our Bucket List Challenge begins this Tuesday, February 18, 2025. This is our third and final round of the challenge. I’m calling this round “Reach for the Stars” because it’s intended to be the stretchiest round of the three.

The core purpose of the Bucket List Challenge is to help you select and accomplish meaningful personal goals that you might otherwise never get around to doing. Each round invites you to select a fresh goal and advance it to inevitability.

The first two rounds happened in 2024, so those are over and done with, but you can still get access to the recordings and go through them at your leisure by signing up for Round 3 now. You may be late to the party, but you can still join us for this third awesome round. You don’t need to have done the first two rounds to join us for Round 3.

Our Round 3 Zoom calls are February 18, 20, 25, and March 4, always starting at 11:11 AM Pacific Time. Each call goes for about 90 minutes. You’ll get the recordings of these calls too, with each call being posted to the Bucket List Challenge portal the same day each live call happens.

Round 1 provided a solid framework for probing, choosing, committing to, and integrating new experiences into your life on a practical level.

Round 2 focused on divergent explorations and variety, including opening up your vibrational range and understanding the relationship between releasing the old and inviting the new.

Round 3 will focus on setting stretchier goals with more depth, purpose, and personal meaning to them. This is your chance to pick a deeply rewarding goal to pursue and accomplish, as you gradually advance from idea to inevitability during the next two weeks.

You could approach Round 3 with the intention to aim for something big that would take courage and fortitude to accomplish. But that’s not the only way to stretch yourself. A core theme that we’ll cover on this Tuesday’s call is intelligence. What’s the most intelligent choice you could make for your Round 3 experience? This includes choosing a goal that’s truth-aligned, love-aligned, and power-aligned, so you can invite a transformational experience that truly enriches your life.

Do you want the memory of having done this challenge with us, so you can add a rich and juicy new experience to your life that you otherwise might have missed?

I’ve had some great new experiences as a result of doing this challenge with our members, and this energy of fresh adventures is still unfolding week by week. Just yesterday I attended my first-ever cacao ceremony, which was a lovely experience to share with people. I designed and offered this Bucket List Challenge to help people in this community get into a continuous flow of meaningful new experiences, so you never have to be stuck in a rut of sameness. Remember that you learn and grow primarily by embracing the new, not by repeating the old.

I’m really looking forward to sharing this final round with you. Sign up for the Bucket List Challenge today to join the rest of the community inside, and I’ll see you soon! ❤️
