Overview

Unify your operations

IBM Maximo® Application Suite is a unified asset and facilities management solution that brings maintenance, inspections and reliability together in one place for critical equipment and infrastructure, using AI to help improve asset uptime, performance and value.

Built for operations, maintenance and facility teams, it helps address downtime, aging assets, workforce constraints and fragmented data.

Features

One solution for all

  1. AI in asset lifecycle management
  2. Enterprise asset management (EAM)
  3. Asset performance management (APM)
  4. Asset investment planning (AIP)

AI for the work that keeps the world running

Maximo delivers embedded AI across maintenance, field execution, reliability and planning to provide the right insights, recommendations and actions when and where they're needed. By supporting better decisions and automating work, teams can improve reliability, reduce downtime and operate more efficiently.

Explore AI for asset management

Streamline operations with connected asset management

Maximo helps build a stronger foundation for reliable operations with enterprise asset management. Teams can manage work, asset history and maintenance processes in one place, with AI to surface insights, support prioritization and reduce time spent navigating disconnected systems. The result is more consistent execution, faster decisions and greater efficiency across daily operations.

Explore enterprise asset management

Boost reliability with asset performance insights

Improve reliability with Maximo asset performance management, which uses AI, analytics and sensor data to detect issues early, support condition-based maintenance and help prevent failures. By turning asset condition insights into action, teams can reduce unplanned downtime, improve availability and extend asset life across operations.

Explore asset performance management

Plan asset investments with more confidence

Make better decisions on when to maintain, refurbish or replace assets. Maximo asset investment planning helps connect asset condition, risk and financial impact so teams can evaluate scenarios, compare priorities and align investments to business goals, budgets and operational needs. This supports more effective capital planning and better lifecycle outcomes.

MAS is purchased using a simplified licensing and usage model leveraging a credit-based system called AppPoints. This provides the flexibility to easily add functionality and users, scale your business and pay only for what you use with a single entitlement.
