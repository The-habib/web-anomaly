what we doLeading the Future of
Clean Energy Solutions

Toshiba America Energy Systems Corporation provides energy industry solutions – new equipment, retrofits, maintenance, outage planning and more – for current and future power generation needs in the Americas and beyond, including thermal, hydro, nuclear and renewable power plants.

 

Toshiba’s energy division was formed in 2015 through the integration of several legacy Toshiba businesses and is headquartered in West Allis, Wisconsin near Milwaukee. This location includes a major manufacturing and service facility. 

 

We take pride in being a technological leader, providing cost-effective, timely and sustainable solutions that address our customers current and future power generation needs.

what we doOur Legacy
of Expertise

Our history in the North American steam turbine/generator marketplace is a testament to the quality and reliability of our equipment, making Toshiba the supplier of choice for utilities building large combined-cycle, thermal, hydro or nuclear power stations. With nearly 40 GW of installed power, we provide new equipment solutions to maximize plant performance and reliability.


We assist customers in optimizing their plant operations, as well as planning and scheduling services, and parts replacement, when the need arises. Toshiba offers one-stop solutions through localized operations and shared services.  Our extensive service offerings cover all major OEM steam turbine and generator units.
