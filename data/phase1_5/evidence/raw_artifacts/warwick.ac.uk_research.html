Research Culture at Warwick

Our research culture work aims to cultivate a vibrant environment where researchers and research-enablers can thrive.

From undergraduate researchers through to technicians and mid and senior career researchers, we believe that a culture that supports all talent and celebrates success can positively benefit research career paths and improve the quality of our research.

Find out more about our research culture projects and take a look at our Research Culture Progress Review.
