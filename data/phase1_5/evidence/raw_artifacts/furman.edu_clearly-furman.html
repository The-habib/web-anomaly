Academics
Inspire Minds Where They Meet

Furman has long stood out for its ability to blend the liberal arts and sciences’ classic emphases on critical thinking, problem-solving and communications skills with hands-on practical learning. Transforming the support possible for scholarships, teaching and innovative and interdisciplinary academic programs, CLEARLY FURMAN will lift the human and programmatic excellence that define the university’s service and reputation.

The Furman Advantage
Power our Defining Difference

Developed with deliberation and intent, The Furman Advantage is an exception in higher education. Concentrating the finest elements of the Furman experience that alumni know well, The Furman Advantage challenges and inspires each student with intensive academic expectations, caring and personal advising, guided reflection with faculty and comprehensive practical learning experiences. CLEARLY FURMAN will strengthen all aspects of The Furman Advantage so that every student can take full advantage of all it offers.

The Furman Advantage funding priorities
  • The Pathways Program
  • Engaged Learning Experiences
  • Mentoring and Advising
  • Campus Vitality and Student Experiences
  • Career Preparation
  • Malone Center for Career Engagement

Wellness, Belonging and Community
Make Personal Promise Matter

Today’s students and families increasingly expect that excellent universities will not only support intellectual growth but also develop the whole person – and prepare graduates for leadership in a diverse society. These holistic qualities of learning and community have long been intrinsic to Furman’s mission. As part of this campaign, we will take our approach to education further, investing in physical and emotional wellness, spaces that support student fitness and teamwork, and initiatives that foster diversity, inclusion and understanding.

Wellness, Belonging and Community funding priorities
  • Academic Commons
  • Belonging & Wellbeing
  • Inclusive Campus Initiatives
learn more about wellness & community
Institutes
Drive Innovations with Impact

Alumni across the decades know the rigors of study and research at Furman. Working alongside faculty in our classrooms, labs and studios, our students come to appreciate that thinking through hard problems is as important as understanding the facts. And we have a special advantage: four institutes, which no other undergraduate liberal arts and sciences institution of our size can claim. Our institutes engage students every day in professional and community decision-making. Through this campaign, we will capitalize on our unique strength to make our learning experience, and the Furman degree, even more powerful.

Institutes funding priorities
  • The Hill Institute for Innovation and Entrepreneurship
  • The Riley Institute
  • The Shi Institute for Sustainable Communities
  • Institute for the Advancement of Community Health
learn more about our institutes
Athletics
Inspiring Greatness

We have great ambitions for investing in our student-athletes because they are champions of today and tomorrow. Here, academic and athletic excellence are one. Through CLEARLY FURMAN, we will create endowments that forever advance our recruitment and support of outstanding student-athletes – and renovate and expand the spaces in which our alumni and fans can come together to cheer on our Paladins.

Athletics funding priorities
  • Basketball Facilities Enhancement

The Annual Fund
Every Day Impact

The Annual Fund is unlike any other giving opportunity, for it unifies and magnifies the gifts of individuals across the community who want to join with others to boost the work of the university, meet urgent campus needs and demonstrate the depth to which supporters stand with students as they learn and grow. Our ambition is to build this fund into a more potent resource than at any time in our history. Every donor who contributes to the Annual Fund will bring Furman a step closer to this pivotal goal and make a meaningful and daily campus difference.
