askUS

Not sure where to go? Our askUS team is here to help.

Our askUS advisers can give you the right advice when you need it most, whether you have a question about finance, wellbeing, faith or visas and immigration or if you need support with your studies, your career or a disability.

  • Call: +44 (0)161 295 5000
  • Email: askus@salford.ac.uk

Want to find more specific information? You can speak to someone on our askUS help desks across our campuses, or check our askUS topics section.

askUS opening hours

  • Monday to Thursday: 08:00-18:00
  • Friday: 08:00-17:00

  • Allerton: 08:00-17:00

  • Allerton: 08:00-17:00

There are no services at MediaCity during Trimester 3 and Vacation times. However, you can travel on the 50 bus between campuses for free, Monday to Friday, with your student ID card.

Mental Health, Wellbeing and Counselling

If you are a student, you can access free and confidential counselling through the HealthHero counselling phone line - available 24 hours a day, seven days a week: +44 (0)800 970 1080.

Wellbeing Advisers offer phone triage and face-to-face sessions throughout the week, as well as follow-up sessions where appropriate.

  • Call: +44 (0)161 295 5000
  • Fill in the online self-referral form (A Wellbeing Adviser will contact you by phone to discuss your support needs and appointment options.)

IT Support and Student Log ins

You can contact our Digital IT Service Desk team by logging a self-service incident or request ​​​​​​via our IT Service Portal (staff and student only link).​

If you require urgent support, please contact us between 08:00–17:30, Monday to Friday (except bank holidays).

  • Call: +44 (0)161 295 2444

Face to face help from the team is available at the Tech Bar on the ground floor in Clifford Whitworth Library.

It is staffed Monday to Friday, 09:00-16:00 (excluding bank holidays and the University closure period during the Winter Break).

Campus safety

Our campus security team is available for advice and assistance 24 hours a day.

We are based at Maxwell Building and can be contacted on the following phone numbers:

  • Campus Security Control Room: +44 (0)161 295 4773
  • Emergencies (for emergencies only and not IT issues): +44 (0)161 295 3333

08

Urgent Assistance

If you feel seriously at risk or are at immediate physical threat call 999 to ask for the police.

Our campus security team is available for advice and assistance 24 hours a day.

  • Campus Security Control Room: +44 (0)161 295 4773
  • Emergencies (for emergencies only and not IT issues): +44 (0)161 295 3333

If you are concerned about the immediate wellbeing and welfare for either yourself or a fellow student, then you can find the relevant support on concerns including violence, suicide and any other immediate risks to individuals.
