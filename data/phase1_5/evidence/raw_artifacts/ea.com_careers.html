Accessibility

EA has taken efforts to ensure that our on-line application system is accessible to individuals with disabilities and disabled veterans. Applicants with disabilities may contact us by sending an email to accessibility@ea.com for assistance accessing our careers.ea.com website. In your email, please include your name and preferred method of contact, and EA will respond as soon as possible.

Recruitment Scam Alert

We are aware that individuals falsely claiming to be either Electronic Arts (EA) employees or companies authorized to speak on our behalf have been contacting people with job opportunities and soliciting personal information. These communications can be through email or via third-party online platforms, including LinkedIn, WhatsApp, Facebook Marketplace, and Telegram. You should always verify the authenticity of communications and protect yourself from these scams.

There are a number of signs that a recruitment communication is a scam, including requests for money or funds (such as for visa fees, taxes, travel expenses) and requests for personal information (such as passport information, bank account information, credit card numbers, social security numbers). EA employees will never ask you for money or to provide personal financial information during our recruiting process. If an offer seems too good to be true, then it probably is.

EA employment offers will usually come from someone using an official EA email address, including one of the following domain names:

  • ea.com (including contractor.ea.com and europe.ea.com)
  • dice.se
  • bioware.com
  • respawn.com

If you believe you’ve received unauthorized or scam communications, we recommend that you do not respond. If you suspect but are still unsure whether a communication is official, please notify us by sending an email to RecruitmentFraudReports@ea.com.
