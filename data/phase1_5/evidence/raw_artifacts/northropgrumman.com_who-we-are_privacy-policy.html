We at Northrop Grumman (“we”, “us”, “our”, “Northrop Grumman”) respect your concerns about privacy. Northrop Grumman, through our independent regional subsidiaries (“subsidiaries”), provides various programs, features and services. Some of our services adopt different privacy statements or notices that supplement this statement in accordance with local or regional privacy laws. This Privacy Notice (“Notice”) is intended to provide you with the information that you need to understand how we may process your Personal Information.

This Notice is supplemented by sections which provide further information related to the processing of your information depending on the relationship or interaction you may have with us. Please be aware that not all sections will be relevant to you. The Data Controller may vary depending on how you interact with Northrop Grumman, our subsidiaries, and our services. If you are unsure which Data Controller controls your data or if you have any questions, please do not hesitate to contact us using one of the means provided in the “How to Contact Us” section below.

This Notice describes the types of Personal Information we may collect, how we use the information, with whom we share it and the choices available to individuals regarding our use of the information. We also describe the measures we take to protect the security of the information and how individuals can contact us about our privacy practices.

This Notice uses certain terms that have the meaning given to them in the California privacy laws. This notice is also applicable to California residents employed by Northrop Grumman.
