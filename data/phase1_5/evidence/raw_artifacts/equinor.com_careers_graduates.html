Why apply to our graduate programme?

In Equinor, we welcome fresh perspectives. As a graduate, you will not just observe, you will contribute from day one. Working alongside leading industry experts and learning from them, you will have a chance to work on projects and issues that really matter, seeing your input come to life.

Equinor’s graduate programme offers you a flying start to your career, giving you a broad introduction to our company and helping you gain experience and develop rapidly. We encourage you to build your career or even change direction as you go — all while enjoying the benefits of our Nordic culture and working ethos.

Our dynamic company has 25,000 ambitious colleagues in more than 20 countries around the world, united by a common ambition to make energy more sustainable.

What can you expect from Equinor’s graduate programme?

Our two-year graduate programme includes one or multiple rotations, giving you the chance to explore different parts of the business, gaining vital hands-on experience across disciplines. All our graduates are full employees of Equinor, entitling you to all the benefits available in your country of work.

You will develop and build your career through on-the job-training and development sessions and gatherings. You will also receive structured learning with access to courses offered by Equinor university. A buddy and mentor will be there to support you – helping you navigate any concerns in your career development.

What does it take to become a graduate?

In Equinor, we are searching for people with the motivation and drive to contribute to the energy transition in a rapidly changing industry. We are looking for new perspectives, new knowledge, and new people who are willing to challenge us.

When faced with complex challenges, you should be analytical and demonstrate excellent problem-solving skills, while seeking learning opportunities and constructive feedback. You will be a prime candidate if you are curious and can solve challenges through embracing new technology and performing in a digitally enabled environment.

And as an employee of Equinor it is essential to put safety first, both for yourself, and your colleagues around you.
