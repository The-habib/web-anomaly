Living in Cleveland

Case Western Reserve University sits in the heart of University Circle, just five miles from downtown Cleveland. Living and working in Cleveland means access to a growing food scene, parks and recreation, and arts and culture—including Playhouse Square, the country’s largest performing arts center outside of New York City.

If you want to cheer on the home team, the Cleveland Guardians baseball team, Cleveland Cavaliers basketball team and Cleveland Browns football team all play downtown.

No matter what you want from a city, you can find it here in Cleveland.
