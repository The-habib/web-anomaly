Lehigh University’s Location

Our campus is located in the heart of the Lehigh Valley, Pennsylvania’s third-most populous metropolitan area, which includes the cities of Allentown, Bethlehem and Easton. The Lehigh Valley boasts a thriving economy and arts & entertainment scene, and many adventures are within driving distance including the 1½-hour trip to New York City and both the 1-hour trips to Philadelphia or the Pocono Mountains. As the region continues to grow, Lehigh helps drive its innovation and economic vitality while providing students with expanding opportunities to learn, work and make an impact.

Things to Do in the Lehigh Valley

Beyond the city of Bethlehem itself, the Lehigh Valley is home to several top-notch entertainment centers, shopping malls and award-winning restaurants. From road trips short or long to sports and recreation, you’ll find plenty to do in the Valley.

Nearly every world cuisine imaginable is available in Bethlehem. The city boasts a wealth of restaurants serving up Japanese, African, Indian, Malaysian, Italian, Thai, Mexican and more. With more than 80 restaurants in downtown alone, local foodies can find everything from high-end dining to pizza shops. In celebration of its local food scene, Bethlehem celebrates Restaurant Week each year, allowing patrons to enjoy prix fixe meals at hugely discounted prices.

Places to Eat in Bethlehem

Places to Eat in the Greater Lehigh Valley

Bethlehem is home to numerous unique shops and national chains, offering visitors and locals alike a great shopping experience. Within walking distance from campus, you’ll find shops for apparel, music, vintage wares, books and more. On the historic north side, shoppers can stroll down Main Street and peruse the Moravian Book Shop, Aardvark Sports Shop and Season’s Olive Oil and Vinegar Taproom. Beyond shopping, you can enjoy a Phantoms ice hockey or Iron Pigs baseball game at the PPL Center or Coca-Cola Stadium - both a short distance away. If you’re looking for something more down to earth, try the great outdoors. The Valley is home to stellar hiking, biking, fishing and more.

Outdoor Recreation

Spectator Sports

The arts are alive in Bethlehem, where you won’t have to look far to find exciting seasonal events, nationally recognized musical acts, comedy, art and more. The city boasts three performing arts centers on the SouthSide alone, and the Banana Factory, a dynamic cultural arts center, is located just steps from Lehigh’s campus. Known as the “City of Festivals,” Bethlehem hosts more than 20 major festivals and over 150 mini-festivals each year. Most notably, Musikfest, taking place right here in Bethlehem, brings over 1 million visitors to town each year.

History runs deep in Bethlehem, weaving its 1741 origins as a Moravian settlement with its iconic legacy as a powerhouse of the American Industrial Revolution. The city’s beautifully preserved downtown stands proudly as a UNESCO World Heritage Site, offering a captivating look at colonial architecture just across the river from campus. From touring the nation’s oldest continuously operating bookstore on the historic north side to exploring repurposed industrial landmarks on the SouthSide, there is an endless wealth of heritage waiting to be discovered.

Community Ties

Community Service at Lehigh

Lehigh students do more than learn at Lehigh, they offer their service to the community while they’re here. Our mission is to provide students, faculty and staff the opportunities to make a difference locally and beyond.

Academic Experiences & Career Opportunities

Bethlehem’s growing economy provides numerous opportunities for internships, networking, and career advancement. The Lehigh Valley is home to major employers in engineering, healthcare, business and more, making it a great place to gain experience beyond the classroom and connect with potential employers during your time at Lehigh.
