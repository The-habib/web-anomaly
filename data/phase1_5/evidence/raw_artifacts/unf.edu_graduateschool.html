Welcome to the UNF Graduate School

At the University of North Florida, we are committed to supporting our graduate students through a variety of funding opportunities designed to promote academic excellence, research, and professional development. Through competitive awards and assistantships, students can receive financial support, gain valuable experience in their field, and enhance their career readiness.

Explore the options below to learn how UNF can support your Graduate journey to find your True North!

Graduate Funding Opportunities

We understand that cost is a major consideration when exploring graduate school, but it shouldn't stand in the way of your aspirations. At the University of North Florida, we’re committed to making graduate education more accessible and affordable. Through a range of financial support options, including graduate assistantships, scholarships, fellowships, and research grants, we help reduce the financial burden so you can focus on what matters most: your academic and professional growth.

    The Graduate School FAQs

    • Why should I consider a graduate degree at UNF?

      At the University of North Florida, graduate education is designed to empower students towards their True North with advanced knowledge and the expertise needed for success. Our programs offer in-depth expertise while encouraging the growth of vital skills such as effective communication, critical thinking and collaboration—skills that are highly valued by today’s employers. 

      According to U.S. News & World Report 2025 Best Graduate Schools, UNF’s part-time MBA, Education, Health Care Management, Nursing-Anesthesia, Physical Therapy, Public Affairs, Public Health and Social Work programs are ranked nationally. 

      In addition to an exceptional academic experience, UNF students benefit from living in Jacksonville, a coastal city known for its diverse culture, thriving business environment, and beautiful beaches. Jacksonville offers a unique blend of professional opportunities, outdoor activities and community engagement, making it an ideal place for students to learn, grow, and launch their careers north.

    • What are my job prospects after graduate school?

      UNF’s strong industry ties, innovative programs, and hands-on learning experiences ensure that our graduates are well-prepared to enter the workforce. Our faculty bring real-world experience and industry insights into the classroom, while our career services team provides personalized support, including resume building, interview preparation and networking opportunities. 

      UNF is also a leader in job placement in Florida for master’s graduates among Florida public universities, ensuring that our students have access to excellent job prospects upon graduation. Additionally, the vibrant Jacksonville area offers a wealth of job opportunities across a range of industries, from healthcare and education to business, technology, and public service. Our strong alumni network further supports you with mentorship and valuable connections to help launch and advance your career. 

      At UNF, graduate school is more than just earning a degree; it’s about preparing for a fulfilling career, and we are here to support your rising potential north.

    • Can I afford graduate school?

      We understand that financing graduate education is a key concern for many students. That’s why we are committed to providing a variety of resources to help make graduate school affordable. 

      UNF offers competitive tuition rates along with numerous scholarships, fellowships, and assistantships to financially support graduate students. We also work closely with students to explore external funding options, including grants and loans, ensuring access to the resources needed for success. Additionally, returning UNF students can take advantage of an Exclusive Graduate Tuition Rate, available to eligible alumni enrolling in graduate school within 15 consecutive semesters after earning their undergraduate degree. 

      You can visit the Assistantships and Awards overview page for information about available financial aid opportunities. You can also visit the UNF Academic Works website to view Foundation scholarships that you can apply for.  

      Graduate assistantships are also offered by each program. Contact your Graduate Program Director for an idea of any open positions in your program of study.

    • How can I fit graduate school into my busy schedule?

      At the University of North Florida, we understand that many of our graduate students balance multiple responsibilities, including work, family, and other commitments. That’s why we offer flexible program options designed to fit your busy life. 

      UNF’s graduate programs include part-time and online learning opportunities, allowing you to complete coursework on your schedule. Our online offerings, ranked by U.S. News & World Report as some of the Best Online Programs for 2025, include the nationally ranked Master’s in Education and Master’s in Nursing, giving you access to high-quality education no matter where you are. 

      Whether you prefer learning in the classroom or the flexibility of online courses, UNF provides the support you need to succeed. Our dedicated faculty and staff are here to help you manage your academic, professional and personal commitments, ensuring that you can achieve your goals without sacrificing your work-life balance.

    • What is student life on campus for a graduate student?

      At UNF, graduate students are an integral part of our vibrant campus community. While graduate school is academically rigorous, we understand the importance of a balanced life. Graduate students have access to a wide range of resources, support systems and activities designed to enrich both their academic and personal lives. 

      Graduate students often take part in specialized events, networking opportunities, and professional development activities that help build valuable connections within their field. Our graduate student organizations provide opportunities to collaborate, share experiences and support one another, fostering a sense of North camaraderie and professional growth. 

      In addition to academic resources, graduate students can enjoy all that our beautiful campus has to offer—from fitness facilities and outdoor spaces to cultural and social events. UNF’s location in Jacksonville also means students can take advantage of the city’s diverse cultural, professional, and recreational offerings.
