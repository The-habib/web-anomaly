June 2026 Bug Accelerator

July 1, 2026, 6:10 p.m.

June has been quite the month for us as we had to catch up from a later start to May. Still, we managed to hit a milestone of 60% completion on the 1.5 Milestones list, even if temporarily, thanks to the hard work of the BAP dev team and Inkscape's dedicated volunteers.

To help us clear the way to handling some of the remaining high priority issues, we did a sprint towards the end of this month where we tackled a vast number of lower priority issues, such as missing tooltips, minor quirks, and other little bugs that could make testing a pain.

Thanks are in order not only for the BAP devs and long-time Inkscape developers, but also for the many volunteer contributors who've helped in other ways, by following up on reports and testing sometimes obscure challenges to ensure that the results we're landing are hitting the mark.
