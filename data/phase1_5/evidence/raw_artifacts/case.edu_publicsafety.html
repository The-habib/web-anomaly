Welcome back, Spartans!

The Division of Public Safety is here to support a safe, connected, and thriving campus community as students, faculty, and staff return for the new academic year. Whether you're settling in, starting fresh, or continuing your journey, our team is ready 24/7 with the resources, training, and services that help you feel confident and supported on campus.

Stay informed, stay prepared, and stay connected, follow us on social media at @cwrupublicsafety for real‑time updates, safety tips, event announcements, and behind‑the‑scenes looks at the work we do every day.

The Case Western Reserve University Division of Public Safety is dedicated to providing quality service, safety, and a positive educational experience for the campus community. The Division is interested in recognizing employees that exhibit exceptional service and areas where the division or employee has failed to meet those standards. Please share your feedback with us.

Stay informed. The Daily Crime Log includes all crimes reported to the Division of Public Safety. Security Alerts are sent to advise the campus community of a crime that occurred on campus and present an ongoing threat. These notifications are known as "Timely Warnings" under the Clery Act, which requires colleges and universities to share information about campus crime and safety.
