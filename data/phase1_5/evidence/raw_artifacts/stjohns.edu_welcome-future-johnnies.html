Upcoming Events

Accepted Student Day!

Join other future Johnnies as the entire St. John's community celebrates your acceptance to St. John's! It's the biggest event of the season and you won't want to miss it!

Campus Tours

Take a Student Ambassador guided tour of our beautiful 102-acre campus, and learn more about the place you'll soon call home!

Academic Experiences

Department Tours allow an inside look at several of our state-of-the-art facilities that may not be highlighted on our Campus Tour. 
Immerse yourself and receive in-person classroom experience, seeing our exemplary faculty in their element, while sitting alongside current St. John's students in our tailored Classroom Visits.
Both Academic Experiences are available for registration within your Accepted Student Portal.   

Virtual Events

Join us for our live online chats to learn more about your financial aid package, hear about student life, talk to your Dean, and more! These virtual events have been tailored with you in mind, and to answer all of your questions so that you have a seamless transition for your fall semester.

New Student Orientation

New Student Orientation is a time where our University family comes together to welcome you to our community. Your participation is important in helping you embark on a successful experience at St. John’s.
New Student Orientation is mandatory for all incoming students.

Parent and Family University

Parent and Family University is created to celebrate and support you - the parent, guardian, family member of a St. John's University accepted student to the incoming Class of 2026! We will offer a series of virtual opportunities and chats for you to join St. John's faculty, administration, and students. Stay tuned for this year's dates.
