Global Engagement

We are committed to enriching the international composition, outlook and understanding of our community. We believe in empowering educational and research excellence, innovation and impact through collaboration with a strong network of partners who share our values from around the globe, so that, together, we can deliver solutions to global challenges and enhance our way of life.

Find out more about our international strategy, values and objectives, as well as some of the latest stats and stories from our global community, our collaborations and the resulting impact and opportunities.

Regional Engagement

As well as investing heavily in our campus, we’re dedicated to developing our presence in the region with projects in Coventry, Solihull and Wellesbourne. Our impact stretches far beyond the boundaries of our campus, and we engage positively with stakeholders across the whole of the West Midlands.

Campus and Community

Our campus is home to our staff and students, as well as being a destination for the local community and the region. With public spaces, state of the art sports facilities and one of the largest multi-art form arts centres outside of London, there's many different ways to enjoy all that our campus has to offer.

Warwick Institute of Engagement

Warwick Institute of Engagement is championing the future of high quality public engagement. It brings together Warwick’s extensive practical engagement expertise with pioneering academic research. We’re putting evidence led best practice at the core of our thinking – ensuring Warwick’s engagement with the public drives real impact for the communities we serve.
