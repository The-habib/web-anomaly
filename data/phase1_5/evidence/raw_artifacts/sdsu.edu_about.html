About San Diego State University

Transform Your Tomorrow

San Diego State University is a nationally ranked public research university that empowers students to make an impact. For over 128 years, SDSU has been rooted in one of the nation’s most dynamic and diverse regions. Located 30 minutes from the U.S.–Mexico border on Kumeyaay land, our university is defined by cultural connection, curiosity and a strong sense of community.

As a top Hispanic-Serving Institution and an Asian American Native American Pacific Islander-Serving Institution, SDSU welcomes students from every background. Here, students discover new possibilities, broaden their perspectives and prepare for a future with purpose.

What Makes SDSU Stand Out?

  • 220+ academic programs across high-demand fields
  • 400+ student organizations, D1 athletics, and a college campus full of life
  • $540K average 20-year ROI (PayScale)
  • Hands-on learning through internships, research and study abroad
  • Located in vibrant, innovation-rich San Diego
  • More than 500,000 alumni worldwide, strengthened by a San Diego community that embraces SDSU as its hometown university.
  • Supportive community for military-connected, first-generation, and LGBTQIA+ students, with nationally recognized programs for student success and belonging

Why Students Choose SDSU

Real-World Learning That Sets You Apart

With more than 200 undergraduate and graduate academic programs and dozens of research centers, SDSU helps students turn ideas into measurable impact. Internships, fieldwork and faculty-led research connect what’s learned in class with hands-on experiences across industries like biotech, education, health, media and engineering, giving students a head start on their careers.  

Incredible Value, Meaningful Results

An SDSU degree opens doors and delivers long-term value. Ranked among the top colleges nationwide for return on investment, our workforce-ready graduates launch careers at organizations like Google, Amazon, Qualcomm and the City of San Diego. With an average mid-career salary of $127,700 and a 20-year net ROI of $540,000 (PayScale), SDSU offers a smart, future-focused education.

A Campus Full of Student Life and Possibility

College life at SDSU is vibrant and energized. With more than 400 student organizations, NCAA Division I athletics, concerts, cultural festivals and outdoor recreation, students have endless ways to connect, express themselves and lead.

A Global, Inclusive Community

SDSU reflects the world students want to live in. More than half of our students identify as students of color, and we are proud to be a nationally recognized Hispanic-Serving Institution, a top campus for LGBTQ+ students, and one of the most diverse universities in the country. Wherever you're from, you’ll find a place to belong and thrive here.

An Unmatched Location

San Diego is a launchpad for opportunity and innovation. From beaches and trails to cultural districts and tech startups, SDSU students live and learn in a region where lifestyle and career growth go hand in hand.

Powered by Purpose

As a nationally ranked R1 university and a proud member of the California State University system, SDSU is committed to student success, community impact, and global engagement. With more than 500,000 alumni leading across California and beyond, the Aztec network is strong and far-reaching.

We recognize that every student’s goals are unique. Whether you're pursuing a professional degree, preparing for graduate school, or seeking ways to give back to your community, SDSU offers the mentorship, flexibility, and support to help you define your path and move forward with confidence.
