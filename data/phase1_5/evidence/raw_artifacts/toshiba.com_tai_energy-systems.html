Powering a Resilient, Sustainable Energy Future

For nearly a century, Toshiba energy systems have shaped modern power infrastructure. Our technologies connect every layer of the energy ecosystem, from power generation and transmission to energy storage and digital management, enabling utilities, industries, and communities to enhance reliability and advance resilience and decarbonization.

Power Generation

Toshiba delivers advanced energy systems that power tomorrow’s grids — including thermal, nuclear, and emerging clean-energy applications. Our turbine and generator solutions are engineered for durability, efficiency, and long-term stability, enabling utilities to maximize output while reducing operating costs.  

As global energy demand grows — driven by the rise of AI, data centers, and expanding digital infrastructure — the need for stable, scalable, low-carbon generation continues to increase. Toshiba helps power producers meet this demand with technologies that enhance reliability, strengthen grid resilience, and deliver the dependable energy supply required for a rapidly digitizing world.

Transmission & Distribution

Toshiba enhances grid reliability and helps utilities modernize infrastructure with advanced transmission and distribution technologies designed for optimal performance, safety, and long-term resilience. Our portfolio includes gas-insulated switchgear, high-voltage transformers, digital substations, and intelligent monitoring systems designed for dependable power delivery.  

As electricity demand grows — especially from electrification and high-density data centers — robust T&D networks are essential. Toshiba’s solutions enable a smarter, more resilient grid capable of managing load variability, integrating distributed resources, and delivering the reliable energy modern industries depend on.

SCiB™ Energy Storage & Mobility Solutions

Toshiba lithium-ion battery SCiB™ are engineered for fast charging, long cycle life, and consistent performance in demanding environments. Built on lithium titatium oxide chemistry, SCiB™ supports reliable electrification across mobility, logistics, industrial operations, and infrastructure.  

From peak-load management and renewable smoothing to powering AGVs, marine vessels, and heavy equipment, SCiB™ helps organizations reduce downtime, improve efficiency, and maintain resilient operations. As energy demands grow across data centers, manufacturing, and transportation, high-cycle energy storage plays a critical role in ensuring uninterrupted performance.

Featured Content: SCiB™ Whitepapers

To support system design and deployment, Toshiba engineers have developed a series of SCiB™ whitepapers highlighting real-world applications and best practices, including:

  • Peak shaving and load management
  • AGV and robotics fleet operations
  • Electric bus charging and fleet electrification
  • Marine and port electrification
  • Mining and heavy equipment applications

Explore how SCiB™ energy storage enables safe, efficient electrification and long-term operational value. Download the SCiB™ Whitepapers →

Digital Energy & Smart Energy Systems

Toshiba merges deep engineering expertise with advanced digital technologies to improve the performance and reliability of modern energy systems. Through EtaPRO LLC, we provide real-time monitoring, predictive analytics, and optimization tools that help operators maximize asset performance.  

EtaPRO is trusted across thermal, nuclear, hydro, and renewable plants to track trends, detect anomalies, reduce downtime, and extend equipment life. Its tools enhance operational visibility and support continuous improvement across diverse energy environments.  

As grids become more dynamic — influenced by renewable variability and fast-ramping loads from data centers — smart digital tools are essential. Toshiba’s digital solutions deliver actionable insights that strengthen reliability, improve efficiency, and support the transition to lower-carbon, data-driven power operations.
