Environmental sustainability

By embedding sustainability we will enable our university community to create positive change for society and environment.

How you can be sustainable
Our sustainability strategy

Sustainability in action

Explore how the University of Salford is embedding sustainability across all areas of its work. Through these pages, you can discover how we are managing performance, tracking progress, and driving meaningful change for our campus and through our teaching, learning, research and engagement with our communities.

You can also learn more about how our three strategic goals, Sustainable Campus, Sustainable Impact, and Sustainable Communities are brought to life through our operations, teaching and research, and engagement activities, including events, blogs, newsletters, and social media that connect our community and inspire collective action.
