Academics That Prepare You to Collaborate

At Case Western Reserve University, our courses are demanding yet collaborative—and always built for impact. As a nationally recognized research institution, we offer nearly 100 majors, minors and concentrations across STEM, healthcare, business, the humanities, social sciences and the arts—programs designed to challenge you and prepare you for what comes next.

Here, you’re admitted to the university, not a single area of study, giving you the flexibility to combine disciplines or refine your focus as your goals sharpen. 

Whether you’re preparing for medical school, graduate study or immediate entry into the workforce, your coursework is paired with applied experience. Nearly every undergraduate participates in experiential learning—research, internships, co-ops, creative projects or global study—often beginning in the first year. And through our human-centered approach to artificial intelligence in academic programs and beyond, you’ll leverage AI not as a replacement for human judgment but as a tool to accelerate your learning and discovery.

You won’t just complete assignments. You’ll build skills, produce work and graduate ready to compete.

Browse by major, minor, area of study or school—and see where a CWRU education can take you.
