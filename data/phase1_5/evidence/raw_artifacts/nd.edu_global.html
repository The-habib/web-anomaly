Global

Empowering encounter and exchange

Notre Dame is deeply engaged with communities and cultures around the world.

Global engagement is not an aspiration, it’s an expectation. Whether it’s international experiences on campus or research halfway across the world, our programs, faculty, and students are learning from the world… and learning for the world.

Notre Dame around the world

Our 12 global locations convene scholars, students, and leaders for research, debate, and cultural exchange.

Impacting global policy

Students in the Keough School of Global Affairs are confronting the world's greatest challenges with a focus on human dignity, in Washington, D.C. and around the world.

Keough School of Global Affairs

Global scholarship

Global encounters are a hallmark of the Notre Dame experience. With 87% of our students participating in study abroad, we challenge each other to learn through the soles of our feet and grow as global citizens.

Education Abroad

We seek to be a reflection of the greater human family

Over 3,000 international community members are supported by Notre Dame’s Office of International Student and Scholar Affairs, before, during, and after their time on campus. Over the past decade, the international population at Notre Dame has increased by roughly 34%.

International Student and Scholar Affairs

Global engagement

Making a meaningful contribution to our world today requires connection and an unwavering commitment to solidarity in the pursuit of human flourishing. 

Whether we talk about poverty or climate, technology or democracy, peace or justice, the biggest challenges humankind faces cut across national borders. At Notre Dame, we are building bridges to address the most pressing issues facing our world.

Rev. Robert A. Dowd, C.S.C

President

Notre Dame Global

Notre Dame Global is at the center of cultivating and administering the broad range of our programs, partnerships, and objectives that bring Notre Dame to the world, and the world to Notre Dame.
