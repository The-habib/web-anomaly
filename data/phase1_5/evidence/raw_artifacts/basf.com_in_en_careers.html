Recruitment Fraud Notice – Stay Safe When Applying

We are committed to a fair and secure recruitment process. We have become aware of fraudulent job offers misusing BASF’s name, particularly targeting jobseekers in the region.

Watch out for:

  • Requests for payment or sensitive personal details

  • Emails from unofficial domains (e.g. Gmail, Yahoo)

  • Job offers made without a formal interview

Our process:

  • All communication will come from @basf.com or @partners.basf.com

  • We do not ask for payment at any stage

  • Interviews are conducted via recognised platforms or in person

  • Please report any suspicious contact to our HR team via official BASF channels.
