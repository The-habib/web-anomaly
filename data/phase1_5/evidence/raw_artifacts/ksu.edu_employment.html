Find a Future in Purple

You Belong at K-State

As the nation's first operational land-grant university, we're dedicated to being a positive force for Kansans - creating a brighter future for our region, nation and the world. We're building a next-generation land-grant workforce rooted in a culture of access and opportunity with flexible benefits and rewards that meet the unique needs of our employees. We are committed to inspire learning, creativity, discovery and engagement that positively impacts society and transforms lives in Kansas and around the world. We want you to join us! Explore our bold vision and values driving us forward.

What's great at K-State

Our Communities

K-State has campuses in Manhattan, Salina and Olathe with engagement in all 105 counties in Kansas. Our impact stretches wide, but our vision is people-centered — supporting, valuing and inspiring a culture of access and opportunity for all. Whether in the heart of the Konza Prairie or a burgeoning great plains city, you'll find your place within a community committed to enriching the quality of life in Kansas.

Making an Impact

From small towns at the forefront of agricultural innovation to large cities elevating the field of aviation and the tallgrass in between, we think big, leading through learning and discovery. Wildcats are tasked with bringing economic prosperity to the state of Kansas to solve global challenges.

Experience Kansas

Hike the Flint Hills at sunset, spot a herd of bison or spend your day kayaking on the lake. There’s plenty to do outside our campuses. Find yourself at rodeos, tailgates and state-of-the-art museums all in one place. Storied western towns paired with bustling cities of working professionals create a culture as vast and inspiring as our landscape. See all that Kansas has to offer.

Benefits and Perks of Working at K-State

K-State is a great place to work and is one of the top employers in Kansas. With three campuses, nine colleges, a graduate school and a commitment to student success, belonging and community, K-State offers a culture of access and opportunity for all. 

Working at K-State offers many benefits and perks, including market-based compensation, excellent benefit programs, outstanding retirement savings options, opportunities for personal and professional development, flexible schedules, remote and hybrid work options, access to sports and cultural events, on-site childcare and much more.

Health and Wellness

 Day one coverage of a variety of medical insurance plans (including prescription, dental, vision, and Employee Assistance Programs) available through the State of Kansas.

Holidays and Leave

K-State offers generous leave programs (vacation, sick, parental, etc.) and 10 paid holidays.

Retirement

Multiple options to help you save for retirement including the mandatory state retirement plans with employer contributions and options for voluntary savings plans.

Education

Tuition Assistance is available for full time employees.  Spouses and dependents of employees may be eligible for a dependent/spouse tuition grant. Certain exceptions apply. 

Compensation

The compensation structure at K-State supports competitive and equitable pay.

Professional Development

K-State strives to promote and support employee development and organizational effectiveness by providing high-quality educational training programs.

Campus Safety

The Annual Security and Fire Safety Report (Clery Report) is intended to inform the public of information about crime on and around campus, efforts to improve campus safety, and university policies.

Its purpose is to provide individuals with information that will help them make informed decisions relating to their own safety and the safety of others. Anyone, including prospective students and employees, may obtain a paper copy by contacting the Clery Act Federal Compliance Coordinator at ksuclery@k-state.edu.

General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) 

Effective May 25, 2018, the European Union (“EU”) General Data Protection Regulation (“GDPR”) places additional obligations on organizations that control or process personally identifiable information about persons in the European Union.

This Privacy Statement and Request for Consent only addresses processing of information collected directly from prospective employees through the Careers website. It describes the practices of how the Division of Human Resources of Kansas State University collects, uses, and maintains (hereinafter “processes”) personal information from visitors who submit information to the Kansas State University’s Careers website (https://careers.k-state.edu/home) as part of applying for employment with Kansas State University.

 

K-State Participates in E-Verify 

K-State participates in E-Verify and will provide the federal government with Form I-9 information to confirm that new employees are authorized to work in the U.S.  US Immigration law protects legally-authorized workers from discrimination based on their citizenship status and national origin.
