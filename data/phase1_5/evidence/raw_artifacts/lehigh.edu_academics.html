A Legacy of Ambitious, Inquisitive Learners

Lehigh University’s approach to academics is centered on you. In a vibrant and collaborative community, you’ll experience a rigorous academic environment, a flexible interdisciplinary curriculum, hands-on learning and innovative research. You’ll gain the skills to solve real-world problems and make a lasting impact.

Interdisciplinary

Our strong belief in the value of bringing multiple disciplines and perspectives together for meaningful collaboration challenges you to learn broadly and think deeply.

Personalized

As a research university with an 11:1 student-to-faculty ratio, Lehigh allows you to be at the cutting edge of knowledge and allows your curiosity to define your curriculum.

Hands-on

Lehigh has always believed in learning through doing. We prioritize both experiential and authentic learning, and give you the opportunity to engage pragmatically with real-world problems.

Learning Beyond the Lecture

At Lehigh, learning extends far beyond the classroom, from campus and across the globe. With opportunities for hands-on experiences, internships and research, we create a space where every student can grow, explore and thrive—developing both academically and personally in a community that supports and challenges them every step of the way.

+
-
Design Labs at WilburPowerhouse and Mountaintop

Lehigh's Design Labs are a network of maker spaces across campus that provide students with resources they need to bring their ideas to life, from electronics and robotics design studios to a woodshop and digital fabrication lab.

+
-
Iacocca International Internship

Iacocca interns apply their skills to real-world challenges. They contribute to global organizations while immersing themselves in a different culture for several weeks—proving that even across the world, our commonalities are greater than our differences.

+
-
LehighSiliconValley

LehighSiliconValley is a week-long program from the Baker Institute for Entrepreneurship, Creativity and Innovation. Students are immersed in the Bay Area entrepreneurial ecosystem through sessions featuring founders, investors and stakeholders from startups and established companies.

+
-
Clubs & Organizations

Lehigh has more than 200 student clubs and organizations. These groups are an integral and active part of campus life and provide students with opportunities to enhance their social connections, leadership skills and interests.

+
-
StartUp Lehigh Valley

Now hosted by Lehigh University, this annual competition is designed to showcase entrepreneurship and innovation throughout the region. Modeled after the TV show Shark Tank, it awards new business owners more than $30,000 in prizes.
