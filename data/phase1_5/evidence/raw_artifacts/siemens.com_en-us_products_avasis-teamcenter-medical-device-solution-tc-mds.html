Why Improve product development efficiency?

The Teamcenter Medical Device Solution (TC MDS) complements the established Teamcenter software. Its highly integrated interfaces to Teamcenter, Polarion, and other authoring tools act as a link and thus simplify the development process throughout the entire product life cycle.

The core functions are:

Excellent visualization and traceability: Complex documentation can be excellently visualized and traced at any time with TC MDS – paperless and digital, from concept to real product.

Comprehensive project overview: With the push of a button, a comprehensive view of the project and its progress is visible, thanks to the simple and clear structure.

Continuous process management: TC MDS enhances security through continuous process management. The status of all tasks and the scheduling situation are clearly visible throughout all development process phases.

Automatic consideration of guidelines: Company-internal and regulatory guidelines are automatically considered and adhered to.

Benefits

  • Interdisciplinarity: Thanks to specific views for each user, work gets faster
  • Usability: offers simple user navigation, despite complex development processes
  • Baseline: a foundation for traceability and decision-making in case of changes
  • Link: TC MDS provides all interaction of the different disciplines in one view
