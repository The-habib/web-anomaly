Student Life at SDSU

There is nothing like the Aztec student experience.

Live a Good Life

Welcome to San Diego State University! With year-round sunshine and the prestige of being a Division I research institution, SDSU truly has it all. We blend laid-back beach vibes with world-class academics. Come explore SDSU—your home away from home!

Student Housing

Living on campus offers first-year students convenient access to college life and opportunities.

Make Yourself at Home

On-campus housing amenities include:

  • Wi-Fi
  • TV streaming
  • Utilities included
  • Diverse meal plans and dining options
  • Live-in faculty and staff support
  • Community activities and programs
  • 24/7 maintenance service for emergency repairs

Dining

Discover a foodie's paradise on campus!

With over 20 dining spots to choose from, satisfy every craving with options like Starbucks, acai bowls, pizza, Panda Express, and so much more. Perfect for those late-night study sessions or a quick bite between classes!

Campus Eateries

Explore San Diego with Ease

At SDSU, getting around is easy and car-free. With the campus trolley station, bus connections, student discounts, Safe Ride shuttles, scooters, and Zipcar, you can get to class, downtown, or the beach quickly, affordably, and sustainably.

Own Your Everyday Vibes

Associated Students, one of the largest student governing bodies in the California State University (CSU) system, plans events; manages the Aztec Recreation Center (ARC) and the Conrad Prebys Aztec Student Union, among other facilities; and works to ensure that your Aztec experience will be unforgettable.

Study

Experience the perfect blend of study and relaxation at the Conrad Prebys Aztec Student Union! The student lounge offers a welcoming space to collaborate on group projects or take a break. Hosting a large meeting? Reserve one of the many available conference rooms.

Eat

The Union offers a wide variety of dining options. From the convenience store’s "grab and go" options to sitting down to a custom-ordered burger, Aztecs have several delicious choices served at affordable prices, including: Oggi's Pizza Express, the Habit Burger, Aztec Market, Shake Smart, Starbucks, and Los Primos.

Play

The Student Union is your go-to spot for entertainment and connection all year long. From concerts and movie nights to food demos and cultural celebrations, there's always something happening. Head to Aztec Lanes to bowl, break a sweat at the Aztec Recreation Center (ARC) Express, or unleash your creativity at The Backdoor Studio.

Elevate Your Experience

SDSU is alive with possibilities. You can see top performers and musicians at Viejas Arena and the Cal Coast Credit Union Open Air Theatre, meet new friends at Aztec Nights, and take the trolley to cheer on your Aztecs at Snapdragon Stadium.

Level Up Your Wellness

Your path to fitness runs through the pool, the gym, and even the bay at SDSU.

Aquaplex

Take advantage of San Diego’s year-round sunshine at the Aztec Aquaplex with two heated outdoor pools, a 20-person spa, tennis courts, and full-service locker rooms. Lounge chairs, rafts, and water sports gear are provided. Entry is included with Aztec Recreation Center (ARC) membership.

Aztec Recreation Center (ARC)

The ARC supports your fitness and wellness seven days a week with 500+ cardio and weight machines, daily fitness classes, drop-in courts, intramurals, sport clubs, climbing walls, an indoor track, Adapted Athletics, and a healthy on-site eatery.

Mission Bay Aquatic Center

Learn new skills, check out watersports equipment rentals, and get on the water at the Mission Bay Aquatic Center, one of the world’s largest instructional waterfront facilities. Earn SDSU credit or take recreation classes in kayaking, sailing, stand up paddleboarding, surfing, wakeboarding, and windsurfing.

Experience D1 Athletics. Aztec Pride.

When you enroll at San Diego State, you become part of over 125 years of tradition. And no tradition is stronger than Aztec school spirit! Currently enrolled SDSU students are admitted FREE for all regular season, home Aztec athletic events with a valid SDSU RedID card, while supplies last.

Embrace Your Supportive Community

You’re never alone—SDSU’s holistic programs support your health, growth, and success.

Health & Wellness

SDSU’s community of care focuses on support and resources dedicated to your complete well-being. The Calpulli Center on campus houses the Economic Crisis Response Team (ECRT) and the Basic Needs Center, Student Disability Services (SDS), Student Health Services (SHS), and Well-Being & Health Promotion (W&HP) — all available to students as part of basic tuition and fees. Students have access to a pharmacy, X-ray lab, immunizations, family planning services, orthopedic care, and more.

Counseling, Psychological Support

Counseling & Psychological Services (C&PS) offers a wide range of services to support students with a variety of concerns. Services include individual counseling, group therapy, workshops, specialized programs, and appointments with Luna, our therapy dog. Need someone to talk to? Talk It Out is a convenient, drop-in service offering informal, confidential consultations with C&PS therapists—no appointment needed. Join virtually or in person!
