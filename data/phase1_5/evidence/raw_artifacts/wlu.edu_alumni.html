Traditions That Keep Us Connected

W&L’s student-run Honor System asks students to not lie, cheat, steal or violate the community’s trust. Personal integrity, combined with a shared commitment to the collective good, creates an open and supportive environment for students to thrive.
W&L’s longstanding Speaking Tradition encourages people to greet each other in passing, which fosters a friendly atmosphere and sense of community from the moment you set foot on campus.

Alumni Engagement

The staff of the Office of Alumni Engagement provides programming and support for W&L alumni.

John A. Jensen III ’01

Executive Director of Alumni and Career Services

  • P: 540-458-8593
  • E: jensenj@wlu.edu

John was appointed executive director of Alumni and Career Services at Washington and Lee University in July 2022. In this role, he leads a combined team consisting of the offices of Alumni Engagement, Career and Professional Development, and Lifelong Learning. Working together, his team engages and supports students, alumni and parents throughout their W&L experience, from the first year on campus to graduation and beyond.

Hillary Cocke

Director of Alumni Engagement

  • P: 540-458-8551
  • E: hcocke@wlu.edu

Hillary is the director of alumni engagement at Washington and Lee University, where she leads the Alumni Engagement team, overseeing chapter programs and on-campus alumni events. She joined W&L at the end of 2024, bringing extensive experience in alumni relations and development.

Hillary earned a bachelor’s degree in economics and American studies from the University of Virginia and a Master of Science in social work from the University of Tennessee, Knoxville. Before joining W&L, she worked in alumni relations and development at UVA’s School of Medicine and UVA’s Darden School of Business.

Katharine Farrar ’11

Director of Alumni Chapter Programs

  • P: 540-458-8464
  • E: kfarrar@wlu.edu

Katharine serves as director of alumni chapter programs, overseeing more than 75 alumni chapters across the country and abroad. She provides creative and structural vision for chapter programming, ensuring alumni stay connected to W&L no matter where life takes them. A proud General with deep ties to the university, Katharine loves helping alumni keep W&L a steady and meaningful part of their lives.

Outside of work, she enjoys spending time with family, cooking and baking, cheering on her favorite sports teams, and seeing live music.

Michelle Jones

Director of Alumni Reunions and Events

  • P: 540-458-8886
  • E: mjones@wlu.edu

Michelle “MJ” joined Washington and Lee University in January 2018, first serving as associate director, leading W&L Fund alumni volunteers. She now serves as director of alumni reunion and events as part of the Alumni Engagement team.

Before W&L, MJ spent seven years as director of public relations and development for Connections+ (formerly Rockbridge Area Hospice). On campus, she can often be found taking early morning walks along the Colonnade. Off campus, she enjoys tending to her flower garden, spending time with family and friends, and reading a good mystery novel.

James Lambert

Director of Lifelong Learning

  • P: 540-458-8724
  • E: jlambert@wlu.edu

James Lambert began serving as Director of Lifelong Learning in July 2025. Previously he served as Provost and professor of English at Southern Virginia University, and prior to that worked as Chair of the English Department at the American University of Kuwait. He received an MA in American Literature at University of Arizona and a doctorate in Early Modern British Literature at the University of Iowa. He has published on Shakespeare, Milton, and a smattering of Renaissance poets and religious thinkers, and he continues to think about teaching, learning, and everything in between. In addition to his work as the Director of Lifelong Learning, James is also the host and executive producer of the W&L After Class Podcast and has a courtesy appointment as professor in the English Department.

Taylor is the associate director of alumni and career services operations and marketing at Washington and Lee University. In this role, she leads a team dedicated to showcasing W&L’s robust offerings in career development, alumni engagement and lifelong learning.
