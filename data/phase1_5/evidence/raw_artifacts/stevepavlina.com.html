Fully Inhabit Your Heart

This website is a beautiful gateway of kind, caring, loving support for people who choose to live rich, juicy, heart-centered lives infused with wonder and delight – and still be able to function well in the practical human world without crashing and burning. That's a big ask, so this is a big site with tons of resources to help you. If you join the email list, you'll receive timely invitations to deepen your journey and to partake in group experiences with like-hearted people, both in-person and online.

I'm Steve Pavlina, and my role here is to serve as Wonder Guide into this space. For this purpose I provide oodles of support: hundreds of free articles, audios, videos, courses, and live events. I work and live in a rich heartspace of ongoing exploration, discovery, and sharing, and this is my outward expression of that.

The ultimate form of support here is Conscious Growth Club, which has been going strong since 2017 and opens for new members to join during the last week of April each year. That's our community space where we connect through a private online forum, regular Zoom calls, and a 24/7 Zoom Lounge.

Please be advised that entering this space is very likely to be life-changing, as it has for many people since 2004. If you've just landed here for the first time, there's a powerful reason for that. Let your intuition guide your exploration, and trust what invites you in. We're here for you. Let us help you create a life that makes your heart sing.
