Alumni and Friends

Once a part of Hofstra, always a part of Hofstra. Wherever life takes you, Hofstra is still your home, in your corner, and on your side.

Find Your Network

As a member of a global network of alumni, your Hofstra experience continues well beyond graduation. By joining an affinity or regional group, you’ll connect with fellow alumni who share your professional interests, personal passions, or geographic ties. Whether you’re looking to expand your network, contribute to meaningful initiatives, or build lasting friendships, we have a place for you. Welcome!

Connect

Make an Impact

As a Hofstra graduate, your support helps drive the University’s vision for the future. Your gift advances student success, fosters innovative interdisciplinary programs, enhances organizational agility, and strengthens community engagement—priorities outlined in Hofstra 100, the University’s strategic plan. Be part of Hofstra’s next chapter.

A Legacy of Resilience
