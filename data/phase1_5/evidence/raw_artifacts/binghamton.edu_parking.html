Parking at Binghamton University is designed to support students, faculty, staff and visitors. See the side tabs for more information on parking options and purchasing a virtual permit. 

Parking portal

When is a permit needed?

When parking from 7 a.m. Monday through 4 p.m. Friday, a valid parking permit is needed. Paid parking options are also available in the parking garage, visitors lot and metered spaces.

On the weekends from 4 p.m. Friday until 7 a.m. Monday, no parking permit is needed to park in a valid parking space. Paid spaces and all other parking rules and regulations are also still in effect, including permit designation and overnight parking rules.
