At Glencore, we are energising today and advancing tomorrow. That means we are focused on supporting the energy needs of today whilst investing in our portfolio of transition-enabling commodities as the world moves towards a low-carbon economy.

So, whether our job is to produce resources safely, efficiently and sustainably, or to get those materials to our customers, we are united by the same Purpose: responsibly sourcing the commodities that advance everyday life.

Working at Glencore

Our workforce is dedicated, driven, and diverse – at every level of our business and in more than 30+ countries around the world. Find out more about our culture and how you can advance your career with us.

Life at Glencore

With operations spanning the production, marketing and recycling of critical metals and transition fuels that support governments, business and societies in their economic development and efforts to decarbonise, Glencore is one of the world’s largest global diversified natural resource companies. 

Our teams

Our integrated marketing and industrial business sets us apart from our competitors and is the basis for our entrepreneurial focus on value generation.

At the heart of this business is a host of talent, diversity, dedication, and passion that is shaping the future of our world by responsibly sourcing the commodities that advance everyday life.  

Fostering a respectful and inclusive culture

We believe a respectful and inclusive culture is crucial to helping us grow our business, strengthening our culture and our ability to deliver our business objectives. 

We are committed to building a working environment where we support each other and encourage the full and active participation of everyone in our workforce.
