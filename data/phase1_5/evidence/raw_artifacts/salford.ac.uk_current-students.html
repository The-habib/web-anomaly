Your services

Below you can find helpful links to the wide variety of services you have available to you as a student. This could be everything from Wellbeing and Counselling to askUS, the best place to go when you’re not sure where to go.

  • Accommodation - Our on-campus accommodation and other student accommodation information.
  • askUS - The best place to go when you’re not sure where to go. The askUS team can help connect you with the most relevant student support service.
  • Careers and enterprise - Explore your career options and develop essential employability skills.
  • Contact us - A directory of contact information for different university services.
  • Digital IT – Find guides for using our systems and log tickets through the Service Portal.
  • Disability Inclusion Service - Our team works with you to ensure you have fair and equal access to your education.
  • Faith - Our on-campus Faith Centre is open to all students and staff, regardless of your beliefs.
  • Health centre and medical services - Look after your physical and mental health with our on-campus health centre.
  • Immigration and visas – Our team can support you with any visa queries and your routes after study.
  • IT Support and Student log ins - A link to key IT support and Student log ins including Blackboard, the IT Service Portal, and the Student Hub.
  • Library - Find academic skills support, study spaces, reading lists and more.
  • Money advice and funds - Resources to help you get to grips with the costs of being at university and financial support you can get.
  • Report It – Where to report it If you experience or witness any form of violence, harassment, bullying, discrimination or hate on campus. Let us know so we can do something about it.
  • Safety and security - Our Security team is available for advice and assistance 24 hours a day.
  • School-based employability support – Your School’s Employability Hub can connect you to valuable opportunities like work placements and internships.
  • Student support teams - Where you can find an overview of the different student support teams available to you, and links to their contact information.
  • Unitemps – Find temporary and permanent jobs within the university and surrounding businesses.
  • Urgent assistance and all safety information - Where to go if you are concerned about the immediate wellbeing and welfare for either yourself or a fellow student.
  • Wellbeing and counselling - Our team can help you succeed by sharing tips for taking care of yourself, or signpost to useful services.

Your responsibilities

Below you can find helpful links outlining some of the key responsibilities you have as a University of Salford student. Here you can find everything from our student conduct policy to how you deal with attendance or absence during study.

  • Attendance and absence - Expectations of students in terms of attendance, including maximum periods of study and absence.
  • Fees and funding - Information on tuition fees, maintenance loans, funding support, and bursaries.
  • ICT Acceptable Use Policy -  The policy covering the rules and behaviours expected from everyone who uses the University’s IT equipment and systems.
  • Intellectual Property Policy - The policy dealing with the ownership, protection and commercial exploitation of intellectual property (IP) created by students.
  • Registration - You must register every year, here you can find information about registering as a returning student.
  • Self-Service Portal - Where you make requests for Exceptional Circumstances (formerly Personal Mitigating Circumstances or PMCs), module transfers, taking a study break, programme transfers, or withdrawal. 
  • Student terms and conditions - These terms and conditions apply to all students who are studying taught or research programmes and set out the provisions that are central to our relationship with you. 

Student conduct

The expectations of student conduct, and the processes which the University will use to consider instances of inappropriate behaviour. 

  • Applicant and Student Criminal and Conviction Policy and Procedure – All students must inform the University of relevant conditional cautions, criminal convictions or pending charges/investigations.
  • Fitness to Practise – Relates to professional misconduct and/or professional unsuitability of students whose professions are regulated by Professional, Statutory and Regulatory Bodies.
  • Sexual harassment and misconduct - How we educate, inform, prevent and protect our community from sexual harassment and misconduct, as well as our expectations for your behaviour regarding consent and relationships.
  • Student Code of Conduct – The standard of behaviour we expect of you while you are on campus, and in certain circumstances, off campus.
  • Student Charter – Covers the mutual commitments and expectations between all students and the University.
  • Student Misconduct Procedure – This procedure allows us to deal with allegations relating to student conduct, discipline or behaviour.
  • Teaching session behaviour expectations - Detailed guidance about what standards and types of behaviour in teaching sessions we expect.

Here you can find key information and course support you may need during your studies to help you achieve your potential. 

  • Academic Appeals - Our Academic Appeals Procedure and the process for submission and review of an academic appeal.
  • Academic Handbook - Academic policies and procedures that support our academic quality assurance and enhancement activities.
  • Academic Misconduct - The Academic Misconduct Procedure, and definitions of the various types of misconduct including plagiarism and collusion.
  • Assessment and Feedback - Support and advice around assessments.
  • Blackboard Learn - Our Virtual Learning Environment (VLE) used to support your teaching and learning.
  • Checking in to class - You need to record your attendance for every academic session, find details on how to register your attendance for all classes, using Study Goal.
  • Check your timetable - Where to find your classes.
  • Complaints - How to raise concerns about services provided by us.
  • Engagement - Engagement information for Student Route and Tier 4 visa holders including our Engagement Policy.
  • Exam Rules - Learn about the rules and regulations, who to contact if you need support and make sure you are prepared.
  • Graduation - Once you've passed your course, you'll be sent an email invitation to attend Graduation.
  • Inclusive teaching and learning - Our Inclusive Support Statement which lays out the inclusive practice in place to support you.
  • Interrupting / Taking a break from studies - The process if, for any reason, you are thinking about leaving your programme or wish to temporarily pause or interrupt your studies.
  • Online module enrolment - Where you make your optional module choices (selected courses only).
  • Exceptional Circumstances (formerly Personal Mitigating Circumstances or PMCs) - If you have suffered from serious exceptional circumstances that are beyond your control and that have affected your ability to complete assessments.
  • Printing and photocopying - How to print and photocopy across campus.
  • Reading lists - How you can access your reading lists for your course.
  • Reasonable Adjustment Plan (RAP) - A plan put in place to ensure you have fair and equal access to your education.
  • Recording a lecture or class - Rules set out for the recording of teaching sessions.
  • Referencing - Information around the approved referencing style.
  • Results letters - Guidance to help you understand the information in your assessment results letters, including resists and retakes.
  • Staff who can help you - Teams to contact if you need support with your studies.
  • Student protection plan - A plan that explains what measures we will take to protect you if there is a risk to the continuation of your programme of study.
  • Study abroad information - Experience the opportunity of a lifetime and take your studies or work overseas.
  • Withdrawing or leaving the university - The process for withdrawing or leaving the university.

askUS

Not sure where to go? Our askUS team is here to help.

Our askUS advisers can give you the right advice when you need it most, whether you have a question about finance, wellbeing, faith or visas and immigration or if you need support with your studies, your career or a disability.

  • Call: +44 (0)161 295 5000
  • Email: askus@salford.ac.uk

Want to find more specific information? You can speak to someone on our askUS help desks across our campuses, or check our askUS topics section.
