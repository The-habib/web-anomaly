Sponsorship

Strengthen the future of open source technologies you rely on

    Become a sponsor

Your impact starts here

Whether you’re an individual developer or an enterprise, your sponsorship directly supports the stability, security, and innovation of open source technologies that power products and industries around the world. Together, we ensure open source remains resilient, trustworthy, and accessible for everyone.

Explore sponsorship opportunities

Why sponsor?

Keep open source secure and sustainable

Your contribution helps fund code audits, vulnerability management, and development best practices that strengthen the reliability, stability, and security of the open technologies you depend on.

Fuel developer-led innovation

Sponsorship supports the continuous improvement of open source software and platforms, making them better, faster, and more scalable for modern development.

Support open and trusted collaboration

The Eclipse Foundation provides a trusted, governance-backed space for collaboration. Your support helps us maintain open participation and transparent development across industries.

Strengthen the ecosystem you rely on

Your investment helps unite developers, companies, and researchers to solve shared challenges and create technologies that drive real-world impact.

Corporate sponsorship enables businesses to invest in open innovation that aligns with their strategies, while individual contributions help sustain and grow the technologies we all rely on every day. Together, this support fuels the initiatives, ecosystems, people, and projects that move open source forward.

Frequently asked questions

How are sponsorship funds used?

Your contribution supports our global open source community. All contributions are tracked and allocated to their designated initiatives or working group to advance their specific objectives.

What payment methods do you accept?

We currently accept credit card payments (processed via Stripe) and PayPal. Stripe supports major international card networks such as Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, JCB, and others, depending on your country of payment.

How do I set up a recurring sponsorship?

Recurring contributions can only be set up through PayPal. On the sponsorship page for your selected initiative:

  1. Choose PayPal as your payment method
  2. Select a 'Monthly' or 'Yearly', as your recurring schedule.
  3. Select your preferred contribution amount.
  4. Click the 'Sponsor and Support' to complete the setup via PayPal

Future contributions will be charged automatically based on your selected schedule.
