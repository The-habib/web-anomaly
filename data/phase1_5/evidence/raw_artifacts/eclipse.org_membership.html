Frequently asked questions

How do I become a member?

In order for your organisation to become a member, you must complete and sign the following documents through our Member Enrolment Portal:

  • Membership Application Form (you will need to indicate which class of membership you are joining. Most organisations join as Contributing members.)
  • Membership Agreement
  • Member Committer and Contributor Agreement (optional, but recommended)

If you would prefer, you may instead download, complete, and sign the documents and email to us for processing.

Note: Membership in an Eclipse Working Group requires the execution of the respective Working Group’s Participation Agreement. For more information, review the list of the current Eclipse Working Groups and their respective Participation Agreement.

For Committer Members

If you are a Committer who is not already a Member by virtue of your employer being a Member, you are welcome to join as an individual Committer Member. The steps involved in becoming a committer are described here; individual Committer Members should download and email us the Membership Agreement.

Which membership level is right for my organisation?

It depends on your goals.

  • Associate is ideal for organisations getting started
  • Contributing is for active participation in projects and collaborations
  • Strategic is for organisations looking to lead, influence governance, and drive industry direction
Can we upgrade our membership later?

Yes. Many organisations start at one level and upgrade as their involvement grows.

Do we need to contribute code to become a member?

No. While many members actively contribute to projects, membership is open to organisations that want to collaborate, influence, and support the ecosystem in different ways.

What is the Member Portal?

The Eclipse Foundation Member Portal gives your organisation visibility into its participation across the ecosystem. Track your involvement in projects and industry collaborations, and access resources and tools designed to support your engagement.

Do we need to join a working group?

No, but many members choose to participate in working groups to collaborate more closely on specific technologies and industry initiatives.

What is Eclipse Foundation AISBL?

Eclipse Foundation AISBL is the Foundation’s European-based legal entity. Most new members join through AISBL, which offers the same benefits as the original U.S.-based organisation.

Who can I talk to before joining?

Our team is happy to help. Use the Contact us about membership form and we’ll connect you with the right person.

Ready to shape the future of open source?

If you can’t see the contact form here, please reach out to us at membership.coordination@eclipse-foundation.org for assistance with your membership inquiry.
