Discover what it means to be a Vandal

Uncover your passion and define your path at University of Idaho. From hands-on learning to a supportive Vandal community, U of I offers the tools you need to reach your goals and educators who will guide your journey. Step into opportunities, explore your potential and discover a college experience that’s built for you and your career aspirations.

Academic opportunities

As a U of I student, you’ll have access to over 200 areas of study across nine academic colleges, ranging from agriculture and engineering to business and law. Our programs provide a comprehensive education to prepare you for the career you imagine. U of I faculty are forward-thinking experts who make time to provide personalized attention and mentorship to their students.

  • Apply classroom learning through internships, research, fieldwork and Co-Op programs starting your first semester.
  • Discover specialized degree programs like PGA Golf Management, make an impact on society with a WWAMI medical education or create healthy spaces with a degree in landscape architecture.
  • Earn your first or next degree: U of I offers associate, bachelor’s, master’s and doctorate degrees as well as certificate programs.

Not sure where to start? Chat with one of our admissions counselors to learn more about U of I’s academic opportunities.
