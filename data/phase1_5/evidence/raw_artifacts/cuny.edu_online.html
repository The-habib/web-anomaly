You’re ready: Get there with an online program

220+ online options | 1:1 Support | Meaningful Affordability

Redesigning what it means to be flexible

When a university founded on access builds an online degree, it’s designed around up-to-the-minute skills you need. It’s built for your busy schedule.

Your CUNY Online experience comes with:

  • Flexible Learning: CUNY’s online classes are designed to fit into your busy schedule.
  • Faster to Finish: We assist in identifying all the credits from previously completed classes to accelerate your path to graduation.
  • Quality Education: Receive the same rigorous academic experience as on-campus students, ensuring a valuable degree.
  • 1:1 Support: Dedicated advisors and professors are available to provide personalized assistance.
  • Affordable Tuition: Benefit from competitive tuition rates, making your education more accessible.
  • Career-Ready Skills: Gain up-to-the-minute skills and knowledge that are highly valued in today’s job market.
  • Interactive ‘Classrooms’: Engage with faculty and peers in a collaborative online environment.

Learn On Your Terms

There are many ways to pay for your education. Online and part-time students can get several types of financial aid, including New York State TAP (tuition assistance program).

Online classes are convenient, but you should plan on 3 to 4 hours of work per week, per credit hour (9 to 12 hours per week for a typical class).

Your online classes will start and end on the same dates as the on-campus calendar at your campus. Most classes will give you the flexibility to do the work when it fits your schedule.

When you graduate, your diploma will be identical to those received by all students studying at that campus.
