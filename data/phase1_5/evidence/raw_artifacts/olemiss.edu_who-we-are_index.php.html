Our legacy of leadership is Who We Are

Your personal legacy is our purpose. We can't wait to see what you will accomplish with us.

We are Ole Miss

We build leaders, serve our community and our neighbors, and compete to win. We are a literary mecca, a research center, a launchpad for careers across the professions, a home away from home, and so much more. We are Ole Miss.

Our community is a diverse and dynamic group of individuals, united by their commitment to learning, growth, and making a positive impact on the world.

Our graduates excel in many fields, thanks to our emphasis on leadership and service that reflects our ideals. Our campus's beauty and our people's hospitality forge unforgettable experiences and connections that make Rebels proud.

We are scholars who welcome people to join us in realizing their aspirations. We commit to providing access to higher education, offer mentorship to students needing extra support, and challenge exceptional students through specialized programs.

Our culture of optimism and compassion fosters a sense of shared community among our faculty, staff, students, and alumni at Ole Miss. Together, all of us create a vibrant and thriving academic community dedicated to excellence, innovation, service, and fun. Join us to start building your personal legacy.

The Ole Miss Community

Stellar academics. A welcoming vibe. A beautiful campus. SEC athletics. An idyllic college town. Ole Miss has it all, and all of that attracts the brightest students, faculty, and staff to our campus. Wherever your dreams will take you, Ole Miss is a university that you can call home.

Campus Culture and Traditions

You can experience the University of Mississippi’s vibrant culture and rich traditions firsthand. You will feel how the university promotes a sense of community and pride that students carry with them for the rest of their lives.

Past, Present, and Future

For 178 years, Ole Miss has served our state with distinction while, at the same time, building a rich and complicated history. After making many great strides to address the past, we are a respected academic institution recognized for its diverse and passionate student body. Today, we are a microcosm of America and will continue addressing the challenges of a changing world.

Community Engagement

We commit to improving quality of life in our state through service.  Our commitment strengthens our community, state, and nation.  At Ole Miss, you will find many opportunities to make a difference in the world and the lives of others.

Mission and Vision

We transform lives, communities, and the world.  Our mission is to provide opportunities for the people of Mississippi and beyond.  We strive for excellence in learning, discovery, healthcare, and engagement.
