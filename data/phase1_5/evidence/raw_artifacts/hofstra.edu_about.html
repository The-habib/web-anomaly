Let’s Spark  Real Change

Discover your drive and creativity, step outside your comfort zone, and surpass your academic, career, and life goals. Hofstra connects academic exploration with experiential learning and community engagement, bridging disciplines to give you both breadth and depth across fields of study. With a distinction of excellence in teaching and scholarship, a residential campus at the nexus of urban, suburban, and coastal environments, and a focus on student success, Hofstra is positioned to spark change and ignite momentum.

A Century of Scholarly Distinction

Beyond the vibrant campus experience and dynamic learning environment, Hofstra offers impressive outcomes that speak volumes. Discover Hofstra’s 13:1 student-faculty ratio, explore 13 distinct schools and colleges, and learn why 92% of graduates are employed or in graduate school within a year.
