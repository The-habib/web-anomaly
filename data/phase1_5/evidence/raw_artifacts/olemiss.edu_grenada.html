Graduate from Ole Miss. 

You don't have to leave home to earn your degree.

View Programs

Shaped by Grenada,

ready for anywhere.

Turn your two-year degree into a four-year degree or earn your master's—all while staying close to the community you love.

Ole Miss-DeSoto partners with Northwest Community College to help you finish what you started.

With Ole Miss classes & advising offered here in Southaven, get the same quality education as students on our Oxford campus—with the support of our tight-knit community.

Student Stories

Ryan Melton

Education

After 10 years in another field, Ryan wanted a career change. He was able to continue working while completing courses to earn his Bachelor's degree right in Grenada. "It was the perfect opportunity for me to follow my dream of becoming a teacher."

Cherisha Moss

General Business

Helping others in her community was a passion for Cherisha and earning her degree at UM-Grenada helped her gain the knowledge and skills to become a leader in non-profit management. "I was able to take my career to the next level without a long commute or having to relocate."

Kayla Morman

Education

As a Grenada High graduate, Kayla dreamed of becoming a teacher. She found the affordability and scholarship opportunities available at UM-Grenada could help her meet that goal. "Being able to complete my degree close to home not only helped me financially, but it helped me step into a fulfilling career in education."

Laigan Conley

Ed. Leadership Grad. Program

Through the K-12 Educational Leadership graduate program offered at UM-Grenada, Laigan was able to join a group of up and coming school leaders working to gain the knowledge and insight to make a positive difference in their local schools. "Earning my graduate degree not only qualified me to step into a leadership role at my school, but helped me to become a better teacher and has opened many doors for me professionally and personally."
