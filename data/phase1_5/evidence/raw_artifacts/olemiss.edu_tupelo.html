Graduate from Ole Miss. 

You don't have to leave home to earn your degree.

View Programs

Shaped by Northeast Mississippi,

ready for anywhere.

Turn your two-year degree into a four-year degree or earn your master's—all while staying close to the community you love.

Ole Miss-Tupelo partners with Itawamba Community College to help you finish what you started.

With Ole Miss classes & advising offered here in Tupelo, get the same quality education as students on our Oxford campus—with the support of our tight-knit community.

Student Stories

Bianey Rodriguez

Accounting

As a graduate of Corinth High School and Northeast Community College, Bianey had a goal to to complete her bachelor's degree and to become a CPA. "Ole Miss-Tupelo gave me the chance to complete my degree while still being able to work full-time. I'm excited for the opportunities ahead of me."

Andrew Phillips

Social Work

As a first-generation college student, Andrew was ready to pursue a career where his knowledge and experience could make a difference. He touts the encouragement of classmates and faculty at UM-Tupelo as a driving force. "I alone can not change the world, but I can cast a stone and create many ripples."

Lillyan Nguyen

Elementary Education

As a mother of three, Nguyen said she thrived in the close-knit environment offered at UM-Tupelo. "I had advisors, professors, and fellow students to reach out to. Everyone here is rooting for your success."

Stephanie Whitaker

Master’s of Educational Leadership

As an English teacher at Mantachie High, Stephanie was looking to advance in her career while balancing family and work responsibilities. "Completing my graduate degree at UM-Tupelo gave me the opportunity to learn from experienced educators and and create important professional connections for the next step in my career."
