    JapanGov is your digital gateway to Japan

    JapanGov, the official web portal of the Government of Japan, provides a wealth of information regarding various issues that Japan is tackling, and also directs you to the sites of relevant ministries and agencies.

    01

    Find Japanese ministries and other organizations

    The Government Directory page contains links to websites for the Cabinet, Ministries and Agencies, the Diet, the Supreme Court, and other government agencies.
    The Visiting Japan page features useful information about traveling, living, and working in Japan.

    Government Directory

    Links to Japanese ministries and other organizations.

    Visiting Japan

    Websites about traveling, living, and working in Japan.

    02

    Learn about the key policies of the Government of Japan.

    JapanGov features information about various topics, such as the creation of innovation and technology, and the appeal of Japan to overseas investors. Also featured are topics such as Japan's contribution to international development, women's empowerment, and regional revitalization in Japan.

    JapanGov Home

    The front page of the Government of Japan features information about key policies, feature articles, and news. It also offers cross-search for the websites of Japan's Ministries and Agencies.

    Topics

    Featuring information about the Government of Japan's key policies.

    03

    Learn basic information about Japan

    The About Japan page showcases the national flag and anthem of Japan, statistical information such as the population and GDP, and more.

    About Japan

    04

    Get the latest from the Government of Japan

    You can get updates on the Government of Japan through our News page, official social media accounts, and "KIZUNA," an official regularly-published online magazine.

    JapanGov News

    The latest news from the Cabinet, Ministries and Agencies, and other government organizations.

    KIZUNA

    An official e-magazine of the Japanese government, KIZUNA, introduces various aspects of Japan in order to deepen "KIZUNA" (bonds) between Japan and the rest of the world.
