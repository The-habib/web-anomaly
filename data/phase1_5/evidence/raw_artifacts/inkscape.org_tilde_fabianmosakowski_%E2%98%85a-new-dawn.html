A New Dawn

by Fabian Mosakowski

Hi everyone !
I decided to create an image that emphasizes on the collaborative work of Inkscape and its accessibility to everyone. So here are my little townies representing the Inkscape community building the software as a colorful city with Inkscape tools.
Version 1.2 is coming and it's literally a new dawn for these digital townies ^^

Fonts used : Roboto (Google Fonts)
