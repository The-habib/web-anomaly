A Home for Collaborative Discovery and Innovation

At Case Western Reserve, we solve pressing problems to improve our world. With the creation of the Interdisciplinary Science and Engineering Building (ISEB), our researchers will be able to take their work even further—collaborating across disciplines to drive change.
