Our Programs and Events

At The Hill Institute, we develop entrepreneurial leaders through education, mentorship, and hands-on experience. Our programs serve Furman students, high school students, and community founders by nurturing creativity, strengthening entrepreneurial thinking, and helping innovative ideas become real-world ventures. Through initiatives such as Furman’s Next Big Idea, the Rinker Paladin Pitch Competition, GVL Starts, the Clemens Angel Analyst Fellowship, and the Innovation Academy, we support entrepreneurs from ideation to launch. We host forums, pitch competitions, and community events that foster connection, expand learning, and strengthen Greenville’s innovation ecosystem.

 “The Hill Institute didn’t just influence my college experience, it completely rerouted my entire life, career and mindset. I came to Furman thinking I knew exactly what I wanted to study, but the moment I got involved with the Hill Institute, everything changed. Their programs led me to switch my major, rethink my future, and step fully into my potential as an innovator and entrepreneur. They opened a door I didn’t even know existed, and once I walked through it, there was no going back.”
