Hofstra University provides exceptional education within a diverse environment. Each year, we welcome a number of students from other colleges and universities to take part in the Hofstra experience. All visiting students are accepted through the Office of Admission and must meet the following criteria:

  • The student must be enrolled at an accredited college or university.
  • The student must obtain and submit written documentation from their home institution confirming good academic standing, eligibility to continue classes at their home institution, and that there are no pending disciplinary proceedings against the student.
  • The student must not have been convicted of any felony or misdemeanor charges or have any pending criminal charges against them.

Note: Visiting undergraduate students shall not be permitted to enroll in graduate courses at Hofstra.
