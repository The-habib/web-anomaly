Outreach

Serving Colorado and Beyond

Partnering with communities on cutting-edge research. Connecting students with real-world challenges. Advancing ideas with funders and solutions with local organizations. Sharing life-long learning and service opportunities with alumni.  

Alongside community partners across Colorado, the nation and the world, CU Boulder is meeting today’s challenges and making a difference for the public good.

Office for Public and Community-Engaged Scholarship

The Office for Public and Community-Engaged Scholarship (PACES) is the lead CU Boulder unit for building the campus’s capacity to undertake public and community-engaged scholarship.

Service Learning & Impact in Community Engagement

Service Learning & Impact in Community Engagement (SLICE) fosters a culture of service by connecting CU Boulder to engagement opportunities that positively impact our communities.

Office of Government and Community Engagement

The Office of Government and Community Engagement (OGCE) supports the University of Colorado Boulder's work to build and maintain relationships and partnerships with local government and community organizations.

CU Boulder’s public outreach and community-engaged scholarship builds relationships with communities across Colorado, the nation and the world.
