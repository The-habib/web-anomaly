Research Services

Have a research question? We are pleased to answer questions about primary resources and VMI's history, and locate requested material from our collections, such as letters, photographs, and maps. Submit questions via:

  • Email - archives@vmi.edu
  • Phone - 540-464-7516
  • Chat - 24/7 chat
  • Research appointment - schedule here

Teach with the Archives

We welcome and encourage teaching with our collections and can provide engaging hands-on experiences for cadets. We offer:

  • Customized classroom sessions
  • Course and subject guides tailored to assignments
  • One-on-one research appointments with cadets

For more information or to schedule a session at the Archives, please email archives@vmi.edu.
