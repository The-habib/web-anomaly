Learn How You Can SOAR at UNF

Find your True North at the University of North Florida. Located in a dynamic city just miles from the coast, UNF’s inspiring campus is the perfect backdrop for learning, leisure and discovery. Experience a collaborative and supportive environment designed to help you thrive. Engage in your community while working on innovative projects and research that prepare you to soar into your future. UNF is where you will find the tools to make it happen and the space the discover what’s true to you.
