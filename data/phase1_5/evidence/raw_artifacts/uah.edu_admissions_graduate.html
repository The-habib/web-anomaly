            Let’s Launch Your Academic and Career Goals

            Pursuing your master’s degree can give you a professional edge in your industry, and taking on the challenge at UAH is a smart choice. Why? Because we consistently offer the highest financial return on investment (ROI) compared to all colleges in Alabama, both public and private.

            In addition to being a great investment, UAH is known as a premier research institution with competitive master's and doctoral degree programs, from engineering to nursing. So what are you waiting for? Start today to get ahead.

            Straightforward Financial Aid

            Your focus should be on getting the best education at UAH—not on how you’re going to pay for it. UAH offers a wide range of scholarships, grants, loans, and work-study opportunities. Our Office of Financial Aid aims to take the guesswork out of what aid you’re eligible to receive and help you find a financial aid package that works for you and your family.

            Step 4
            Prepare & Enroll

            Once you’re admitted, the next step is to officially enroll. Our Charger Checklist will guide you through the final requirements so you’re prepped for your first semester at UAH, starting with New Student Orientation.
