Eclipse Foundation projects

Bring your project to the
Eclipse Foundation

Bringing your project to the Eclipse Foundation means joining a vendor-neutral, professional open source ecosystem backed by strong governance, IP protection, and a global community.

  • Submit a project proposal

What makes a great project

At the Eclipse Foundation, we host open source projects that thrive on collaboration, transparency, and neutrality. Our community-led approach ensures that every project, whether a developer framework, technology platform, or cross-industry initiative, receives the support it needs to grow and succeed.

We're especially well-suited for projects that unite contributors across organisations, rely on open innovation, or aim to set new standards in their field. With trusted governance, clear IP management, and a sustainable community model, the Eclipse Foundation is where open source projects grow into ecosystems.

Why projects choose us

The Eclipse Foundation advantage

Bringing your project to the Eclipse Foundation means joining a vendor-neutral, trusted, professional open source ecosystem backed by strong governance, legal protection, and a global community.

Here's what you gain:

Vendor-neutral governance

Your project belongs to the community, not a single company. 

Clear legal & IP framework

Our rigorous IP process and licence vetting give adopters and contributors confidence.

Transparent development

Everything happens in the open, fostering collaboration and accountability.

Project infrastructure

Git, issue tracking, mailing lists, download sites, and CI/CD pipelines, professionally maintained.

Global ecosystem & visibility

Reach thousands of developers, contributors, and industry stakeholders through the Eclipse community.

Marketing & outreach support

Foundation staff help amplify your project through blogs, events, webinars, social media, and press opportunities.

Commitment to security

Projects benefit from a secure development lifecycle, vulnerability reporting channels, and support generating SBOMs and dependency scans.

Welcoming community

All contributors are expected to uphold the Eclipse Community Code of Conduct, fostering a respectful and inclusive environment for collaboration.

Starting a project: what to expect

Getting started

Launching a project at the Eclipse Foundation means joining a well-governed, transparent, and community-focused ecosystem. Here's how the process works:

  1. 01

    Submit a proposal

    Define your project’s goals, scope, initial contributors, and technical context.

    Submit your project proposal Trademark guidance
  2. 02

    Community review

    Your proposal is posted publicly to gather community feedback and support.

  3. 03

    Project creation and provisioning

    The Eclipse Management Organization (EMO) provisions your project infrastructure, including Git repositories, issue tracking, mailing lists, and project metadata, and formally launches your project in the Incubation phase.

  4. 04

    Intellectual Property review

    Your team moves your codebase into resources provided by the Eclipse Foundation. Our team reviews your code to validate provenance and licence.

  5. 05

    Active development

    You and your team continue working on your project following the Eclipse Foundation Development Process (EDP).
