The new plan, developed through a campuswide strategic planning process launched in July 2023, sought answers for the question: “What does the world need Baylor for?” Thousands of faculty, staff, students, alumni and others participated in the process through listening sessions, interviews, online submissions and the development of white papers. A common idea developed from this effort: Baylor has a significant opportunity to adopt a global perspective that also fulfills its scriptural calling.

Explore

Pro Ecclesia. Pro Texana. Pro Mundo.

The mission of Baylor University is to educate men and women for worldwide leadership and service by integrating academic excellence and Christian commitment within a caring community.

Strategic Plan Commitments

Equipping Students to Flourish
Broadening Interdisciplinary Research and Impact
Building a Vibrant, Caring and Global Community
Demonstrating Christian Stewardship

Imperatives

Four imperatives are interwoven across the Commitments. 

Affordability, Value & Completion
Human-Technology Interface
Health & Engineering
Civil Discourse

Foundational Pillars

The Strategic Plan is guided by four foundational pillars that have shaped the University throughout history.

Distinctive Christian Environment and Impact
Transformational Undergraduate Educational Experience
Impactful Research, Scholarship and Graduate Education
Renowned Programs in the Arts and Athletics

History

Built on the momentum of successful Strategic Plans of the past 20 years, Baylor is positioned for global impact.
