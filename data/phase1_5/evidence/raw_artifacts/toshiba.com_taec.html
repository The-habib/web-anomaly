Topics

Design / Development

Model-Based Development (MBD) Technology to Resolve EMC Issues at the Early Design Stage
We have established a model-based development (MBD) technology that enables highly accurate prediction of EMI and EMS characteristics at the circuit design stage for automotive ICs.This technology allows EMC-related issues—which have traditionally been identified in the later stages of development—to be resolved at an early design phase, significantly reducing the risk of redesign.

MOSFETs, Automotive MOSFETs, 12V - 300V MOSFETs

40V N-channel Power MOSFET "XPMR7404PB" with SOP Advance(EWF) Package Contributing to Higher Efficiency and Larger Current Capacity in Automotive Equipment
The new products adopt the SOP Advance (EWF) package, which improves current-carrying capability and reduce package resistance. In addition, the wettable flank structure contributes to automated inspection on manufacturing lines.

MOSFETs, Automotive MOSFETs, 12V - 300V MOSFETs

Lineup Expansion of Automotive 40V N‑channel MOSFETs in Small, High Heat Dissipation S-TOGL™ Package
Toshiba has launched a 40V N‑channel MOSFET rated at 120A for 12V automotive systems, expanding its lineup of S-TOGL™ package products. This product offers both small size and high heat dissipation, along with low On-resistance, thereby helping to improve efficiency and reduce the size of automotive equipment.

Motor Driver ICs, Stepping Motor Driver ICs

40V/2A Stepping Motor Driver IC with Phase Interface Resistant to External Noise for Industrial Applications
The TB67S531FTG uses the phase input method for motor control. By directly controlling the output current of each phase, it suppresses motor step-out caused by noise and operates stably even in environments with external noise.

Isolators/Solid State Relays, Solid State Relays, Photorelays (MOSFET Output)

Small-Package Photorelays for Semiconductor Testers Combining High ON-State Current and Low OFF-State Leakage Current
The TLP3491 and TLP3487 achieve both high ON-state current and low OFF-state leakage current in a small P-SON4 package through optimized device design of the MOSFET output section. This allows configuration with a single device even in applications requiring high current.

MOSFETs, 12V - 300V MOSFETs

Small, High-Power Dissipation MOSFETs with 60V and 100V Ratings to Improve Efficiency of Power Lines in Industrial Equipment
Toshiba has launched three 100V MOSFETs for 48V power lines and six 60V MOSFETs for 24V power lines —nine products in total. These new products feature SOT-23F, TSOP6F, and UDFN6B packages, contributing to high efficiency, low loss, and miniaturization.

MOSFETs, Automotive MOSFETs, 12V - 300V MOSFETs

Automotive 80V N-channel MOSFETs to Support 48V Systems in Automotive Equipment
The new MOSFETs, featuring the U-MOSX-H process, offer low drain-source On-resistance: 2.55mΩ (max) for the XPH2R608QB and 3.9mΩ (max) for the XPH3R908QB, contributing to reduced power consumption in equipment. The package adopts the SOP Advance (WF) with a wettable flank structure.

Isolators/Solid State Relays, Standard Digital Isolators

Quad-Channel Standard Digital Isolators Contributing to Lower Power Consumption in Industrial Equipment
The DCL341L0B and DCL341H0B incorporate Toshiba’s newly developed proprietary circuit technology in the isolation signal transmission section, achieving low power consumption of 0.2 mA per channel while maintaining stable data transmission at speeds of up to 25 Mbps (max).

General Purpose Logic ICs, Level Shifters

Lineup Expansion of the "74AVC Series" Dual Power-Supply Bus Transceivers with New 1-bit and 2-bit Products－Supporting communication interfaces such as UART, GPIO, and SPI－
The new products support low-voltage operation and enable bidirectional voltage-level shifting between 0.7V or 0.8 V and up to 3.6V. In addition, a push-pull buffer configuration is adopted to achieve high-speed operation.
Small bit configurations and packages increase flexibility in system design.

MOSFETs, 12V - 300V MOSFETs

Dual MOSFET with N-Channel + P-Channel Configuration for Miniaturization and Low Power Consumption of Consumer and Industrial Equipment
SSM6L826R features the TSOP6F package with flat leads, which enhances chip‑mounting capability. The drain-source On-resistance values of the P-channel and N-channel are low and nearly equivalent.

Automotive Devices

Automotive SmartMCD™
The SmartMCD™ series is a system-on-chip (SoC) that integrates a motor driver and an Arm® Cortex®–based microcontroller, achieving high performance within a compact package. In addition, it supports communication interfaces such as LIN, offering a new solution for controlling automotive DC motors and BLDC motors.
