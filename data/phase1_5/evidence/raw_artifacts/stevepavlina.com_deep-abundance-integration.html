Deep Abundance Integration

Join me for a 30-day online deep dive into the core principles, mindset, heartset, action steps, practices, tools, and habits to escape stuckness and progress to a life of freedom, balance, growth, and abundance.

Deep Abundance Integration is a 30-day video deep dive that was recorded live August 1-30, 2018. It has since been turned into a complete video course with more than 36 hours of material on how to transition from scarcity to abundance. Watch the original launch video to learn all about it. The key details are shared in the first 11 minutes.

Let's do this!

Key Details

30-day series of recorded video calls

This course was recorded live online in August 2018 with hundreds of people attending the video calls. The average lesson is 73 minutes, and there are 30 lessons total, so this is more than 36 hours of very deep material, much of it new. We're talking about twice as much content as I'd normally include in a 3-day live workshop.

You can stream and/or download the entire course

You get the recordings of every call (video and audio, streamable and downloadable). Go at whatever pacing works for you since the course is entirely self-paced. Watch video lessons whenever you want, or listen to the audio versions while you commute. Mix and match formats however you like. This will accommodate any schedule.

Chat logs from every call

Learn from your peers too, not just from me. All text chat logs from the live calls are provided (PDF format), averaging around 30 pages per call, so you'll get a copy of every community insight – and plenty of jokes – that live attendees shared along the way. 🙂

A caring and supportive approach

Deep Abundance Integration has a uniquely caring, supportive, growth-oriented, and playful style. There's no shame or guilt here. We focus on depth of understanding, practical solutions, and improving your results while also sharing the reasoning behind each idea. We connect the dots in ways you've surely never seen before.

Deep and comprehensive

We cover many aspects of abundance – mindset, heartset (feelings), powerful reframes, action steps, practices, tools, habits, and more. We cover multiple types of abundance too – financial, lifestyle, social, sexual, energy, time, and behind. We even discuss how to transform your relationship with death.

A co-creative experience

After each live call, I reviewed the chat logs and email feedback, and I designed upcoming lessons based on the real needs and desires of participants as we went along, while also allowing room for inspiration. So this course has a uniquely co-creative design that balances structure and spontaneity.

Accessible pricing – $97 USD

Your investment is $97 all-in. There aren't any tiers, scholarships, or refunds. This is priced to be an easy yes for the majority of my blog readers and newsletter subscribers who may wish to participate. I explain the reasons for not offering a money-back guarantee in the video at about the 55-minute point if you'd like to know why.

Key Topics

Here are some of the topics we cover during our 30 days together:

  • Use core principles of abundance and growth to understand why and how you experience stuckness or inconsistency in certain areas, and know which tools and strategies will work best for restoring your alignment.
  • Learn and adopt scalable approaches for creating value and achieving specific income goals.
  • Learn how to align your abundance path with your deepest values, including how to feel good when spending money.
  • Overcome plateaus and level up without burning out.
  • Prepare for the next-level challenges of abundance... and how to tell when you're ready to progress from the challenges of scarcity.
  • Replace self-sabotage with self-compassion and self-confidence, and deepen your trust in life, your feelings, and your unique path of personal growth.

So basically we're shredding scarcity to pieces here. 😉

Powerful Intentions

Several readers suggested this idea, so we've made it so. We're doing a group manifestation experiment for all interested participants by holding the following intention together:

In an easy and relaxed manner, in a healthy and positive way, in its own perfect time, for the highest good of all, I intend and invite more abundance to come into my life and into the lives of everyone who holds a similar intention.

I'm holding this intention with you, so let's co-create some major abundance mojo for the whole group.
