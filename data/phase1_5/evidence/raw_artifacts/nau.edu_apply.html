General application must-knows

Understand why our admission application includes the following fields.

Parents/guardians graduating from college/university

We ask to determine if you would be considered first in your family to attend college. As a trailblazer in your family, we want to make sure you have the support you need to succeed!

Residency

There are several ways a student can be classified as an Arizona resident for tuition purposes. Find out if you're an Arizona resident.

Identification

Preferred name

  • What it is: A name you use other than your legal name.
  • Where it's used: Class rosters, LOUIE, and other platforms to support respectful communication.
  • Legal use: Your legal name will appear on official documents (e.g., tax forms, paychecks, mailed communications).
  • Who sees it: Faculty and relevant staff.

Sex

  • What it is: Your legal sex, matching your Social Security records.
  • Why it's collected: Required for federal reporting and compliance.
  • Who sees it: Staff with federal reporting responsibilities or a verified need to know.

Gender pronouns

  • What they are: Words like “she/her,” “he/him,” or “they/them” used to refer to you.
  • Where they're used: Class rosters and Salesforce for respectful communication. Update anytime via LOUIE.
  • Who sees them: Faculty and staff.
