2017 September Wallpaper

Each month the Salk e-newsletter ends with a stunning photo to take your desktop on a journey into scientific discoveries.
Pick your favorite images, and download them as your desktop wallpaper.

This month’s image features microscopy showing chromosomes (red) with telomeres (green).

Just choose an image and right click on a link to download the proper image for your screen size.

  • Desktop
  • Tablet
  • Facebook
  • Phone

Desktop Wallpaper

Installing your wallpaper is easy:

  • Click on the link below.
  • Right click image to show menu of options.
  • Select “Set as Background” or “Set as Desktop Background”.

Tablet Wallpaper

Installing your wallpaper is easy:

  • Click on the “IPad cover photo” link below.
  • Touch and hold the image and select “Save Image”.
  • Navigate to the photo in your photo album.
  • From the “options” menu, select “Use as Wallpaper.”
  • IPad cover photo »

Facebook Cover Photos

Setting up your cover photo is easy:

  • Click on the link below for the full-sized version.
  • Right-click and download the image, then upload it to Facebook.
  • Select “use as cover photo” option.
  • Facebook cover photo »

Smartphone Wallpaper

Installing your wallpaper is easy:

  • Touch the photo you want to use as wallpaper.
  • Touch and hold the image and select “Save Image”.
  • Navigate to the photo in your photo album.
  • From the “options” menu, select “Use as Wallpaper.”
