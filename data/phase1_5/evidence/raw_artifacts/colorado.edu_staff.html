Staff

CU Boulder wouldn’t be the same without our extraordinary staff, whose work is instrumental to our mission of solving the humanitarian, social and technological challenges of our time. We’re here to help you thrive in and out of the workplace. Discover news, resources and events available for CU Boulder staff members.

What’s Your Big Idea? Help transform the future of CU Boulder’s public research mission.

This year, we celebrate CU Boulder’s 150 year legacy of transformative impact. We’re asking students, staff and faculty to share your big ideas that will help define CU Boulder’s next era of societal impact. Today’s Big Ideas will become tomorrow’s CU Boulder legacy.

What Makes Us Boulder?

As the state's flagship university and one of only 38 U.S. public research institutions in the Association of American Universities (AAU), CU Boulder has proudly served Coloradans since the state's founding. Learn more about our mission is to serve as the state of Colorado’s comprehensive graduate research university our vision is to transform lives in service to a just and sustainable world.
