Rankings & Achievements

Rankings & Achievements

As one of the top-ranked R1 research institutions in the U.S., CU Boulder is the perfect place to pursue your passions and make your mark in your chosen field. Learn from some of the nation’s most productive faculty and explore exciting new research questions in cutting-edge lab spaces. Once you graduate, leave campus with increased career potential, a greater opportunity to positively impact society and lifelong ties to our far-reaching Buff community.

CU and the Nobel Prize

CU Boulder boasts five Nobel laureates, four in physics and one in chemistry. Perhaps the world's premier honor for intellectuals, and certainly the best known, it celebrates individuals who have "conferred the greatest benefit to human kind" through their work.

“The achievement of Bose-Einstein condensation in dilute gases of alkali atoms, and for early fundamental studies of the properties of the condensates.”

Physics, 2005

“Contributions to the development of laser-based precision spectroscopy, including the optical frequency comb technique.”
John “Jan” Hall

Physics, 2012

“Ground-breaking experimental methods that enable measuring and manipulation of individual quantum systems.”
David Wineland

Committed to Supporting Student Success Beyond Academics

 

 

The Colorado Department of Higher Education has awarded CU Boulder the Comprehensive Basic Needs Campus Designation, recognizing CU Boulder’s commitment to addressing essential student needs, from food security to mental health and more.

CU Boulder has helped advance statewide conversations and partnerships focused on student basic needs, contributing to innovative approaches that support student success across Colorado higher education.

Providing Access to Students in Colorado

Be Boulder pact saves students millions of dollars a year in fees.

Automatic Merit Scholarships attract Colorado’s highest-achieving high schoolers with weighted GPAs of 3.8 to 4.0+.

CU Promise pays low-income students’ share of tuition and fees.

CU Boulder tuition and fee guarantee offers a predictable, four-year guarantee, providing complete transparency.
