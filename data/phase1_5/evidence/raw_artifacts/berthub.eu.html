Intro

Hi! I'm bert hubert, and I am an entrepreneur & software developer. I sometimes contribute to science, I care a lot about Europe 🇪🇺, innovation, climate, biology & health. I'm a former shareholder of Open-Xchange, the parent company of PowerDNS which I founded in 1999. In 2020, I joined a government oversight board, which I have since left. These days I am a (very) part-time technical advisor at various government departments, including the Dutch Electoral Council. Furthermore, I write articles, some of which have been published by places like the IETF, IEEE, RIPE, APNIC, the Swedish Electronics Standardisation Institute, but also by newspapers/magazines like Nature Scientific Data, De Volkskrant, Nederlands Dagblad, Villamedia, De Ingenieur, Knack, dnip.ch and others. A bit more about me and what I (want to) do can be found here. I hold several small secondary positions or "nevenfuncties".

By popular demand (really), I now also have a newsletter in English, and also een nieuwsbrief in het Nederlands. On those pages you can also subscribe to some further more dedicated lists.
