Commencement

The 172nd commencement ceremony for the law school will take place Friday, May 14, at 10 a.m. on the Front Lawn. The 240th undergraduate commencement will take place Thursday, May 27, at 10 a.m. on the Front Lawn. The Baccalaureate Service will occur Wednesday, May 26, at 10 a.m., also on the Front Lawn. Both commencement ceremonies will be broadcast live online.

Student Affairs Staff

W&L is a close-knit community, and most students will get to know the staff within the Division of Student Affairs, which works with all aspects of student life, both inside and outside the classroom.

Alex Miller

Vice President for Student Affairs

Alex oversees the majority of student life and student services functions at W&L, collaborating with various constituents in order to meet the developmental needs of our students.

Dave Leonard

Dean of Students

Dave assists with the management and direction of departments in Student Affairs including the First-Year Experience (Leading Edge, Orientation, and FYE 100), Housing and Residence Life, and Community Values and Expectations (student conduct). He works closely with class and academic deans, chairs the Automatic Rule and Reinstatement committee, coordinates the University’s leave of absence and withdrawal processes and serves on several University committees.
