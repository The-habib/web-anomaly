A global community of open source builders

At the Eclipse Foundation, innovation happens in the open. With hundreds of projects spanning cloud computing, IoT, automotive, edge computing, AI, and more, there’s a place for every developer, contributor, and organisation. Whether you want to explore cutting-edge technologies or roll up your sleeves and contribute, you're in the right place.
