    Academics at UAH

    Academics at The University of Alabama in Huntsville gives you a competitive edge. A massive range of areas of study; small, average class sizes; and a host of research centers and laboratories help you stand apart from the pack. UAH academics equip you to explore, discover, create, and communicate knowledge. And thanks to UAH’s robust academic support services, the resources you need to be successful are always within reach.

    Academic advising is your primary resource for advice, information, and guidance regarding your academic career.
