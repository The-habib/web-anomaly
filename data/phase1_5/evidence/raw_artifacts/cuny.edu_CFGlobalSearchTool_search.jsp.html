Meet the Chancellor

Chancellor Félix V. Matos Rodríguez is CUNY’s first Latino Chancellor after working his way up in our University for two decades. He is ushering in a new era of equity for CUNY.

CUNY’s Value for Students

Our University provides life-changing and high-value degrees for our students, while serving as a major engine of social mobility for New York.
