Graduate from Ole Miss.

View Programs

Shaped by Northeast Mississippi,

ready for anywhere.

Turn your two-year degree into a four-year degree or earn your master's—all while staying close to the community you love.

Ole Miss-Booneville partners with Northeast Community College to help you finish what you started.

With Ole Miss classes & advising offered here in Booneville, get the same quality education as students on our Oxford campus—with the support of our tight-knit community.

Student Stories

Clay Herring

Multi-Disciplinary Studies

As a father and full-time employee, Clay took advantage of the flexible class times and online courses offered at the Ole Miss-Booneville campus to work toward his goal of completing his bachelor's degree. "I had the opportunity to meet great people and learn from wonderful instructors at UM-Booneville," Clay said.

Erica Miller

Multi-Disciplinary Studies

Unsure of her next steps in her career journey, Erica decided to return to college to complete her college degree and now serves as a student success coach that helps others do the same. "With strong encouragement from the Ole Miss-Booneville team, I was able to finish what I started."

Alison Patton

Education

Teaching was a life-long goal that Alison was able to make come true this spring by completing her Bachelor's degree in Education through the UM-Booneville campus. She said the campus in Booneville felt like the right fit for her. "The campus feels welcoming and comfortable, and it's nice being a part of a place where everyone is rooting for you."

Thomas Kimmons

Multi-Disciplinary Studies

Through his time in the Army, Thomas realized that enjoys serving and teaching. By completing his MDS degree, he plans to earn his alternate route teaching certification and hopes to serve as an Army JROTC instructor in the coming days. "Earning my degree will open the doors for me to make a difference in someone else's life – just like others have done for me."
