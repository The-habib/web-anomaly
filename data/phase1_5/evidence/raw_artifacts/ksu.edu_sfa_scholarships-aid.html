Office of Student Financial Assistance

Your financial path to success

If you are planning to use financial aid to help pay for your K-State education, you are in good company. More than 80 percent of undergraduate students receive some form of financial assistance, whether in the form of scholarships, loans and/or work-study.

Support every step of the way

Our office is here to help you make your way through the financial aid process. This leaves you to focus on what's most important: your education! You will be assigned a dedicated financial assistance advisor who can help you manage your aid and build a plan that sets you up for success.

 

Future student scholarships and awards

See our scholarship options for incoming domestic or international freshmen, transfer or graduate students planning to attend any of K-State's campus locations.

Continuing student scholarships and awards

Looking for your next round of scholarship opportunities? We've got you covered with available options for current students.

K-State Scholarship Network (KSN)

The K-State Scholarship Network (KSN) is an online portal that can connect you to additional scholarship opportunities, whether you're an incoming student or continuing student.

Get instructions for KSN

 

GrantsGrants are a form of financial aid that do not need to be repaid. Check out K-State, federal and state opportunities.
Work studyWork study is a federal program available to undergraduate and graduate students to earn money for personal and educational expenses.
LoansLoans are financial obligations that must be repaid. Learn more about types of loans and how to manage loan repayment.
