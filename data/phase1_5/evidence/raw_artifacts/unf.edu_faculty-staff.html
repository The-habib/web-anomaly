Welcome Faculty and Staff

For all our valued employees — more than 1,800 strong — who work together campuswide to make UNF a distinctive place for students to learn, here are some essential and popular services that we hope will make your workplace life a bit easier. 

So, whether you’re faculty or staff, these resources are for you.

UNF Central is Now Live

We are excited to share that UNF Central, our new employee intranet, is now live! It is a place to find news, resources, announcements, and tools designed specifically for faculty and staff. The site is built to make your workday easier and to bring everything you need into one convenient, secure space.

Access UNF Central through myWings or by visiting the site directly.
