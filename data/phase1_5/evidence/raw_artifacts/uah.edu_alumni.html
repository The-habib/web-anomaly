    Welcome, Chargers!

    The Office of Alumni Relations is proud to represent you and more than 55,000 UAH alumni worldwide.

    Our mission is simple: to keep you connected to UAH long after graduation. Whether you live in Huntsville or across the globe, we want you to find your Charger community and celebrate the pride of being part of the blue and white family.

    The Alumni Association

    Every graduate of the University of Alabama in Huntsville is automatically a member of the UAH Alumni Association—no dues required. The association’s purpose is to provide opportunities for you to remain engaged with UAH and the Charger Community. With support from the Office of Alumni Relations, the association connects alumni through regional and affinity groups, uniting Chargers by geography, shared interests, and academic ties. This also includes alumni benefits, events and university resources such as Career Services, transcripts, and professional networking.

    Regional and Affinity Groups

    Find Your Charger Community
    Connect with Chargers based on your location or interest - from Georgia to D.C. and athletics to esports. Get involved with an existing chapter, or start a new one and build your own Charger network.
