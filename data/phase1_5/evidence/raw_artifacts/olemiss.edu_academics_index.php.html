Build your legacy Academics

We provide an environment that promotes learning and encourages academic excellence.

Explore our majors

Learning at the University of Mississippi is a life-changing experience. 

At Ole Miss, you'll find one of the finest academic experiences at a public university. Our 16 academic divisions are known throughout the nation. With diverse programs, supportive faculty and top-notch facilities, you'll have the tools to carve your path. And it takes place on our beautiful, classic campus.

Our education reaches beyond the classroom. Work in the communities we serve, discover alongside top researchers, and lead by example every step of the way. You'll have opportunities to take your academic journey to places across the globe.

We'll make sure you are supported during your time here. Our staff is ready and willing to help you thrive.

So, are you ready to start building your legacy?
