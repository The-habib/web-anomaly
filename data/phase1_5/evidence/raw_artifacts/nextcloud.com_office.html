Nextcloud gives you the freedom to choose the office suite that best fits your needs regarding file format compatibility and performance.

Nextcloud Office powered by Collabora

Built on LibreOffice technology, Collabora Online prioritizes Open Document Format (ODF) fidelity and low infrastructure overhead. Processing is handled primarily on the server, reducing the load on client devices.
More about Collabora

Nextcloud Office powered by Euro-Office

A web-native office suite highly compatible with Microsoft Office (OOXML) file formats and focused on a modern editing experience. It uses a client‑centric architecture to increase performance.

Thinkfree Office

Frequently asked questions

Yes, Euro-Office is a powerful open source alternative to proprietary office suites like Microsoft Office and web-based editors like Google Docs, Sheets, and Slides. It features simultaneous editing and is compatible with propietary file formats (e.g., DOCX, XLSX, and PPTX) as well as open file formats (e.g., ODT, ODS, and ODP).

Euro-Office gives organizations full control over where and how data is processed, making it fully compatible with GDPR-compliant deployments. If you use a provider, please check with them for their specific data processing agreement.

Fresh installations using the latest version of the Nextcloud All-in-One Docker image give you the option to enable Euro-Office during the initial configuration. For instructions on how to activate it in your existing Nextcloud setup, please contact your Nextcloud provider or follow our guide on how to install Euro-Office.

Yes, Euro-Office was released on June 9, 2026, and is available as an office suite option in the latest Nextcloud versions, starting with Nextcloud Hub 26 Spring.

Among the organizations supporting and contributing to Euro-Office are: Abilian, BTactic, EuroStack, IONOS, Nextcloud, Office.EU, Open-Xchange, OpenProject, Proton, Soverin, Tuta, and XWiki.

Euro-Office is based on the ONLYOFFICE Open Source AGPL codebase. The codebase has been extensively reviewed, cleaned up, and refactored to make it easier to build, maintain, and contribute to. This independent codebase allows Euro-Office to evolve separately from ONLYOFFICE.
