Define Your Journey

With over 4,000 courses across 100 departments, hundreds of student groups and abundant opportunities for student research, entrepreneurship and community engagement, Cornell is your springboard to success. That’s part of how Cornell lives up to its founding principle of “… any person … any study.”

An Ivy League Education Within Reach

Our investments in financial aid mean that the cost of a world-class Cornell education may be less than expected. All admitted students with demonstrated financial need have their full need met with our financial aid packages, and nearly half of Cornell undergraduates receive Cornell grant aid — money that does not need to be repaid. Grant funding has more than tripled in the past 20 years, making Cornell more affordable today than it was two decades ago.

Life as a Cornellian

On-campus living keeps students close to a community of faculty, staff and peers, and provides easy access to Cornell’s world-class dining facilities and recreation services. Beyond campus, the Ithaca community offers a unique college town living environment, stunning natural beauty and a plethora of cultural and culinary activities.
