Members of the Case Western Reserve community, guests and visitors have the right to be free from discrimination and harassment.

Find out more about our Non-Discrimination Policy.
Annual Title IX and anti-discrimination (CAMPUS Act) training

All students, faculty, staff, and postdoctoral scholars and fellows are required to annually complete a Title IX and anti-discrimination and harassment training to ensure they have the critical information and support resources they might need if they experience discrimination or harassment.
