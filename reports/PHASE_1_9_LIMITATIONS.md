# Project Atlas — Scientific & Methodological Limitations (Phase 1.9)

## 1. Statistical Power & Sample Size
- **Primary Limitation**: Although the effect direction replicated ($2.0\%$ Treatment vs $0.0\%$ Control, $RR_{\text{HA}} = 5.00$), the domain-level sample size of $N=200$ domains yielded only 2 total discoveries.
- **Power Calculation**: Detecting a $2\%$ vs $0\%$ discovery advantage at $\alpha = 0.05$ and $80\%$ power requires a sample size of approximately $N \approx 800$ to $1,200$ domains per arm ($N \approx 2,000$ total).
- **Classification Consequence**: Because statistical power is insufficient to reject $H_0$ at $\alpha = 0.05$, the preregistered outcome is formally classified as **`PROMISING_BUT_UNCONFIRMED`**.

## 2. Path Sparsity in Archive Indices
- 31% of Treatment domains and 36% of Control domains contained fewer than 10 historical paths in public CDX indices, exhausting their candidate pools before consuming all 10 slots.
