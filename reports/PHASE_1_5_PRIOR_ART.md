## 1. Terminology & Scientific Principle

In Project Atlas:
- **`NEW_TO_ATLAS`**: An unmodernized web surface discovered by the automated discovery pipeline that was previously unindexed in Atlas's anomaly ledger.
- **`OBSCURE`**: A surface that is neither linked from current search-engine homepages nor mentioned in major curated lists of vintage websites.
- **`DOCUMENTED`**: Well-known nostalgic landmarks (e.g. `spacejam.com`, `zombo.com`, `heavensgate.com`).
