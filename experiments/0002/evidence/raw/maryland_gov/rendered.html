What's happening in Maryland

  • “Miles for Maryland” Trail Challenge

    To commemorate the MARYLAND 250, Maryland State Parks is challenging you to hike 250 miles in State Parks this year!

  • MVA adds a new butterfly symbol to driver’s licenses

    The symbol supports people with hidden disabilities. It was created to help save lives and promote understanding.

  • Chesapeake Bay Legacy Act protects the Bay

    The new law sets out to improve Bay water quality. It also promotes economic growth by using methods that are safe for the environment.
