In the middle of everywhere

UC Davis is centrally located in Northern California, making it easy to explore some of California’s most iconic destinations. From San Francisco and Napa Valley to Lake Tahoe and Yosemite National Park, students can enjoy unforgettable day trips and weekend adventures just a short drive from campus.

A top-ranked public institution

UC Davis is a tier-one research university. Several of our programs are among the best in the country and have been for decades. We hope you will help us continue this legacy and solve some of the world’s greatest challenges.

A research powerhouse

UC Davis is a leading research university where undergraduates work alongside MacArthur Fellows and world-renowned faculty. Together, we advance discoveries that solve the world’s most pressing challenges.
