    Your Path Starts Here

    One of the most unique traditions found on any campus, Senior Walk includes the names of more than 230,000 University of Arkansas graduates.

    It’s a campus path of sidewalks that symbolize the individual successes and collective impact of 150+ years of difference-makers determined to build a better world.
