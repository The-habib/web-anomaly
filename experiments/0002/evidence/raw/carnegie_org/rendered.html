A Foundation for Opportunity and Peace

The Andrew Carnegie Foundation, originally established as Carnegie Corporation of New York, was founded in 1911 by Andrew Carnegie. Today, our funding supports the issues that Carnegie considered most important: education, democracy, and peace.

Our Work

Our overarching goal is to reduce political polarization through grants that support ladders of opportunity and a more peaceful world. Our funding provides individuals with the knowledge and tools needed to improve their lives, participate fully in society, and advance peace.

Learn more
National Program

Ladders of Opportunity

Our National Program helps individuals achieve upward mobility and greater civic participation, and seeks to increase trust in public institutions, elections, and government.

International Program

A More Peaceful World

Our International Program works to advance peace by eliminating armed conflict and supporting the conditions needed for understanding and resolving global security challenges.
Libraries

A Legacy of Libraries

Andrew Carnegie funded the construction of more than 2,500 libraries worldwide, one of the most significant philanthropic contributions of all time. Today, we continue his commitment to knowledge and understanding.
