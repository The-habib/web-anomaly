Equinor. Providing reliable energy for a world in transition.

In a world shaped by uncertainty, growing demand and the need to reduce emissions, we deliver secure and lower-emission energy.

In Equinor, we combine a strong oil and gas business with a growing power business, supported by trading and market expertise. This is how we will meet energy needs, strengthen energy security and create long-term value.

We aim to grow across oil, gas, power and trading, and to capture opportunities in a changing energy system.

Energy for people. Progress for society. Searching for better

Latest news

  • 17 August 2026

    Equinor strengthens position in US power market

    Equinor has agreed to acquire an interest in the Lackawanna Energy Center (Lackawanna), a 1,483 MW gas-fired combined cycle power plant (CCGT) in Pennsylvania. The transaction represents a step forward in building an integrated power business in selected markets.

  • 13 August 2026

    Ex. dividend first quarter 2026

    The shares in Equinor ASA will be traded on the Oslo Stock Exchange (OSE) and New York Stock Exchange (NYSE) exclusive the first quarter 2026 cash dividend as detailed below.

  • 22 July 2026

    Equinor second quarter 2026 results

    Equinor delivered an adjusted operating income* of USD 11.48 billion in the second quarter of 2026. Equinor reported a net operating income of USD 12.99 billion and a net income of USD 4.84 billion. Adjusted net income* was USD 3.22 billion, leading to adjusted earnings per share* of USD 1.33.
