About Us
More than just food

At General Mills, we make food for people and pets across everyday occasions, with more than 100 trusted brands in over 100 countries.

We focus on great taste, quality and value, making food that meets a wide range of needs and preferences.

Food is much more than what’s on a plate. It’s how our ingredients are grown by farmers, how our food is made with care and ultimately how it makes its way into someone’s home.
