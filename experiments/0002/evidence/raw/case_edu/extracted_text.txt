Addressing hearing differences
In her lab, Lauren Calandruccio's research team focuses on understanding and improving challenging listening situations, often manipulating factors to assess types of listening. In one example, Calandruccio and an undergraduate student co-developed an online pure-tone audiometry stimulator for students to learn and practice hearing tests.
