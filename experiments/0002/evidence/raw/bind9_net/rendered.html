BIND 9.18 - Older Stable, Extended Support version

BIND 9.18 is a stable branch, supported from 2022-2026. This version was declared ESV in 2023 and will be supported for 4 years in total. In addition to completing the network socket refactoring, this branch also includes BIND support for DNS over HTTPS (DoH) and DNS over TLS (DoT) two new encrypted transports for DNS.
