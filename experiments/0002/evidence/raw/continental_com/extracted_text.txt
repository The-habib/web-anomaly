Tire Treads: How Patterns, Grooves and Sipes Keep You Safe
In this episode, we show how every line in the tire tread is precisely engineered to ensure safety and performance. Together with our expert Claudia Bauer, we illustrate the roles that grooves and sipes play. Like high-performance channels, they drain water to prevent aquaplaning, provide traction on snow, and even influence road noise. We’ll also show you how simulations, AI, and real-world testing make the tread pattern a crucial safety feature.
