Symbols of Iowa
- 
                      
        
                                        
          
                      State Bird: GoldfinchIn 1933 the legislature named the eastern goldfinch, also called American goldfinch or wild canary as the state bird.
- 
                      
        
                                        
          
                      State Tree: OakThe oak was named as the official state tree in 1961 for its source of food and shelter for Iowa wildlife.
- 
                      
        
                                        
          
                      State Quarter: EducationMinted in 2004, the quarter features Grant Wood's painting Arbor Day, symbolizing Iowa's "Foundation in Education."
- 
                      
        
                                        
          
                      Iowa State FlagDesigned by Dixie Cornell Gebhardt of Knoxville and adopted in 1921. It features Iowa's state motto and an eagle.
